Ticket conditions

Aurelia Theatres Ltd · Conditions of sale

These conditions apply to every ticket the Aurelia sells, whether it is bought at the counter, held online, or collected at the window on the night.

Holding and buying

A booking passes through two steps. A hold reserves the seats you have chosen against your name and takes them off sale; confirming the purchase turns the hold into a sale and issues a collection code. Until you confirm, nothing is sold and nothing is charged. Every hold lapses at curtain on the night of the performance, and lapsed seats go straight back on sale, so a hold you do not confirm protects nothing once the house opens.

The counter checks each request against the terms of the booking it belongs to before it holds anything. Where a request cannot be met, the counter says which term it failed rather than holding something close to it. Requests the counter cannot sell are counted, and a line that keeps sending them is paused briefly before it may try again.

Exchanges and refunds

  • Tickets are exchanged for another performance of the same production up to noon on the day of the performance you hold, subject to seats being available, and once only.
  • Tickets are not exchanged after the interval has begun, and collected tickets are not exchanged at all once the performance has ended.
  • We refund in full only where the Aurelia cancels or abandons a performance, or where the running time is cut by more than a quarter.
  • A cast change is not grounds for a refund. Billing is a statement of intention, not a term of sale.
  • Refunds return to the means of payment used at the counter.

Restricted view

Some seats at the outer ends of the stalls rows sit behind the closed circle's supporting columns. We describe those as restricted view, mark them as such wherever seats are shown, and sell them at the counter only, so that somebody can tell you in plain words what you will and will not see from them. A restricted-view seat is never substituted for a full-view seat, and it is not refunded on the grounds of the restriction itself.

In the house

Latecomers are seated at a suitable break in the programme, which on a revue may be several numbers after curtain up. Photography and recording during the performance are not permitted. We may refuse admission, or ask a patron to leave, where behaviour spoils the performance for others; no refund follows. Children are welcome at any performance not otherwise billed, and every patron occupying a seat needs a ticket for it whatever their age.

Resale

Tickets are sold for personal use. Resale above the price paid, or resale in the course of a business, voids the ticket and the holder may be refused admission. Queries about any of the above go to the box office on 0151 496 0114 from noon daily, or to the duty manager at 11 Granville Street.
