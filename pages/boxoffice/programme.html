The Brass Nightingale

A revue in two acts · This evening at 7:30 · Curtain down 10:05

Devised and directed by Vernon Ashe. Orchestrations by the Aurelia house band under Constance Mireau. First staged at this house in March and held over by demand.

  • Overture: Granville Street at DuskThe house band
  • Copper and SmokeLena Vasseur
  • The Porter's WaltzDouglas Trent & company
  • Nightingale, Sing TwiceLena Vasseur, Rufus Cole
  • Tableau: The Brass FoundryFull company

Act two

  • Two Seats on the Late CarRufus Cole

One interval of twenty minutes. The foyer bar serves through the interval, and interval orders are taken at your seat in rows A to D.

Visiting

The Aurelia Playhouse stands at 11 Granville Street, two minutes from the Corn Exchange tram stop. Doors open one hour before curtain. The auditorium is a single raked stalls floor of eight rows, A nearest the stage, with a centre aisle between seats 6 and 7; the circle above is closed this season for plasterwork restoration, and its supports break the sight line from a handful of outer stalls seats, which we sell as restricted view at the counter only.

Step-free entry is by the Granville Street foyer, and the accessible booking page lists the same allocation as the seating plan for patrons using screen readers. Assistance dogs are welcome; ask at the cloakroom.

House rules

Latecomers are seated at a suitable break in the programme. Photography during the performance is not permitted. Held seats lapse at curtain and are returned to sale; collected tickets are not exchanged after the interval.
