Privacy

What the box office keeps, and for how long

Aurelia Theatres Ltd, 11 Granville Street, company number 00000000, is responsible for the records described here.

What the box office records

A booking record holds the name the seats were reserved under, the performance, the seats themselves, what was paid, and the collection code issued at purchase. Where a booking reaches us through a will-call request, the record also holds the terms of that request, because the counter must be able to show why it sold what it sold. Nothing else about you is written down. We ask for no address, no date of birth and no marketing preferences, and the counter has no field to type them into.

Why we keep it

We keep booking records to seat the house, to settle takings against the production's contract, and to answer a patron who arrives at the window without a code. Card payments are taken by our payment provider at the counter terminal; the theatre never holds a full card number, and what returns to us is the last four digits and an authorisation reference, both of which sit on the takings sheet rather than in your booking record.

How long

  • Booking records: the season they belong to, and the season after it.
  • Takings sheets and payment references: seven years, as the accounts require.
  • Access requirements you tell us about: kept only while the booking stands, then removed with it.
  • Telephone notes at the counter: destroyed at the end of the run.

Who sees it

Box office and front-of-house staff see booking records for performances they are working. A visiting production receives a settlement statement of seats sold and money taken, never patron names. We pass nothing to advertisers or ticket resellers, we run no tracking on this site beyond the session that carries your booking through its steps, and we send no post or mail you did not ask for. Where the law obliges us to disclose a record, we disclose that record and no more.

Asking us about your record

Write to the Box Office Manager, The Aurelia Playhouse, 11 Granville Street, or ring 0151 496 0114 from noon daily, and we will tell you what we hold against your name, correct it, or delete it where no accounting duty requires us to keep it. We answer within a month. If our answer does not satisfy you, the theatre's trustees hear complaints about records at their quarterly meeting and will write to you afterwards.
