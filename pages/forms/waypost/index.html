How Waypost works

One network, one rate card

Waypost carries parcels between zones on a single Standard service, with collection from your own address and delivery to a street address or a neighbourhood collection point. Every parcel is priced from the zone pair of the origin and destination postcodes and from its chargeable weight, so a light but bulky box prices on volume rather than mass. The rates guide explains how the figures are built, and the rate calculator returns an exact figure for a specific parcel.

Book in the morning, collect the same day

Shipments created before 18:00 can be collected on the next working day, and recurring collections can be added to your account so you never have to book one twice. Your next scheduled collection is 30 July 2026 between 09:00 and 12:00 at 41 Foundry Lane, Unit 2. Print the labels before the window opens and leave the parcels where the driver can reach them.

Billing on account

Nothing is charged at the point of booking. Everything you send in a calendar month is gathered into one statement, with the fuel levy already included in each line and taxes added by destination. Statements sit on the invoices page for seven years.

Bigger consignments

Parcels over 32 kg, single dimensions over 200 cm, pallets, multi-piece consignments and dangerous goods move to a freight booking priced by an account manager rather than by the calculator. Write to us with the dimensions and the lane and we will come back with a rate.

Working with your own systems

For teams that ship from a shop or an order tool.

The Waypost API creates shipments, returns labels and streams tracking events straight into your own order system, so warehouse staff never have to retype an address. Keys, endpoints and the event reference are on the developers page.

If something looks wrong before you reach for support, check service status first, then the help center. Anything else reaches a person at support@waypost.example — quote the shipment or invoice number and the team can find the booking straight away.
