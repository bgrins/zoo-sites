Privacy policy

How Waypost Logistics handles the information you give us when you hold an account or send a parcel. Last revised 1 June 2026.

What we collect

Account details such as names, billing contacts and payment references, and shipment details such as sender and recipient addresses, parcel dimensions and weights, and tracking events recorded as parcels move through our network.

How we use it

To collect, carry and deliver parcels, to invoice the account, to respond to claims and support requests, and to keep aggregate statistics about network performance. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Delivery addresses and contact details are shared with the local carriers and drivers who complete the delivery, and with authorities where the law requires it. Nothing else is shared without your instruction.

Retention and your choices

Shipment records are kept for seven years to meet accounting and carriage obligations, then deleted. You may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold, or ask us to correct it, by writing to support@waypost.example.
