Terms of carriage

The conditions that apply to every parcel tendered to Waypost Logistics on the Standard service. Effective 1 March 2026.

Service limits

Waypost Standard carries single parcels up to 32 kg gross weight with no single dimension over 200 cm. Parcels outside these limits, multi-piece consignments, pallets and dangerous goods move to a freight booking priced by an account manager. Each parcel is charged on its chargeable weight, the greater of its gross and volumetric weight.

Prohibited items

  • Cash, bearer instruments and precious metals
  • Live animals and plants
  • Perishable food and goods requiring refrigeration
  • Firearms, ammunition and explosives
  • Undeclared dangerous goods, including loose lithium batteries

Tendering a prohibited item voids all liability and may result in the parcel being returned, destroyed or handed to authorities at the sender's cost.

Liability

Unless additional cover is declared and paid for at booking, our liability for loss or damage is limited to the lesser of the repair or replacement value of the contents and $100 per shipment. Additional cover may be declared at booking up to $5,000 per shipment. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for delay.

Claims

Claims for loss or damage must reach support@waypost.example within 30 days of the booked delivery date, with the shipment number and evidence of the contents' value. Packaging must be retained until the claim closes.

Changes

We may revise these terms with 30 days' notice to the account's billing contact. Bookings made before a revision takes effect are carried under the terms in force when they were booked.
