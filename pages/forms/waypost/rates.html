How Waypost Standard rates work

Zone-based pricing

Every rate is derived from the zone pair of the origin and destination postcodes. Lanes within the same zone price lower than cross-zone lanes, and remote-area destinations attract a surcharge that is added at booking.

Chargeable weight

Each parcel is priced on its chargeable weight: the greater of the gross weight on the scale and the volumetric weight calculated from the parcel's outer dimensions. Lightweight but bulky parcels are therefore priced on volume rather than mass. The calculator shows both figures with every quote.

Fuel levy

The fuel levy is already included in every quoted rate. Taxes are added at booking based on the destination.

Limits

Parcels over 32 kg, or with any single dimension over 200 cm, cannot be carried on Standard and move to a freight booking priced by an account manager. Multi-piece consignments, pallets and dangerous goods are also priced by an account manager.
