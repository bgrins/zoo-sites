What Waypost Standard includes

Collection from a booked pickup or any drop-off point, line haul, and one delivery attempt with a second attempt the next working day.

When to use freight instead

Parcels over 32 kg, or with any single dimension over 200 cm, cannot be carried on Standard and are referred to a freight booking priced by an account manager.
