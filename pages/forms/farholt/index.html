Farholt Freight Union

A mutual freight union of licensed carriers and customs agents. Since 1934 the union has cleared, carried and warehoused consignments for its member traders, working through a network of licensed branch offices.

What the union does

Customs clearance

Union agents prepare and lodge customs entries on behalf of consignors and consignees, and attend examinations at the quay or railhead.

Cartage

Licensed cartage between depots and door addresses within each branch's district, booked through the traffic desk and carried under the union's terms of carriage.

Bonded storage

Goods awaiting clearance or onward carriage may be held in bond at branches equipped with bonded stores. Charges accrue by the week.

Where to start

  • Read the services the union offers to member traders.
  • Check the tariff classes before asking the traffic desk for a quotation.
  • Use the branch directory to identify the branch that holds the cartage licence for your destination district.
