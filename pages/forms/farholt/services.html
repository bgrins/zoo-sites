Services

Four standing services are offered at every branch. All work is booked through the traffic desk and carried under the union's terms of carriage.

Customs entries

Union agents prepare import and export entries, lodge them with the customs house and attend any examination called on the consignment. Entries are drawn from the shipping documents lodged at booking; late or incomplete papers delay clearance and are held at the consignor's risk.

Cartage

Collection and delivery between depots and door addresses within the booking branch's district. Cartage is booked against a branch code attached by the traffic desk; use the branch directory to identify the correct branch before booking. Bookings close at 16:30 local time.

Bonded storage

Goods awaiting clearance may be warehoused in bond at harbour and rail branches. Storage is charged by the week, and goods are released only against a cleared entry or a transfer order to another bond.

Consignment insurance

Cover above the standard liability limit may be taken at booking by declaring the consignment's value and paying the premium quoted by the traffic desk. Claims under a declared-value booking follow the ordinary claims process.
