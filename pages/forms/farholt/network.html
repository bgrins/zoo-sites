The network

The union works through licensed branch offices. Each branch holds the cartage licence for its own district, and every consignment is cleared through the branch licensed for its destination.

Branch classes

Harbour branches

Established at the quaysides, harbour branches handle ship manifests, quay examinations and transhipment. They are the union's oldest offices and carry the heaviest customs traffic.

Rail branches

Rail branches sit at the principal railheads and marshal consignments moving by wagon. They receive traffic handed forward from the harbours and break it down for inland delivery.

Inland branches

Inland branches serve districts away from the water and the main lines. They book cartage, receive claims and keep the district ledgers, but pass customs work back up the chain.

Bonded storage

Bonded storage is available at harbour and rail branches only. Inland branches hold no bond; goods requiring bonded storage are handed off to the nearest harbour office and held there until cleared.

Finding your branch

Branch licences are reassigned from time to time, so no printed list of offices is published here. To identify the branch that holds the licence for a destination district, work through the branch directory and confirm the branch before booking with the traffic desk.
