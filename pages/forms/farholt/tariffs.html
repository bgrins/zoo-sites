Tariffs

Carriage is charged by tariff class, not by a published price list. The class is set by the zone of the destination branch, and the figure itself is quoted at booking by the traffic desk.

Tariff classes

Harbour zone

The lowest class. Consignments to a harbour branch move over the shortest cartage legs and clear at the quay where they land.

Rail zone

An intermediate class covering the rail leg from the harbour and cartage within the railhead district.

Inland zone

The highest class. Inland deliveries carry the full harbour and rail legs plus road cartage to the door, and any bonded holding at the hand-off harbour is charged in addition.

How quotations work

No prices are published per branch, and no figure quoted for one district applies to another. The traffic desk quotes each booking on the tariff class, the chargeable weight or measure of the consignment, and any bonded storage or declared-value insurance taken with it.

Identify the destination branch in the branch directory first, then ring the traffic desk with the branch confirmed. Quotations hold for seven days.
