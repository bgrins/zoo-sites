Terms of carriage

Every consignment booked with the union is carried on these terms. Booking a consignment is acceptance of them.

Liability

The union's liability for loss or damage is limited to the standard scale unless a higher value is declared at booking and the insurance premium quoted by the traffic desk is paid. Loss or damage must be notified within 7 days as set out under claims; claims notified out of time are barred. The union is not liable for delay caused by the customs house or by weather closing the quays.

Dangerous goods

Dangerous goods must be declared in writing at booking, packed and marked to the union's satisfaction, and tendered only at branches licensed to receive them. Undeclared dangerous goods may be discharged, destroyed or rendered harmless at any time without notice, at the consignor's cost and without compensation.

Lien on consignments

The union holds a general lien over every consignment in its charge for all charges owed by the consignor, on any booking. Goods under lien are warehoused at the owner's cost and may be sold after 28 days' written notice, the proceeds applied to the debt and the balance held for the owner.

Booking hours

Cartage bookings close at 16:30 local time at every branch. Goods tendered after close are booked to the next working day and warehoused overnight at the consignor's cost.
