Traffic desk

The traffic desk takes every booking, quotes every tariff and attaches the branch code to each consignment. Identify the destination branch in the branch directory before ringing.

Before you ring

Have the confirmed destination branch, the chargeable weight or measure of the consignment, and your settlement account number if you hold one. Quotations are given against the tariff class of the destination zone and hold for seven days.
