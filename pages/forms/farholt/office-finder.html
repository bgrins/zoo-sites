Notes

Codes

Branch codes are issued by the union registry and change when a licence is transferred. Always re-check the directory before quoting a code from an older booking.

Bonded work

Bonded storage is available at harbour and rail branches only. Inland branches hand off to the nearest harbour office.

Corrections

Report a missing or closed branch to the registry at registry@halveck-freight.example.
