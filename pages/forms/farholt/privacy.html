Privacy notice

What the union records about its member traders, and why.

Records kept

The union keeps booking records, consignment notes, customs entries and settlement account ledgers. These records name the consignor, the consignee and the persons authorised to book against an account, and nothing more than the trade requires.

Use and disclosure

Records are used to carry and clear consignments, to render statements and to assess claims. They are disclosed only to the customs house when the law requires it, and are never sold or passed to any other party.

Retention and enquiries

Booking and ledger records are retained for seven years and then destroyed. To see the records held against your name, write to the registry at Registry House, 4 Ordnance Wharf, Korrin, or to registry@farholt.example.
