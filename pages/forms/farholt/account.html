Account

Regular shippers may settle monthly instead of paying each booking at the counter. Settlement accounts are opened in person at any branch counter; bring the trading name, the registered address and a specimen signature for each person authorised to book against the account.

Account facilities

Account holders book cartage and customs work by telephone against the account number, receive one statement per calendar month, and settle by draft or standing credit within 14 days of the statement date. Accounts in arrears are suspended and bookings revert to counter terms.
