Operations handbook — depot extract

Extract of the sections most used on shift at depot 41. The full handbook is held in the depot office.

Shift handover

  1. Outgoing supervisor walks the incoming supervisor through open items on the dispatch board and any quarantined loads in receiving.
  2. Handheld scanners are counted, docked and signed over in the handover book.
  3. The incoming supervisor confirms the state of the nightly attestation on the attestation queue before accepting the shift.

Scanner use

  1. Sign in to the handheld with your own operator code; codes must not be shared.
  2. Scan pallet moves at the time of the move, not from memory at the end of shift.
  3. At the end of the nightly count, export from the handheld and transmit through the depot console. The receipt code is the only proof the count was filed.
  4. Report faulty scanners to the ops desk and place them in the red dock slot.

Escalation to the ops desk

The regional operations desk covers 20:00 to 06:00 for all depots. Escalate held runs, failed temperature checks, late attestations and any door or plant alarms.

Telephone: +44 1632 960 415

During office hours contact the depot manager first; the ops desk remains the route for anything affecting more than one depot.
