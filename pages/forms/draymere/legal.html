Privacy notice

The depot console is operated by Draymere Cold Chain Limited for staff and approved contractors. Operator codes, transmission logs and shift records are processed to run depot operations, to meet food safety obligations and to support regional audit.

Records are retained for the current audit year plus six years, then destroyed. Access is limited to depot supervisors, the regional operations desk and auditors. Console activity is logged against the signed-in operator code.

Data subject requests go to privacy@draymere.example or in writing to the registered office below.

Terms of service

Use of the depot console is subject to these terms.

  • Access is granted per operator; you must not share your operator code or act under another operator's session.
  • Entries made through the console form part of the company's operational and audit record and must be accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • The company may suspend console access during maintenance windows published on the service status page.
  • Misuse of the console is handled under the staff disciplinary procedure.
