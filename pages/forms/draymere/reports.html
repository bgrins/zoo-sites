Reports index — depot 41

Period reports available to depot supervisors. Regional and multi-depot reports are compiled at the regional office and are not available from this console.

  • Attestation queue

    Filing state of the nightly count attestation for each period in the open audit window, with the due period highlighted.

  • Period count summary

    Pallet positions by chamber at the close of each period, as reconciled by the regional office from each accepted count. The current position is shown on the inventory tab.

  • Period movements

    Inbound and outbound pallet movements per period, reconciled against the dispatch board and inbound receipts.

  • Temperature excursion log

    Excursions recorded per period under the cold chain policy, with disposition rulings from the regional operations desk.

For anything covering more than one depot, contact the regional office; depot consoles only hold local period data.
