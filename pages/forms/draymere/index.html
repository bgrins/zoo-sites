What the shift runs through here

Before the shift starts

Take the handover from the outgoing supervisor, walk the open items above, and confirm the state of the nightly attestation before you accept the shift. Sign in to your handheld with your own operator code and check that the scanners on the dock are docked and counted.

Through the operating day

Loads are probed at the tailgate as they arrive and put away by chamber; failed checks stay on the inbound dock until the regional operations desk rules on disposition. Outbound trailers are pre-cooled and sealed before the depart time on the board, and a held run only moves once the shift supervisor releases it. Record every pallet move on the handheld at the time of the move, because the nightly count reconciles against those movements rather than against memory.

Before the count window closes

Export the count from the handheld and transmit it through this console during the window. The console issues a receipt on acceptance, and that receipt is the only proof the count was filed. Anything transmitted after the window is treated as a late filing and is escalated to the regional operations desk.

Console sections

  • Dispatch

    Outbound runs for the operating day, with trailer, depart time and release state.

  • Receiving

    Inbound loads booked into the depot, with the tailgate temperature check result.

  • Inventory

    Pallet positions by chamber as at the close of the last accepted count.

  • Attestations

    Filing state of each period in the open audit window, and the intake form for the period that is due.

  • Vehicles

    Units allocated to the depot, with inspection dates and open defect notes.

  • Reports

    Period reports available to depot supervisors.

Reference and escalation

Procedure sits in the operations handbook and setpoints, excursion limits and quarantine rules in the cold chain policy. Check intake service status before reporting a transmission problem, since the console stays readable during a maintenance window while transmissions are refused.

The regional operations desk covers 20:00 to 06:00 for every depot. Reach it on +44 1632 960 415 or at ops.night@draymere.example. During office hours raise it with the depot manager first.
