Visiting the showroom

Building C sits on one level. The visitor entrance off Switchback Road has a ramped approach with a handrail on the right, a level threshold, and a door that a staff member will hold open on request. Two marked accessible spaces sit nearest that door, and the gravel apron is compacted rather than loose.

The washroom is on the ground floor with a turning circle and grab rails. Seating is available in the showroom and in the yard, and there is no step between the two.

The service bay is a working floor with air lines, cables and open trenches. Come in with a staff member rather than alone, and ask for a bay walk-through to be scheduled outside the busiest hours.

Getting into a trailer

All three chassis are compact by design, and the standard step-in height is high enough to matter to some owners. These are the numbers we are asked for most often.

  • Cabin door width24 in on all three chassis
  • Step-in height, standard21 in from level ground
  • Step-in height, lowered axle17 in from level ground
  • Galley counter height34 in, or 30 in on request
  • Cabin sitting headroomFull sitting height on all three

What a build can change

  • A lowered axle and a wider fold-out step, ordered together, drop the step-in height and give it a handhold.
  • Grab handles inside the cabin door and beside the galley hatch, positioned on the side you actually use.
  • A galley counter cut lower than standard, with the burner slide set back so it can be reached seated.
  • Latches and catches specified for one-handed operation throughout, in place of the standard twin-catch hatch hardware.
  • Panel switches with raised markings, and a cabin light circuit that can be switched from the bed rather than the door.
  • Hand-control hitch handles that need no downward push to lock.

None of these carry a charge on a new build, because they are specification choices rather than additions. Some can be retrofitted; call the shop with your VIN and we will say which.

This site and our paperwork

Pages here are built to work at any text size, from the keyboard alone, and with a screen reader. Color is never the only way a status or a required field is marked, and nothing on the site moves or plays on its own.

The brochure, price sheet and owner's manual are all available as large-print or plain-text copies, and a specialist will read a spec sheet through over the phone if that is easier than reading it on a screen. If something here gets in your way, write to the shop and describe what happened; we would rather fix the page than send a workaround.
