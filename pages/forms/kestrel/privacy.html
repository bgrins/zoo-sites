How we handle what you send us

Privacy

What we collect

  • Brochure requests: your name and email address, which is all we need to send the brochure and price sheet.
  • Anything else you choose to add on the brochure form (phone, date of birth, income bracket, taxpayer ID) is optional. We use it only to prepare a financing pre-check when you ask a build specialist for one.
  • Orders and deposits: the contact, delivery and payment details on your build agreement.
  • Warranty and service: your VIN, build sheet and the history of work on your trailer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.
  • We share a build with the dealer you name, so they can arrange delivery or a warranty inspection, and with nobody else.
  • This site sets a session cookie for its forms and no advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting it

  • Brochure requests that do not become an order are deleted after two years.
  • Build and warranty records are kept for the life of the frame warranty plus three years.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold, call 541-555-0142 or email privacy@kestrelpeak.example.
