Deposits and build slots

  • A $1,500 deposit reserves a build slot and is credited against the final price.
  • The deposit is refundable in full until the frame is cut, roughly three weeks before your build week.
  • Your spec stays open until the frame is cut; changes after that are quoted separately.

Price and payment

  • The price on your signed build sheet is fixed for that build, whatever the price sheet later says.
  • The balance is due at pickup from the shop, or before a dealer delivery is dispatched.
  • Oregon has no sales tax; buyers registering a trailer in another state pay that state's tax and fees there.

Delivery and warranty

  • Title passes when the balance clears. Delivery by a dealer is at the dealer's published rate.
  • The six-year frame warranty and two-year component warranty start on the delivery date.
