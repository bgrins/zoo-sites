Option groups

Galley packages

  • Trailhead: single-burner slide, 42 L cooler tray, birch counter.
  • Basecamp: two-burner slide, 55 L compressor fridge, pull-out prep board.
  • Expedition: Basecamp plus hot-water tap and a second pantry drawer.

Off-road running gear

  • Independent trailing-arm suspension with 4 in of travel.
  • All-terrain tires on 15 in wheels, matched full-size spare.
  • Articulating hitch and stone guards for washboard roads.

Solar upgrade

  • Every chassis ships with 200 W on the roof and a 100 Ah battery.
  • The 400 W upgrade adds a second panel and a 200 Ah bank.
  • Both systems charge from the tow vehicle while underway.

Awnings

  • Side awning: 8 ft roll-out with quick-pitch legs.
  • Galley awning: covers the hatch and counter in one pull.
  • 270-degree wrap for the Col 12 and Cirque 14.

Build slots and deposits

  1. Agree a spec with a build specialist and receive a written quote, valid for 30 days.
  2. A refundable $1,500 deposit reserves the next open slot on the jig. Slots are assigned in deposit order.
  3. Lead time runs 14 to 22 weeks from deposit depending on chassis; we confirm your build week about a month out.
  4. The spec can be revised until your frame is cut, roughly three weeks before the build week.
  5. Balance is due at pickup from the Switchback Road shop, or before dealer delivery.
