Six-year frame warranty

  • The aluminium monocoque frame is covered against weld failure and structural corrosion for six years from delivery.
  • Coverage transfers once to a second owner if we are notified within 60 days of the sale.
  • Running gear, appliances and electrics carry the two-year component warranty instead.
  • Damage from overloading beyond the rated payload, or from unrepaired collision damage, is excluded.
  • Claims start with a phone call to the shop; most inspections happen at your nearest dealer.
