Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you request an appointment we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the visit details you provide. We use this information only to schedule and confirm your care.

This site sets no advertising cookies and shares no data with marketing networks.

How long we keep it

Appointment requests that do not lead to a visit are deleted after ninety days. Records for patients of the practice are retained as required by state dental-records law.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your contact details at any time by calling 312-555-0188 or writing to privacy@drennhilldental.example. Health-record rights are described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.
