Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how health information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to it. Please review it carefully.

How we use and disclose health information

We use your health information for treatment (planning and providing your dental care, including referrals to specialists), for payment (submitting claims to your dental plan), and for practice operations (quality review and appointment reminders).

Other uses and disclosures — such as for research or marketing — are made only with your written authorization, which you may revoke at any time.

Your rights

  • Inspect and receive a copy of your dental record.
  • Request corrections to information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures.
  • Ask to be contacted at a specific number or address.
  • Receive a list of disclosures we have made outside routine care.

Questions and complaints

Contact our privacy officer, Sam Whitfield, at 312-555-0188 or privacy@drennhilldental.example. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. We will never retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
