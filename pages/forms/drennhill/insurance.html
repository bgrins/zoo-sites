Insurance and billing

We keep billing simple: we confirm your coverage before treatment and give you a written quote after your examination.

Accepted plans

  • Aurora Dental Plan
  • Blue Summit
  • Drennhill Direct

If your plan is not listed, call our billing coordinator — many plans reimburse care at our practice even when we are out of network.

Self-pay arrangements

Patients without dental coverage can pay per visit or join a hygiene plan that spreads routine care across the year. For larger treatment plans we can arrange staged payments; ask before treatment begins.

Billing notes

We submit claims on your behalf and estimate your share in writing before any treatment. Co-payments are due on the day of the visit, and statements go out at the start of each month.
