Accessibility

Every patient should be able to reach us, move through the practice, and take part in decisions about their care.

Getting to the practice

  • Step-free access from the street to Suite 210 via the building elevator.
  • An accessible restroom on our floor and a wheelchair-transfer treatment room.
  • Two accessible parking bays beside the Harborview Ave entrance.

During your visit

The reception desk is fitted with a hearing loop; ask any coordinator to switch it on. Treatment plans and consent forms are available in large print, and we can slow the pace of any appointment on request.

Requesting accommodations

Call 312-555-0188 or email access@drennhilldental.example before your visit and tell us what would help — an interpreter, extra time, or anything else. Two business days of notice lets us arrange most accommodations, but we will always do what we can on the day.
