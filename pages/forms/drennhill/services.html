Our services

Preventive and restorative care for the whole family. Fees for all treatments are quoted after examination, so you know the cost before any work begins.

Cleanings and hygiene plans

Routine cleanings with one of our hygienists, including scaling, polish, and a gum-health check. Most patients visit every six months.

Our hygiene plans spread the cost of two annual cleanings and any interim fluoride treatments across the year. Ask a coordinator for details at your next visit.

Whitening and fillings

In-chair whitening completed in a single appointment, with take-home trays available for gradual top-ups. We review shade goals with you first.

Tooth-colored composite fillings matched to your natural shade. Older amalgam fillings can be assessed and replaced where appropriate.

Consultations

New-patient examinations and second-opinion consultations cover a full charting of your teeth and gums, any needed imaging, and a written treatment plan. Fees are quoted after examination.
