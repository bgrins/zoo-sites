Getting here

  • Parking: the building lot on Tanner Street is free for patients for the first two hours. Two accessible bays sit beside the Harborview Ave entrance.
  • Bus: routes 14 and 31 stop outside the library, a two-minute walk.
  • Bicycles: racks under cover at the Tanner Street entrance.

The elevator is to the left of the lobby; Suite 210 is the second door on the right. See accessibility for step-free access and on-site support.

Dental emergencies

If you have a knocked-out tooth, swelling that is spreading, or bleeding that will not stop, call us first thing: we keep same-day slots every morning for patients in pain.

Outside opening hours, the recorded message on 312-555-0188 gives the number of the on-call dentist. For facial swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.
