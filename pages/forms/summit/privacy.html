Privacy notice

Controller Solstice Conference Services Ltd Last updated 1 June 2026

We hold what it takes to print your badge, seat you at a roundtable and send you an invoice, and nothing we sell.

What we collect

  • The name and work email of every attendee entered at group registration — printed on the badge exactly as typed
  • The group code and the size of the delegation — for badge collection and invoicing
  • Anything you write to the delegation desk — access needs, dietary requirements, purchase order references, name changes

How we use it

To print badges, place delegations at the sector roundtables, send confirmations and room changes, and invoice the organisation that registered. Access and dietary notes go to the venue team and the caterers and to nobody else.

No sponsor lists Sponsors and exhibitors never receive attendee names or emails, as the sponsors page sets out. A badge is never scanned without being handed over first.

How long we keep it

Attendee records are kept until the invoice for the delegation is settled and the summit accounts are closed, and for six years after that where the law requires financial records to be kept. Conduct reports follow the shorter rule set out in the code of conduct.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted once the summit is over. Write to the delegation desk at groups@solstice-events.example with your group code. If you are not satisfied with our answer you may complain to the data protection regulator.
