Solstice Conference Services presents

Sponsors

Exhibition Hall E, all three days Stands Eleven, none in a session room

Sponsors keep the delegate rate where it is and pay for the bursary places. In return they get a stand in Hall E and a named session — and nothing on the platform in anyone else's slot.

Principal partner

Verkholm Group

Workforce planning · Main Auditorium host

Verkholm has backed the summit for four editions and hosts the Main Auditorium, which means the morning keynotes and the day-two panel. Their planners run an open clinic at the Hall E stand through the afternoons for anyone wrestling with a headcount plan they cannot make add up.

The bursary fund

A share of every sponsorship goes to the bursary fund, which covers places for people leading teams inside organisations that cannot send a delegation at all — small charities, single-site care providers, volunteer-run services. Bursary delegates register through the delegation desk rather than the public form, and nothing on their badge says which places were funded.

What sponsors do not get

No delegate list Sponsors never receive attendee names or emails. A stand conversation is the only route to a delegate, and a badge is never scanned without being handed over first.

No platform time Named sessions are named, not sold. Every speaker on the programme was invited by the organiser, and a sponsor cannot buy a slot or veto one.

No session-room stands All eleven stands are in Hall E, so a session room is a session room.

Sponsoring the next edition

Packages for the next edition open once this one closes, and the Hall E floor plan is redrawn each year rather than rolled over. Write to the organiser at groups@solstice-events.example and the sponsorship lead will call you back with what is left and what it costs.
