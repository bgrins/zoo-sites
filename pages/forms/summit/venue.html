Solstice Conference Services presents

Venue

Dates 14–16 October 2026 Venue Harborview Exhibition Halls

The summit takes over the Harborview Exhibition Halls, a five-hall waterfront complex ten minutes from the central station.

Getting there

Tram Lines 3 and 7 stop at Harborview Quay, directly outside the north entrance.

Rail From the central station, a signposted ten-minute walk along the quay promenade.

Car Paid parking beneath Hall E; enter from Quay Road. Spaces are limited on keynote mornings.

Halls layout

  • Main Auditorium — keynotes and plenary panels
  • Hall A — Leading Teams track and workshops
  • Hall B — Leading Change track and breakout rooms
  • Hall C — badge collection, open from 08:00 each day
  • Hall E — catering, exhibitors and the quiet lounge

Badge collection

Collect badges in Hall C from 08:00. One member of your delegation presents the group code issued at group registration and receives badges for the whole group.

Nearby hotels

  • The Quayside House — across from the north entrance, 2 min walk
  • The Lantern Court — old town, 8 min by tram
  • Harbour Gate Inn — beside the central station, 10 min walk
