Registration

Dates 14–16 October 2026 Venue Harborview Exhibition Halls Intake Delegations, one submission each

Everyone registers as a delegation — a team of three to twelve, or a delegation of one. The desk would rather have one submission it can read than twelve it has to reconcile.

How the intake works

Open group registration and enter every attendee's name and work email, one entry per person. Add rows until the list matches the people who are actually coming. Submit once, and the desk issues a single group code for the whole delegation.

Badges are printed from exactly what is typed on that form, so a nickname in the name field becomes a nickname on the lanyard. One person brings the group code to Hall C from 08:00 and collects every badge in the delegation in one go.

Before you fill it in

Get the emails right

Work addresses only. Every confirmation, room change and roundtable placement goes to the address on the form, and the desk cannot forward from a personal inbox it does not hold.

One list, not several

Four separate submissions of one attendee each produce four delegations and four codes, and the badge desk will hand you one badge at a time. Grow the form instead.

What a place includes

  • Every keynote, track session and workshop — nothing is ticketed separately
  • The Wednesday welcome reception — Harborview Terrace, from 16:30
  • Catering across all three days — Hall E
  • A sector roundtable seat — assigned when the delegation is confirmed

Changes and cancellations

Name changes Swap a name for a colleague's up to the Friday before the summit by writing to the delegation desk with your group code. The seat stays with the delegation, so a late substitution costs nothing.

Adding people Register the extra attendees as a second delegation and tell the desk which existing group code to attach them to. The desk merges the two before badges are printed.

Withdrawing Write to the desk. A place released before the Friday deadline is refunded in full; after it, the delegation keeps the badge and may still substitute a name.

Invoicing Public-sector and healthcare delegations are invoiced to the organisation rather than paid at registration. Send a purchase order reference to the desk with the group code and the invoice follows within a week.

On the day

Hall C opens at 08:00 all three days. Bring the group code; a printout, a photograph of the confirmation screen or the code written on paper are all equally welcome. The desk keeps a short queue for delegations of eight or more so a large team does not hold up the line behind it.

Tell the desk about access needs, dietary requirements or a quiet-lounge booking at the same time as you register, and it will all be arranged before you arrive. The venue page has the hall layout, and the code of conduct applies to everyone holding a badge.
