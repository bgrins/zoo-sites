Solstice Conference Services presents

Solstice Leadership Summit

Dates 14–16 October 2026 Venue Harborview Exhibition Halls Tracks Leading Teams · Leading Change

Three days on the waterfront for people who carry other people's work: six speakers, two tracks, and rooms small enough that the questions get answered.

The two tracks

Leading Teams

Hall A. Hiring, delegation, feedback and the daily craft of keeping a team steady — sessions built for managers of managers and the people about to become one.

Leading Change

Hall B. Stakeholder maps, succession, and change programmes that finish without the customary exodus. Sessions run for anyone answerable for a transition already under way.

Both tracks Keynotes and plenary panels bring the whole summit into the Main Auditorium each morning, so nobody has to choose between the two headline sessions of the day. Full timings sit on the programme.

Who comes

Roughly six hundred delegates, most of them arriving in groups of three to twelve from the same organisation. Operations, public-sector programme teams, logistics, healthcare administration and founder-led firms make up the bulk of the room, and the sector roundtables on the Thursday afternoon are grouped accordingly.

Delegations tell us the roundtables and the terrace reception are where the summit earns its keep, so both are scheduled generously rather than squeezed around the keynotes.

What a place includes

  • All keynotes, track sessions and workshops — no session is ticketed separately
  • The Wednesday welcome reception — Harborview Terrace, from 16:30
  • Catering across all three days — Hall E, alongside the quiet lounge
  • Sector roundtable seat — chosen when your delegation is confirmed

Registering a delegation

Groups register once, as a delegation, rather than person by person. Enter each attendee's name and work email at group registration and the desk issues a single group code; one person brings that code to Hall C from 08:00 and collects every badge in one go.

Names can be changed up to the Friday before the summit by writing to the delegation desk. Sole delegates are welcome and register the same way, as a delegation of one.

Before you travel

Getting there Trams 3 and 7 stop at Harborview Quay, and the central station is a ten-minute walk along the promenade. Parking under Hall E fills early on keynote mornings.

Staying over Three hotels hold rooms for the summit, the nearest two minutes from the north entrance. Details on the venue page.

Access The halls are level throughout and the quiet lounge in Hall E is open all three days. Tell the delegation desk what you need and it will be arranged before you arrive.
