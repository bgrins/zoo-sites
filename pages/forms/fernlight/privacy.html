Privacy notice

What Fernlight Software Ltd collects, and why. Version 2.1.

Who we are

Fernlight Software Ltd, 42 Marrask Row, Suite 5, Pellowick PW4 7AL, is the controller of the personal data described here. Write to privacy@fernlight.example with any question about it.

Waitlist requests

A waitlist request records your name, your email address, the referral field and the time it arrived. We use them to place the request in the queue, to send your install link and workspace key when a seat opens, and to answer you if you write to us about the request. The record is deleted when the programme ends, as clause 3 of the beta terms describes.

Client telemetry

The beta client reports crash traces, timing samples and feature usage counts, tied to the workspace rather than to a named person. Clause 7 of the beta terms explains why telemetry cannot be switched off during the beta; the telemetry settings explain how to reduce it and how to inspect a payload before it is sent.

This site

These pages set one session cookie, which the waitlist form needs to accept a request. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we answer within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer you may complain to the UK data protection regulator.
