Policies

The notices below cover the twenty-second meeting and the services run from the abstract desk.

Privacy notice

The desk holds the details delegates give it: names, affiliations, contact addresses, dietary and access notes, and the study records and text lodged for the printed programme. This information is used only to run the meeting, produce the programme, and correspond about lodged material.

Lodged text appears in the printed programme under the delegate's name; access and dietary notes are seen only by desk staff and the station housekeeper. Records are kept until the close of the following meeting and then destroyed, except for the printed programme itself, which is archived in the station library. Write to desk@nerrowstrait.example to see or correct what the desk holds about you.

Terms of participation

Registration covers admission to all plenaries and sessions, the welcome supper, and the printed programme. Registrations may be transferred to a named colleague at any point but are refundable only until the middle of October.

Delegates speak and present on the understanding that others may cite what they hear with attribution. Photography and recording in session rooms require the speaker's consent and the chair's. The station's shore and laboratories are working facilities: delegates follow staff instructions there, and the station accepts no liability for personal equipment brought to the meeting.

The convenors may decline or withdraw participation from anyone who disrupts the meeting or disregards these terms; decisions of the convenors are final.
