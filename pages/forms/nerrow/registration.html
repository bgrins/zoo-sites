Registration

How a delegate is enrolled for the twenty-second meeting, what a place carries with it, and the dates by which the desk needs to hear from you.

The meeting is deliberately small: the lecture hall seats a hundred and thirty, and the desk stops enrolling once the poster corridor and the two parallel session rooms are full. Places are given out in the order they are asked for, with a standing reservation of twenty for student delegates until the middle of October.

Enrolling

Write to the desk at desk@nerrowstrait.example with your name as it should be printed, your affiliation, and whether you are presenting. Presenters should also say which study record their material was lodged under at the abstract desk, so the enrolment and the placement can be read together.

The desk answers on weekdays between 09:00 and 16:00 and confirms an enrolment by return, usually the same day. Delegates without institutional email are enrolled the same way; the desk simply asks for a second contact address.

What a place carries

  • Admission to all four plenaries and to every parallel session.
  • A seat in the poster corridor for the two middle days, whether or not you present in it.
  • The welcome supper in the boathouse on the Tuesday evening.
  • The printed programme, and the pocket programme that carries the capsules.
  • Tea and midday meals in the station dining room on all four days.
  • A place on the low-tide foreshore walk, subject to the tide and to boots.

Lodging is not included and is not arranged by the desk. The station bunkhouse, the village guesthouses and the Sound House Inn are all described on the travel and lodging page.

Dates the desk works to

Friday 4 September 2026
Material for the printed programme must be lodged. Enrolment stays open after this, but a study lodged later cannot be placed in a session.
Friday 25 September 2026
Session placements returned to authors. Presenters confirm at this point that they will travel.
Friday 16 October 2026
Last day for access and dietary notes, for a bunkhouse or parking request, and for refundable withdrawal under the terms of participation.
Tuesday 3 November 2026
The desk opens in the station foyer at midday. Late enrolments are taken there if the hall has room.

Transfers, withdrawals and letters

A place may be transferred to a named colleague at any point, including on the morning of the meeting; write to the desk with both names. A withdrawal notified before the middle of October is refunded, and one notified after it is not, though the place may still be transferred.

Delegates who need a letter of invitation for a visa application should ask early. The desk issues them in the order they are asked for and cannot expedite one, since each is signed by a convenor who is not on the station every day.

Concessionary places for retired members and for delegates from the volunteer survey groups are settled case by case. Ask the desk rather than assuming the answer is no; the meeting has never turned away a surveyor who wanted to come.
