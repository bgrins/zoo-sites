Reviewers

The reviewer panel places lodged studies into sessions and advises the desk on the shape of the programme.

How review works

Material lodged at the abstract desk is first checked by desk staff for completeness. Each study record is then read by two members of the panel, chosen for subject fit, who recommend a session and flag anything that needs clarifying with the authors.

Reviewers declare a conflict for any study involving their own group or field site, and a third reader is brought in where the first two disagree. Placement decisions are returned through the desk within three weeks of lodging, and the panel's recommendations are final once the printed programme goes to press.
