Contact

One desk answers everything: enrolment, lodged material, session placements, lodging, access notes and the tide table. It is staffed by two people, so patience is repaid.

Write to desk@nerrowstrait.example. The desk reads mail on weekdays between 09:00 and 16:00 and answers most of it the same day. During the week of the meeting it moves into the station foyer and the mailbox goes unread until the Monday after, so anything needed on the day is better asked at the foyer table.

Reaching the desk

desk@nerrowstrait.example, weekdays 09:00 to 16:00. Put a study record number in the subject line if your question is about lodged material.

The Abstract Desk, Weyburn Point Marine Station, 4 Cobble Quay, Weyburn Point. Allow a week; the village post comes up on the ferry.
In person
The foyer table from midday on Tuesday 3 November, and through the meeting from 08:30 each morning.

What to ask whom

  • Enrolment, transfers, withdrawals and letters of invitation: the desk. See registration for the dates it works to.
  • Lodged material, capsules and confirmation of receipt: the desk, quoting the study record.
  • Session placement and anything that reads as a challenge to it: the desk, which passes it to the reviewer panel. Reviewers are not contacted directly, so that a placement stays a panel decision rather than a negotiation.
  • Timings, room changes and speaker order: the chair named for your session on the sessions page, or the desk before the meeting opens.
  • Bunkhouse places, station parking and the foreshore walk: the desk, by the middle of October.
  • Access and dietary notes: the desk, which passes them to the station housekeeper and to nobody else.

The convenors

The meeting is convened by Weyburn Point Marine Station with the Halden Sound Institute of Coastal Studies and Carrickmoor University. Convenors are on the station intermittently and do not hold a separate mailbox: mail addressed to a convenor by name reaches them through the desk, which is faster than it sounds.

Press enquiries and requests to photograph in session rooms go to the desk as well. Photography and recording need the speaker's consent and the chair's, as set out in the terms of participation.

Finding the station

The station stands on Cobble Quay, a short walk from the ferry steps at Weyburn Point. Crossings from Carrickmoor are tide-limited in November and the connecting coach from Halden meets only two arrivals a day, so settle the crossing before the room. Full directions, lodging and the step-free route into the hall are on the travel and lodging page.
