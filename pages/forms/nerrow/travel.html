Travel and lodging

Weyburn Point sits at the narrow end of the Nerrow Strait. Plan the crossing before booking anything else; __NERROW_MONTH__ sailings are tide-limited.

Getting to Weyburn Point

  • By water: the foot ferry from Carrickmoor harbour crosses in about fifty minutes, with five sailings a day in __NERROW_MONTH__. The station is a short walk along the quay from the ferry steps.
  • By rail and coach: mainline trains run to Halden; the connecting coach to Weyburn Point village meets the mid-morning and mid-afternoon arrivals.
  • By road: the coastal road ends at the village green. Parking at the station itself is limited to a handful of marked places and must be reserved through the desk.

Lodging

  • The station bunkhouse offers simple shared rooms a minute from the lecture hall. Places are few and go to student delegates first.
  • Guesthouses in Weyburn Point village hold rooms for the meeting until a month before it opens; mention the symposium when booking.
  • The Sound House Inn at Carrickmoor harbour suits delegates who prefer to cross by ferry each morning.

Accessibility

A step-free route runs from the ferry steps along Cobble Quay to the station entrance, and the lecture hall, poster corridor and dining room are all on the level ground floor. The Harbour Room, where the B sessions meet, is upstairs and reached by stair only, so a session with a registered need is moved into a ground-floor room.

A quiet room in the station annexe is available throughout the meeting, and reserved seating is kept at the front of the hall for lip readers. Send requests, including dietary and mobility notes, to the desk at desk@nerrowstrait.example by __NERROW_ACCESS_DM__.
