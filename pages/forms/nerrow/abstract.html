Field summary as filed

Between March and September 2025 the Weyburn Point field crew surveyed eleven bull kelp beds along the Nerrow Strait. Four beds were cut on a rotating fourteen-day harvest schedule; the remaining seven were left uncut as controls.

Cut beds regrew to 82 percent of control canopy density within six weeks, but holdfast counts in the two beds harvested closest to the spring recruitment window fell by roughly a fifth. The crew recommends moving the season's first cut to late April and capping removal at 30 percent of standing biomass.
