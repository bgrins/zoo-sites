Programme

An outline of the twenty-second meeting, __NERROW_CONVENED__, at Weyburn Point Marine Station. Session-level detail is on the sessions page.

__NERROW_DAY1__ — Shorelines observed

Registration from midday in the station foyer, followed by the opening plenary, The strait as archive: eight decades of shoreline notebooks, given by Prof. Maren Aldous of Carrickmoor University. Sessions 1A and 1B run in the afternoon, with a welcome supper in the boathouse from 18:30.

__NERROW_DAY2__ — Water and sediment in motion

Morning plenary: Sediment memory in a narrow channel, Dr. Tobias Reyner, Halden Sound Institute of Coastal Studies. Sessions 2A and 2B follow, and the afternoon closes with a low-tide walk along the station foreshore led by resident staff.

__NERROW_DAY3__ — Life along the strait

Morning plenary: Counting what moves: survey design for tidal passages, Dr. Sylvie Okafor, Northern Channel Observatory. Sessions 3A and 3B run through the late morning and 4A and 4B through the afternoon, with the poster corridor open until 19:00.

__NERROW_DAY4__ — Synthesis

Closing plenary: What the strait should be measuring by the thirtieth meeting, Prof. Callum Herriot, Weyburn Point Marine Station. A synthesis panel drawn from the session chairs closes the meeting at 13:00, in time for the afternoon sailings.
