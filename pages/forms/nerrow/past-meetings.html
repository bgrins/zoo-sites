Past meetings

Twenty-one meetings precede this one. The shape has barely changed: one plenary a morning, parallel sessions after it, and a walk on the foreshore whenever the tide allowed one.

The gap of __NERROW_YEAR-6__

No meeting was convened in __NERROW_YEAR-6__. Two field seasons were lost with it, and the seventeenth meeting the following year was given over largely to what could be reconstructed from partial records and from the volunteer survey groups, who had kept walking the shore throughout. Several of the methods papers that came out of that meeting are still the ones new delegates are pointed at.

Where the meetings were held

Nineteen of the twenty-one meetings convened at Weyburn Point. The eighth and the eleventh met at Carrickmoor while the station's lecture hall was being reroofed, and delegates who were there still describe the ferry crossings as the most productive part of the week.

The station has hosted since the meeting outgrew the boathouse, which happened somewhere around the fifth. The boathouse now takes only the welcome supper, which is the arrangement everybody prefers.

Proceedings and archives

Printed programmes for every meeting are held in the station library, along with the pocket programmes carrying the capsules from the eleventh meeting onward. The library is open to delegates through the week of the meeting and by arrangement at other times.

The station does not publish formal proceedings. Contributed talks are for the room they are given in, and authors are free to publish the same work anywhere afterwards without reference to the meeting. Requests to consult an archived programme go to the desk, which will have a copy pulled before you arrive.
