Policies

How the Trust handles applicants' information, the terms of the portal, and how to raise a concern

Privacy notice

The Thornbury Trust is the controller of the information you give the grants portal: the names of the principal applicant and host organization, the project details and budget in each application, and any correspondence with the grants office. We use it to assess applications, to administer awards and to publish the annual list of awards made, which names the grant holder, the host organization, the project title and the sum awarded.

Application details are seen by the grants office and by the members of the assessment panel considering the round. Unsuccessful applications are deleted two years after the round closes; the records of awards are kept for seven years after the final report. To see or correct what we hold, write to the grants office. You may also complain to the data protection regulator.

Terms of use

The portal is provided for preparing and tracking applications to the Trust's grant rounds. Drafts are held against your portal session and are cleared when a round closes; keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose. Submitting an application does not create any obligation on the Trust to fund it, and the decisions of the trustees on the panel's recommendations are final.

By queuing an application you confirm that its contents are accurate and that the host organization has agreed to hold any award. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Accessibility statement

The portal is built to be used with a keyboard alone and with screen readers: every form field has a visible label, errors are shown beside the section they concern, and nothing depends on colour alone. If any part of the portal is difficult to use, the grants office will take an application by email or on paper instead, and it will be assessed in the same round on the same terms.

Complaints

Write to the grants office, saying what went wrong and quoting your application reference if you have one. We acknowledge a complaint within five working days and reply in full within twenty. If you are not satisfied, ask for the complaint to be referred to the chair of trustees, whose decision closes the Trust's own procedure. You may then contact the charity regulator.

A complaint does not reopen an assessment decision: the panel's scoring is final for the round, though you are welcome to apply again.
