Attachments

Supporting documents for queued applications

When attachments open

Attachments cannot be added to a draft. Once a draft reaches the review queue, the grants office writes to the principal applicant to confirm what is required and how to send it.

Complete all five sections of the application form first. Once the draft is queued, it shows as queued in My applications.

Accepted formats

  • PDF for letters of support, site permissions and method summaries
  • CSV for budget lines, one line per cost with a short description

Other formats, including word-processor files and spreadsheets with formulas, are not accepted.

Size limits

  • Each PDF: no more than 10 MB
  • Each CSV of budget lines: no more than 1 MB
  • All attachments together: no more than 25 MB per application

Checklist

  • Draft queued before uploading
  • PDF and CSV only
  • Budget lines match the requested total
