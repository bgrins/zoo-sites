Assessment criteria

How the Round 14 panel weighs queued applications

The four criteria

Scientific merit
Whether the question is worth asking and the survey or sampling design can answer it.
Feasibility
Whether the applicant, host organization and stated duration make the work achievable within 24 months.
Value for money
Whether the budget is proportionate, in whole US dollars, and within the round's caps.
Site permissions
Whether access to the field sites is secured or clearly achievable before work begins.

Timetable

The assessment panel meets on 4 November and considers every application queued by the close of the round. Awards are confirmed on 19 November, and outcomes are published to the portal within eight weeks of the round closing.

Applicants are not interviewed. Questions about a queued application go to the grants office.
