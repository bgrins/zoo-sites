Applicant guidance

Round 14 · Read this page in full before starting an application

Awards and budgets

Round 14 makes awards of up to £12,000 for coastal ecology fieldwork. All figures must be given in whole pounds sterling, with no separators. Overheads charged by the host organization are capped at 8 per cent of the total request; budgets that exceed the cap are returned unassessed.

Work funded under the round must conclude within 24 months of the award being confirmed. Shorter projects are welcome and should state a realistic duration, for example 9 months.

What we fund

  • Survey and sampling fieldwork
  • Equipment hire and consumables
  • Field assistant time

What we do not fund

  • Permanent staff salaries
  • Overheads above 8 per cent
  • Conference attendance

Completing the form

The application form has five sections and saves automatically as you type. Only complete drafts reach the review queue; anything unfinished when the round closes on 30 September is cleared. Assessment is described on the assessment criteria page.

At a glance

  • Awards up to £12,000
  • Whole pounds only
  • Overheads capped at 8 per cent
  • Work concludes within 24 months
