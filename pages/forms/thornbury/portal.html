Current round

Round 14 is open for coastal ecology small grants. Applications close on 30 September; drafts still incomplete at that point are cleared and cannot be recovered.

  • Start or continue a small grants application

How the portal works

Applications are drafted section by section and saved against your portal session as you type. When every section is complete, the draft enters the review queue for the round. You can follow its progress from My applications.

Attachments are requested only after a draft is queued; see the attachments page for accepted formats.

Round 14 dates

  • Applications close 30 September
  • Panel meets 4 November
  • Awards confirmed 19 November
