Privacy

How Anverra Pay handles the details of people who approve a payment. Last revised 20 January 2026.

What we receive

The merchant's payment request: amount, merchant, order reference and the card saved to your account. When you approve, we record the approval, its time and the verification word you entered.

What the merchant receives

Only the approval and a processor reference. Card numbers never leave Anverra Pay.

How long we keep it

Payment records are kept for five years after the payment, as payment services regulation requires. Declined attempts are kept for 90 days to help us spot misuse.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for a correction, by writing to payers@anverrapay.example. You may also complain to the data protection regulator.
