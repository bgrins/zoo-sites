Trade accounts

Shop / Trade accounts

A trade account puts printing and binding businesses on monthly settlement and gives them first call on short stock. Accounts are approved by the counter, not by a form; expect a telephone call.

What an account gives you

Monthly settlement by transfer on the 15th, a standing 5% off list on paper and board, first refusal on rebuilt presses before they are listed, and priority at the cutting bench on Monday mornings.

Account orders placed before 2pm still leave the same working day; settlement terms do not change dispatch.

Opening an account

Write to counter@ollistercrane.example with the business name, the registered address, and two suppliers who will speak for you. New accounts start with a monthly limit of £1,500, reviewed after six months of clean settlement.

Payment terms and our obligations to account holders are set out in the terms of sale.
