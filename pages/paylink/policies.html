Shop / Terms of sale

Shipping

Flat cartons leave Tolbridge Yard on the next working day. Sheets over 22 x 30 in travel by pallet carrier and take three to five days within the county. We do not ship ink by air.

Returns

Uncut sheets may be returned within 30 days in their original board backing. Cut, trimmed or perforated stock is not returnable. Ink tins are returnable only if the seal is unbroken.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Anverra Pay, an authorised payment institution. Card numbers are never held on our systems: the authorisation happens in an Anverra Pay window and returns only an approval to us. Trade accounts settle by transfer on the 15th of the month.

Privacy

We keep order records for six years for tax purposes and share nothing with third parties beyond our carrier and our payment institution. The full privacy notice explains what we hold and why.
