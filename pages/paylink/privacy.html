Privacy notice

Shop / Privacy

Ollister & Crane Ltd is the controller of the information you give us when you order, open a trade account or write to the counter. This notice was last revised on 3 February 2026.

What we keep

Your name, delivery address, email and telephone number; what you ordered and when; and, for trade accounts, the business details and references you sent us. We never see or keep card numbers: card payments are confirmed in an Anverra Pay window and we receive only the approval.

Why, and for how long

To pack and send your order, to run your trade account, and to keep the records the tax authorities require. Order records are kept for six years from the end of the financial year they fall in. We do not send marketing unless you ask to hear about classes at the bench, and you can stop that at any time.

Who else sees it

Our carrier sees your delivery address, and Anverra Pay sees the amount and the order reference. Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we are not required to keep. Write to counter@ollistercrane.example or to the address below. You may also complain to the data protection regulator.
