Payer terms

The terms that apply when you approve a payment through an Anverra Pay window. Effective 20 January 2026.

Approving a payment

Approving authorises Anverra Pay to hold the amount shown on your saved card for the merchant named. The merchant captures the hold when it places the order; an uncaptured hold lapses after seven days.

Keeping approvals yours

Approve only payments you started, from the merchant page that opened the window. Never read a verification word to anyone, and never approve a payment someone else asks you to.

If something goes wrong

Tell the payer help desk within 60 days of a payment you did not authorise or that went wrong, quoting the processor reference. See payer help.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
