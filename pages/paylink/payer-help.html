Payer help

Answers for people asked to approve a payment through Anverra Pay.

What is this window?

A merchant that uses Anverra Pay never sees your card. When you choose to pay, the merchant opens this window and Anverra Pay takes the authorisation directly. When you approve, the merchant receives only the approval, never the card details.

Where is the verification word?

The merchant shows it on its own checkout page while this window is open. It exists so that only the person looking at the real checkout can approve the payment. It changes with every payment, so an old one will never work.

The word is refused

Check you are reading it from the merchant page that opened this window, not from an earlier attempt or another order. If the merchant page has been reloaded since this window opened, start the payment again from the checkout.

Was I charged?

Nothing is charged until you approve. An authorisation holds the amount shown; the merchant captures it when the order is placed. If you close this window without approving, the hold lapses and nothing is taken.

Disputes

Raise a dispute with the payer help desk within 60 days of the payment, quoting the processor reference from your approval and the order details from the merchant. Write to payers@anverrapay.example or call (646) 555-0114, Monday to Friday.

To finish a payment you were in the middle of, use your browser's back control to return to the authorisation, or start the payment again from the merchant's checkout page.
