More from the games desk

Lexvane shares its page with four other puzzles. All are set in-house at Calder Wharf, and all appear in both the print and digital editions. Lexvane is free; the other four are played online with either subscription.

Mini crossword

A pocket grid you can finish with your morning tea: general-knowledge clues, nothing obscure, and never more than a dozen entries. The setters aim for a two-minute solve; readers report anything from one minute to twenty.

Published daily Play with a subscription

Numberline

Arithmetic without algebra. Place the given digits along the line so every bracket balances; one arrangement works, and the desk promises it can be found without trial and error.

Published Monday to Friday Play with a subscription

Anagram ladder

Climb from the bottom rung to the top, rearranging as you go: each rung is clued, and each is built from the letters of the rung below plus one more. The ladder gets taller through the week.

Published daily Play with a subscription

Saturday cryptic

The desk's flagship: a full-size cryptic crossword from a rotating panel of setters, each with a signature style and a pseudonym readers argue about. The solution and setters' notes run the following Saturday.

Published Saturday Play with a subscription
