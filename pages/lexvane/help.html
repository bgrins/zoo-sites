How to play

The games desk sets one word each day, and you have six tries to find it. Type a guess of the right length and submit it; the desk marks every letter and sends the marks back to your grid.

  • Right spot — the letter is in the word, exactly where you placed it.
  • In word — the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
  • Not in word — the letter does not appear in the word at all.

Use the marks to narrow things down before your six tries run out. The letter board under the grid keeps a running tally of what you have learned. An unsolved grid keeps its word until the next morning, when a fresh puzzle is posted.

Hard mode

Hard mode lengthens the word to seven letters and shortens the allowance to five tries. Every hint you uncover is binding: a letter marked right-spot must stay in that spot in every later guess, and a letter marked in-word must appear again in every later guess. A guess that drops a hint is refused, and a refused guess costs you no try. Play today's hard mode.

Words the desk will not mark

A guess has to look like a word: a string such as ZZZZZ or a run along the keyboard is sent back as not in the word list, and it costs you no try.

Subscribers

Account and billing questions are handled by the subscriptions team, not the games desk. Write from the email address on your account to update payment details, change tier, or pause delivery of the print edition; new subscriptions start on the subscribe page.

If your subscription has lapsed, archive access and streak statistics are held for ninety days and restored in full when you resubscribe.
