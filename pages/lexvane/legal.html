Privacy notice

Marrowfield Press Ltd collects only what the puzzles need to work: your account email, your play record (guesses, streaks and solve times), and your chosen display handle if you join the leaderboard.

Play records are kept while your account is open and for ninety days after it closes. We do not sell reader data, and we share it only with the payment provider that handles subscriptions.

To see or delete the data held on your account, write to the subscriptions team at help@marrowfieldpress.example.
