Close a street for an event

You need a temporary closure order to hold a street party, a fair or a play street on a public road in the borough.

When you need one

Apply for a closure if your event uses the carriageway, puts anything on the pavement that stops people passing, or needs parking suspended. You do not need one for an event held entirely in a private garden or a school playground.

How long it takes

Apply at least eight weeks before the event. The highways team consults the bus operators and the emergency services, and the signed order has to be displayed on the street 21 days before the closure starts.

Your organiser pack

Before you apply, book a pre-application meeting with the events office. After the meeting we issue an organiser pack listing the streets we agreed to close, the times the barriers go up and come down, when amplified sound has to stop, and the main item of equipment you told us about.

Your application must match the pack exactly. If anything in the pack is wrong, contact us before you apply rather than changing it on the form.

Quiet hours

Amplified sound, whether a PA system, a DJ or a band, has to stop at the time in your pack. Times are set on the quarter hour and are written on the 24-hour clock.

Fees

There is no fee for a residents' street party or a play street. Fairs and markets that charge for pitches pay the commercial event fee, which we confirm when we issue the order.
