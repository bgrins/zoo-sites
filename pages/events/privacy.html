Privacy notice for event applications

Ivrelby Borough Council is the data controller for the information you give the events office.

What we collect

Your name and contact details as the organiser, the details of your event, and the streets and times of any closure you apply for.

Why we use it

We use it to decide your application, to make the closure order, and to tell the emergency services and bus operators about the closure. The order itself is a public notice and is displayed on the street.

How long we keep it

We keep application records for six years after the event, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by writing to the events office at the Town Hall.
