Terms of use

These terms cover the events office pages and the online application for a street closure.

Applications

Submitting an application does not give you permission to close a road. The closure is lawful only once the council has made and published the order.

Accuracy

You are responsible for the answers you give. An order made on wrong details may be withdrawn, and a new application is needed to change a submitted one.

Availability

We try to keep the online application available at all times, but it may be taken down for maintenance without notice.
