Plan an event in Ivrelby

Street parties, fairs, markets and filming on the borough's roads all need our permission before they go ahead. Talk to us early: most applications take six to eight weeks.

Close a street

Residents' parties, play streets and fairs that need a road closed to traffic.

Events in parks

Booked through the parks and open spaces team at the Town Hall.

Filming

Location permits for film, television and photography crews.

The events office counter at the Town Hall is closed on Wednesday afternoons for staff training. Telephone and email are answered as usual.

Apply for a street closure

Once we have met you and agreed the route, we send you an organiser pack. The pack sets out the streets, the times and the conditions we agreed, and your application has to match it.

News from the events office

Generators at street events

From this autumn every generator has to be declared by its equipment code, so the highways team can check the fuel and noise rating before the day. The codes are listed in the street closure guidance.

Quiet hours reminder

Amplified sound has to stop at the time set in your organiser pack, even if the road stays closed later for clearing up.
