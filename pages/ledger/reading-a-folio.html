A folio is one bound sheet of the ledger. The pager at the head and foot of each folio moves between them; the folio number in the top right corner tells you which one you are looking at.

The four columns

Date
The day the posting was settled, written as year, month, day. Several postings can share a date, and they then appear in the order the bookkeeper entered them, which carries no meaning of its own.
Description
What the money was spent on, in the bookkeeper's words. A description is not a code and is not guaranteed to be unique: the same wording recurs whenever the same expense recurs.
Tag
One of hardware, travel, software or misc. Every posting carries exactly one tag, chosen when the posting is entered and never left blank.
Amount
The sum posted, right aligned, with a thousands separator and two decimal places. Amounts are entered as posted and are not adjusted for anything.

What the tags mean

Hardware covers physical goods the trust keeps and uses: culvert sections, gate furniture, a battery bank, a weather station mast. Travel covers moving people and vehicles about, which in practice means fuel, mileage claims and parking. Software covers anything licensed or subscribed to, whether it is billed monthly or renewed once a year. Misc carries everything else, from filing fees and printing to first aid restocks and volunteer refreshments.

A tag is a bookkeeping convenience and not an accounting category. Do not read a tag as a line in the annual accounts, where the same spending is grouped differently.

Working across folios

Two things trip people up when they read the ledger as a whole rather than a folio at a time.

  1. The subtotal at the foot of a folio covers the postings on that folio and nothing else. It is not a running balance, it is not carried forward, and adding subtotals is not how the trust arrives at any published figure.
  2. Folios are filled in date order as postings are settled, so which folio a posting lands on follows from its settlement date and from nothing else. A posting is never moved to a neighboring folio to tidy a sheet.

Where you want a figure across the whole year, use the Export CSV control at the head of any folio rather than adding subtotals. The export covers every posting in the ledger, in the same four columns and the same order, and it is the only view the bookkeeper will reconcile against on request.

Asking about an entry

Quote the folio number and the date, and the office can find the filed document behind the posting. A description on its own is rarely enough, because the wording repeats through the year. Write to bookkeeping@trelowentrust.example, or telephone during weekday mornings.
