What this site records

The site keeps an ordinary server log of each request: the page asked for, the time, and the network address it came from. The log is used to keep the site running and to spot automated collection that is heavier than the access notes ask for. It is kept for ninety days and then deleted. The site sets one cookie, which keeps a CSV export tied to the visit that asked for it; it carries no name and follows you nowhere else.

What the folios hold about people

As a matter of practice the ledger names work, not people. Payees are kept out of descriptions, and volunteer mileage claims appear by route rather than by driver. The documents behind the postings stay in the office and are not published.

Donors and members

Donor and member records are not kept on this site and are never published. The trust does not sell, rent or trade names or addresses with other organizations.

Questions

Write to the bookkeeper at bookkeeping@trelowentrust.example or at the office address below, and the office will answer within ten business days.
