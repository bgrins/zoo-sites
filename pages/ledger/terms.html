Using the figures

You may read, copy and republish the published folios and the CSV export, on the three conditions set out under reusing the figures: attribute them to Trelowen Land Trust with the fiscal year, say whether the folios were marked unaudited when you took them, and do not present an extract or a figure of your own as the complete ledger.

Accuracy

Folios are published as they are completed and stay unaudited until the annual review is finished. The trust corrects errors by a fresh posting, never by editing a published folio, so a figure you took may later be joined by a correcting entry. The trust is not responsible for decisions made on unaudited figures.

Fair use of the site

Automated collection is welcome at a gentle rate. Take the CSV export once rather than fetching every folio repeatedly; the office may block traffic that slows the site for other readers.

Governing law
