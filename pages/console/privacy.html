Privacy

Updated 2026-01-15

What Cindergrid Systems Ltd collects about the people who use the console, and what happens to it.

What we hold

Account records (name, work email, organisation role), console access logs (who viewed or changed what, kept two years for audit), and the metadata of runs your organisation executes. We do not profile users or sell any of this to anyone.

Where it lives

All customer data is stored in the region your organisation selected (for gridworks-eu that is eu-west-2) and never leaves it except for encrypted backups within the same jurisdiction. Sub-processors are listed in the signed data processing agreement.

Your rights

You can request a copy or the deletion of your personal records by writing to privacy@cindergrid.example.net. Deletion requests complete within thirty days; audit log entries are pseudonymised rather than removed, as the law requires us to keep them. The supervisory contact is our data protection officer at the registered address below.
