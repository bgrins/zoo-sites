Service

Cindergrid provides build, scan, and deployment execution on shared runner infrastructure. Availability is targeted at 99.9% measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead on the status page. Service credits for missed targets are defined in the order form.

Your content

Source, images, artifacts and logs remain the customer's property. Cindergrid processes them solely to execute runs and retains them only for the published retention windows. Secrets are stored encrypted and are never written to logs by the platform; a pipeline that prints its own secrets does so at the customer's risk.

Acceptable use

Runner capacity may not be used for anything other than building, testing and deploying the customer's software. Cryptocurrency mining, load testing third parties, and attempts to access other tenants' workspaces are grounds for immediate suspension.

Termination

Either party may terminate at the end of the committed term with thirty days notice. On termination, artifacts and logs are available for export for fourteen days and then destroyed.
