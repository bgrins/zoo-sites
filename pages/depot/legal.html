Privacy and terms of use

Privacy notice

The operations console records operator sign-ins, yard moves, intake postings and incident reports against the operator identity signed in at the terminal. Records are kept for the periods set by the site retention schedule: operational logs for 13 months, incident records for 6 years.

Console records are used for shift planning, stock accuracy, carrier settlement and health and safety compliance. They are shared with carriers only where a settlement query requires it, and are not otherwise disclosed outside Marlowe Depot Systems Ltd except where the law requires.

Questions about records held against your operator identity go to the site data controller via the ops desk on 01632 960 210.

Terms of use

The console is provided for Marlowe Depot Systems staff and authorised contractors performing yard duties. Access is granted per operator; sharing a terminal PIN is a disciplinary matter.

Entries posted to the console are operational records. Deliberately posting false intake, manifest or incident records is treated as gross misconduct under the site handbook.

Availability is not guaranteed during maintenance windows published on the yard notices board.
