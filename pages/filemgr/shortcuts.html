Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work on any folder page, except while you are typing in a box.

/Focus the search box
EscClear the search, or cancel a rename in progress
EnterSave a rename in progress
Up / DownMove the selection between rows
G then WGo to Working files
G then RGo to Recent

Shortcuts cannot be remapped on the Team plan.
