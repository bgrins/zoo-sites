Privacy notice

Effective 10 January 2026. Applies to the Boxelder workspace service.

What we hold

Files belong to the workspace that stores them. Boxelder Software Ltd holds account records (name, email, workspace membership and role) and service logs (sign-ins, file operations) needed to run the service and meet our contract with the workspace owner.

Access to file contents

Boxelder staff do not read file contents except with the workspace owner's written consent on a support case that requires it.

Retention

Service logs are kept for 12 months. Deleted files are recoverable from Trash for 30 days and then removed from our systems within a further 14 days.
