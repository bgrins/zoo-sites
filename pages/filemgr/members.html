Roles on this workspace

  • Owner - manages members, roles, the plan and storage.
  • Editor - renames and edits files. The owner can restrict uploads and new folders for an editor; that restriction is why those controls are greyed out for you.
  • Commenter - opens files and leaves comments; cannot change anything.
  • Viewer - opens and downloads files only.

Changing a role

Ask the owner. Role changes take effect the next time the member loads a page. See plan and billing for the seat allowance.
