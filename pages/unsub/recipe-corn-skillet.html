Brown Butter Corn Skillet with Torn Basil

From the Tealwave test kitchen · Serves 4 as a side · 20 minutes

The trick here is patience in the pan. Corn that is stirred constantly steams; corn that is left alone long enough browns, and browned corn tastes of caramel and toast. Use the best corn you can find, cut from the cob the same day.

Ingredients

  • 4 ears sweet corn, kernels cut from the cob
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon flaky salt, plus more to finish
  • A squeeze of lime
  • A handful of basil leaves, torn

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a heavy 10-inch skillet over medium heat and keep cooking, swirling now and then, until the solids turn hazelnut brown and smell nutty, about 4 minutes.
  2. Add the shallot and cook for 1 minute. Tip in the corn, spread it into an even layer, and leave it alone for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir once, spread it out again, and leave it another 3 minutes, until plenty of kernels are spotted dark gold.
  4. Take the pan off the heat. Season with the salt and lime, scatter the basil over, and serve from the skillet.

Leftovers make a good cold salad the next day with a few halved cherry tomatoes.
