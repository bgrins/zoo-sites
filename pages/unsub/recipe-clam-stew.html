Weeknight Clam Stew for Two

From the Tealwave test kitchen · Serves 2 · 35 minutes

Littlenecks from the Bellhaven flats are small and sweet, and they open in minutes. Scrub them well and discard any that stay open when tapped. The cider keeps the broth bright; a dry one works best.

Ingredients

  • 2 dozen littleneck clams, scrubbed
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 leek, white and pale green parts, sliced
  • 2 small waxy potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch dice
  • 1 cup dry hard cider
  • 1 cup water or light stock
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • Chopped parsley and black pepper, to finish

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a wide pot over medium heat. Add the leek and cook until soft but not browned, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes, cider and water. Simmer, covered, until the potatoes are just tender, 10 to 12 minutes.
  3. Add the clams, cover, and raise the heat to medium-high. Cook, shaking the pot now and then, until the shells open, 5 to 7 minutes. Discard any that stay shut.
  4. Stir in the cream, heat through, and finish with parsley and pepper. Taste before salting; the clams bring their own.

Serve in wide bowls with bread for the broth.
