Policies · Privacy

Privacy at Tealwave

Tealwave is reader-funded, so we have no advertisers to share your data with and no incentive to collect more than we need.

What we hold

Your name, email address, membership plan and billing history; the recipes you save; and delivery statistics for the letters we send you. Payment card details are held by our payment processor, never on our servers.

What we do with it

We use this data to deliver the letters and benefits you signed up for, to answer support requests, and to understand in aggregate which stories readers finish. We do not sell reader data, rent lists, or embed third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold, or its deletion, by writing to support@tealwave.example. Requests are answered within thirty days. Deleting an account also removes the saved recipe box and reading history, which cannot be restored afterwards.

This policy was last revised in March 2026. Material changes are announced in the Thursday letter before they take effect.
