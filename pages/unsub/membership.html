Membership

Reader-funded, cover to cover

Tealwave carries no advertising. Members fund every story, every tested recipe and every ferry ticket our contributors buy. In return, membership includes:

  • The Thursday letter, plus the Sunday recipe archive
  • Member pricing on every workshop and pantry box
  • Your saved recipe box and reading history
  • First access to seasonal supper club tickets
  • The full story archive, back to issue one

Plans

The Weekly plan is 6 a month or 60 a year, in your local currency, and includes everything above. The Patron plan, at 120 a year, adds a printed year-end anthology and a guest seat at one supper club.

Joining

New memberships open on the first Thursday of each month. Write to support@tealwave.example with the subject line "Membership" and the desk will send an invitation for the next opening. Gift memberships are arranged the same way, via the contact page.

Workshops and supper clubs are ticketed separately. Member pricing is applied automatically at checkout.
