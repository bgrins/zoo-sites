Tomato Toast, Three Ways

From the Tealwave test kitchen · Each serves 2 · 10 to 90 minutes

A ripe tomato and good bread need very little help. These three versions run from ten minutes to an hour and a half, and every one of them starts with thick slices of country bread toasted hard in a dry pan or under the broiler.

Grated

Halve two large tomatoes and grate the cut sides on the coarse holes of a box grater, stopping at the skin. Rub a cut garlic clove over two slices of hot toast, spoon the tomato pulp over, and finish with olive oil and flaky salt. Ten minutes, start to finish.

Marinated

Slice three medium tomatoes thickly and lay them in a shallow dish with a sliced shallot, a spoonful of red wine vinegar, two tablespoons of olive oil and a good pinch of salt. Leave them for thirty minutes, turning once. Pile the slices on toast and spoon the juices over; they are the best part.

Slow-roasted

Halve a pint of cherry tomatoes and roast them cut side up on a sheet pan with olive oil, salt and a few thyme sprigs at 275°F for about ninety minutes, until they slump and wrinkle. Spread the toast with ricotta and heap the tomatoes on top while they are still warm.

All three are better eaten standing up at the counter, over the plate.
