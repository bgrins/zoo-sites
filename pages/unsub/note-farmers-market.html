Bellhaven Farmers Market, 6:04 a.m.

Filed by Dario Welk · Saturday, September 12, 2026

The market opens at six. By 6:04 there is a line eleven deep at the Tolliver Farm stand, and it is not for coffee. The coffee cart does not open until 6:30. The line is for peaches.

The early crowd has a method. They come with their own bags, cash in small bills, and a clear idea of what they want. Nobody browses before seven. The regulars nod to each other and to the growers, and several of them, we noticed, are other growers, buying what they did not plant this year.

By 6:40 the stone fruit is gone: peaches, then the last of the plums, then a crate of nectarines the Tollivers had not planned to bring. The second wave, arriving for coffee and pastries, finds a hand-lettered sign: sold out, see you next week.

Asked for advice, one regular offered three rules. Arrive before the coffee. Know which stand you are going to first. Do not stop to talk until you have what you came for.
