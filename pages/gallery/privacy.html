Privacy notice

Northmarsh Outfitters Ltd, Quay Lane, Northmarsh NM1 4QL, is the controller of the personal information you give us. Last revised March 2026.

What we hold

When you reserve an order: the name and telephone number you give, the depot you chose, and what you ordered. When you bring kit in for repair: the item, the fault and a contact number. When you write to us: your address or email, the letter, and our reply.

Why we hold it

To pick and hold your order, to tell you when it is ready or when a repair is done, to handle returns and warranty claims, and to keep the records the law requires for sales.

How long

Order and repair records are kept for six years for tax and warranty purposes. Enquiries that do not become an order are deleted after two years.

Who else sees it

The carrier, when you ask for courier delivery, sees your name, address and telephone number. Our card processor sees your payment details at the counter. Nobody else, and we do not send marketing unless you ask for the seasonal catalogue.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion where we are not obliged to keep it, by writing to hello@northmarsh.example or to the Quay Lane address. We answer within one month.
