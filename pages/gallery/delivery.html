Delivery and returns

Collection is free from any depot. Courier rates are quoted at the counter.

Depot collection

Free from all four depots, ready the next working day at Quay Lane and Thrandle Vale, within two at Duncrieff, and on its next opening day at the seasonal Wrenfold Mill. Bring your order number to the collection counter. We hold a collection for fourteen days and then put the kit back into depot stock, so tell us if you are delayed and we will keep it aside.

Courier delivery

Two to four working days across the mainland, longer to the islands and the far north. The autumn rate card is still being settled with our carrier, so courier charges are quoted at the counter or by telephone rather than shown against the basket. Shelters and long poles travel as an oversize consignment and are booked separately.

Order cut-off

Orders placed before four in the afternoon are picked the same day. After that they join the next morning's run. Depot-marked and clearance lines are collection only and never travel by courier, because most are one-offs and a lost consignment cannot be replaced.

Sixty-day returns

Return anything within sixty days for a refund or an exchange, worn or unworn, so long as it comes back clean. Take it to any depot counter with your order number, or post it to the Quay Lane workshop. Refunds go back to the original payment within five working days of the kit reaching us, and we refund the outward courier charge if the return is our error.

Faults and warranty

Manufacturing faults are put right for the working life of the item, whether that is three seasons or twenty. Send us the item and a line about how it failed. Where a repair is the better answer we will say so, and where a pattern has been discontinued we will offer the nearest current equivalent rather than a credit note.

What we cannot take back

Base layers and socks that have been worn, gas canisters, and any item returned with mud, sap or fish on it. Wash it first and we will look at it. Filters and water treatment come back only unopened, for the obvious reason.

Questions about a consignment: ring the Quay Lane depot on 01632 960477 or write to orders@northmarsh.example.
