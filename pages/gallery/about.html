About Northmarsh

A chandlery, then a walking shop, now four depots and a workshop.

  • Where we started

    Northmarsh Outfitters opened in 1974 in a former chandlery on Quay Lane, selling oilskins to the estuary boats and, before long, to the walkers who came through the town on the coast path. The wader boot was the first thing we had made to our own pattern, because nothing on sale locally stood up to a season in the salt. Quay Lane is still the head office, still the workshop, and still the depot where the awkward jobs land.

  • How we choose kit

    Nothing goes into the catalogue until the counter staff have used it. Shelters are pitched on the gravel behind the Askern Vale yard and left out through a winter; packs go on the hill loaded with plates; stoves are run in wind until we can say honestly whether they hold a simmer. Where a sample fails, we tell the maker what failed and how, and we do not list it in the meantime. Some things have taken three seasons to reach the shelf.

  • Repair, then replace

    The workshop was set up in 1989 to re-proof shells and re-cord tent poles, and it now turns round most jobs in ten working days. We would rather sell one pack that lasts fifteen years than three that last five, which is why the repairs counter is at the front of every depot and not behind the stockroom.

  • The depots

    Quay Lane, Askern Vale, Duncrieff and the seasonal mill shop at Wrenfold. Each keeps its own stock rather than drawing from a single warehouse, so what is available depends on where you are standing. The counter can see the whole network and will move an item between depots for collection at no charge.

  • Ownership

    Northmarsh Outfitters Ltd is owned by the people who work here, through an employee trust that took over from the founding family in 2011. Company number 00000000, registered at Quay Lane, Northmarsh. Nobody outside the trust has a claim on the profits, which is the main reason the repairs workshop still exists.

Our kit is used by mountain rescue teams and outdoor centres across the region. We do not pay for endorsements and never have.
