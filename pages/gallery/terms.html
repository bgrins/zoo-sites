Terms of sale

These terms apply to orders reserved online or placed at any Northmarsh depot counter.

Prices

Prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT at the standard rate. Stock is held at each depot separately, so an item shown in stock can sell out at the counter before your reservation reaches it; if that happens we will call you before your collection date.

Reservations and payment

A reservation is not a sale. We pick the order, hold it for fourteen days at the depot you chose, and take payment when you collect. An order not collected in that time goes back into depot stock.

Returns

Return anything within sixty days for a refund or exchange, worn or unworn, provided it comes back clean. Your statutory rights are not affected. Full details are on the delivery and returns page.

Faults

We put right manufacturing faults for the working life of the item, by repair, replacement or refund. Wear and tear, and damage from misuse, are repaired at the workshop rates on the repairs page.

Clearance lines

Depot-marked and end-of-line items are sold as seen, with the same sixty-day returns and full warranty as full-price stock, but are collection only.
