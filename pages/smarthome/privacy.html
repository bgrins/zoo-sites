Privacy notice

This notice covers the Hearthline Hub console, the relay service behind it and the devices paired with the hub. It was last revised in March 2026.

Who we are

Hearthline Systems Ltd, Unit 3, Kilnworth Park, Pellowdean PW3 6HD, is the controller for the relay service and your account. What stays on the hub in your home is yours, and we never see it unless you send it to us.

What the hub stores

Device states, schedules, scene definitions and calibration records are stored on the hub itself. Energy readings are kept for thirteen months to support year-on-year comparison. Camera footage never leaves the hub unless you export a clip yourself.

What leaves your home, and why

The relay carries push notifications and remote access when you switch them on, because you asked for those features; it keeps delivery logs for thirty days. Half-hourly meter totals go to your energy supplier where your tariff requires settlement data, because the tariff contract needs them; per-device breakdowns never do. Your account record (name, email, hub serial) is kept while the account is open and for twelve months after, to handle warranty and billing questions.

Your choices and rights

You can switch off remote access, notifications or supplier sharing in hub settings without losing local control. You can ask for a copy of what the relay holds about you, ask us to correct or erase it, or object to a use of it: write to privacy@hearthline.example and we answer within one month. If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the data protection regulator.
