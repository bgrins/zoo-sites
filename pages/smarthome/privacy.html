Privacy notice

This notice covers the Hearthline Hub console and the devices paired with it. It was last revised in March 2026.

What the hub stores

Device states, schedules, scene definitions and calibration records are stored on the hub itself. Energy readings are kept for thirteen months to support year-on-year comparison. Camera footage never leaves the hub unless you export a clip yourself.

What leaves your home

The console talks to Hearthline's relay only to deliver push notifications and remote access you have switched on. Half-hourly meter totals are shared with your energy supplier where your tariff requires settlement data; per-device breakdowns are not.

Your choices

You can disable remote access, notifications or supplier sharing from the hub without losing local control. Write to privacy@hearthline.example for a copy of the data held by the relay service, or to have it erased.
