Media assets

Assets in the media kit are supplied for editorial use in coverage of Northwind Industrial Group. They may not be used to imply endorsement or in any commercial context.

What is in the kit

  • Group wordmark in print and screen formats, with usage guidance
  • Photography of the executive committee and board, captioned and cleared for editorial use
  • Site photography for the group's principal manufacturing locations
  • A one-page group fact sheet, updated at each results announcement

The kit is issued by the press office to accredited media. Requests are normally turned around within one working day; please state your publication and the piece you are working on. High-resolution files are supplied by transfer link.

Requesting assets

Write to press@northwind-group.example or call +44 20 7946 0180 between 08:30 and 18:00 GMT. Interview requests are handled in the order received and are subject to availability on the day.
