Newsroom

Subscribe to alerts

Receive group announcements by email on the day they are released, including results, board changes and calendar updates.

How to subscribe

Subscriptions are managed by the press office. To join the distribution list, send an email to alerts@northwind-group.example with the subject line "Subscribe" from the address at which you wish to receive alerts. You will receive a confirmation message within one working day.

To change your address or unsubscribe, write to the same address with the subject line "Unsubscribe". Requests are actioned within one working day.

Your address is used only to send the alerts you have requested and is handled as described in the privacy notice.

What you will receive

  • Press releases, at the time they are published to the newsroom
  • Regulatory filings notices, with a link to the definitive text
  • Financial calendar changes, when dates are confirmed or moved
