Privacy notice

This notice explains how Northwind Industrial Group plc handles personal information collected through the newsroom and investor relations site. It was last revised in March 2026.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you provide it to us: when you subscribe to email alerts, contact the press office or investor relations team, or request documents. This is typically your name, email address, organisation and the substance of your enquiry.

The site sets a session identifier necessary for it to function. It is not used to build a profile of you and expires at the end of your visit.

How we use it

Information you provide is used to respond to your enquiry or to supply the service you have requested, such as email alerts. We do not sell personal information, and we share it only with service providers who process it on our instructions, including our email distribution provider and our registrar.

Alert subscriptions are kept while your subscription is active and removed within one month of an unsubscribe request. Enquiry correspondence is retained for up to two years.

Your rights and how to reach us

You may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the data protection contact at privacy@northwind-group.example or to the registered office. If you are dissatisfied with our response you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
