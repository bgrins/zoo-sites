Group announcement

Sale of the Ellersby coatings site completes

London, 28 May 2026, 07:00 BST

Category Disposal Distribution Wire and accredited media Release 26-114

Northwind Industrial Group plc announces that the sale of its Ellersby coatings manufacturing site to Harwick Specialty Materials Limited, first announced on 12 February 2026, has completed following the satisfaction of all closing conditions.

Gross cash consideration of 46 million pounds was received at completion. Net proceeds, after transaction costs and separation expenses, will be applied to the reduction of group borrowings in line with the framework set out at the full-year results.

The Ellersby site produced protective coatings for the marine and offshore markets and employed around 240 people, all of whom transfer with the business. Northwind will continue to supply certain intermediate materials to the site under a three-year agreement entered into at completion.

Marianne Holt, Chief Executive, said: "Ellersby is a good business, but one that sits outside the process industries where Northwind competes best. This sale sharpens the portfolio and strengthens the balance sheet, and we wish the team every success under their new ownership."
