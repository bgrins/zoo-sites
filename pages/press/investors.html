Investor relations

Northwind Industrial Group plc is listed on the main market under the ticker NWG. This section brings together the information most often requested by shareholders and analysts.

The group supplies engineered components, protective surface technologies and industrial services to customers in the process industries, reporting in sterling across three divisions. Its strategy and capital allocation framework are set out in the annual report and were most recently updated in the June 2026 capital allocation review.
