Shareholder services

The group's share register is maintained by Pravenly Registrars Limited. The registrar is the first point of contact for all holding enquiries.

Registrar

Pravenly Registrars Limited, Quillrow Court, Tessary Lane, Salford M71 4QX. Shareholder helpline +44 161 496 0320, open 09:00 to 17:00 on business days, or holders@pravenly-registrars.example.

The registrar can help with changes of address, lost share certificates, dividend mandates and payment instructions, and transfers of holdings. Please quote your shareholder reference number, which appears on your share certificate and dividend confirmations.

Dividends

The interim dividend of 8.4 pence per ordinary share will be paid on 11 September 2026 to shareholders on the register at 7 August 2026. Shareholders are encouraged to have dividends paid directly into a bank account; mandate forms are available from the registrar.

Dividend dates for the year are listed on the financial calendar.

Beware of unsolicited approaches

Shareholders are occasionally approached by parties offering to buy shares at inflated prices or charging for services that are free of charge. The group never makes unsolicited calls to shareholders. If you receive such an approach, take no action and report it to the registrar.
