Group announcement

Half-year results for the period to 30 June

London, 14 July 2026, 07:00 BST

Category Results Distribution Wire and accredited media Release 26-117

Northwind Industrial Group plc today announces its results for the six months ended 30 June 2026, reporting revenue of £812 million, up 4 per cent on the prior period, and adjusted operating profit of £96 million.

Order intake across the group's flow control and industrial services divisions remained firm through the half, with the book-to-bill ratio finishing the period at 1.06. The surface technologies division returned to growth in the second quarter following the completion of its site consolidation programme.

Net debt at the period end was £214 million, equivalent to 1.1 times adjusted earnings, after the receipt of proceeds from the disposal of the Ellersby coatings site in May. The board has declared an interim dividend of 8.4 pence per ordinary share, payable on 11 September 2026 to shareholders on the register at 7 August 2026.

Marianne Holt, Chief Executive, said: "The group has delivered a disciplined first half. Demand in our core process industries held up well, our margin programme is running ahead of plan, and the balance sheet gives us room to keep investing where returns are strongest."

The group's outlook for the full year is unchanged. A presentation for analysts and investors was held at 09:30 BST on the day of this announcement; the accompanying materials are available from the investor relations team.

Notes to editors

Northwind Industrial Group reports in sterling. Statements that refer to future performance are subject to risks described in the group's most recent annual report and should not be read as guidance.
