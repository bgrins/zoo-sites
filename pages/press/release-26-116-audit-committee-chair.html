Board appoints new chair of the audit committee

London, 2 July 2026, 07:00 BST

Category Board changes Distribution Wire and accredited media Release 26-116

The board of Northwind Industrial Group plc announces that Priya Nandra, independent non-executive director, has been appointed chair of the audit committee with effect from 1 August 2026.

Ms Nandra joined the board in September 2024 and has served on the audit committee since her appointment. She is a chartered accountant with more than two decades of senior finance experience in the engineering and materials sectors, including nine years as a divisional finance director of a listed industrial group.

She succeeds Colin Braithwaite, who will step down from the board at the conclusion of his third three-year term, in line with the board's succession plan. Mr Braithwaite has chaired the committee since 2019.

Alistair Renshaw, Chair, said: "Priya brings deep financial and sector experience to the role, and the committee will be in excellent hands. On behalf of the board I thank Colin for seven years of rigorous and thoughtful service as chair of the committee."

There are no further matters requiring disclosure in connection with this appointment under the listing rules.
