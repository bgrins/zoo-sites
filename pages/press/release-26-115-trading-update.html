Group announcement

Trading update and capital allocation review

London, 19 June 2026, 07:00 BST

Category Trading update Distribution Wire and accredited media Release 26-115

Northwind Industrial Group plc provides the following update on trading for the period from 1 January 2026 to date, ahead of its half-year results on 14 July, and announces the conclusions of the capital allocation review begun in January.

Group trading has been in line with the board's expectations. Aftermarket demand in flow control remains the strongest part of the portfolio, and the surface technologies order book has stabilised following two soft quarters. Pricing has held, and input cost inflation continues to ease.

The capital allocation review has confirmed the group's priorities: organic investment in the core divisions, a progressive ordinary dividend, and bolt-on acquisitions where returns clear the group's hurdle rate. After these commitments, the board intends to return surplus capital to shareholders and has approved a share buyback programme of up to 60 million pounds, to commence following the half-year results.

David Okafor, Chief Financial Officer, said: "The review gives us a clear framework. With disposals completed and leverage comfortably inside our target range, we can fund growth and still return surplus capital with discipline."

Notes to editors

Northwind Industrial Group reports in sterling. Statements that refer to future performance are subject to risks described in the group's most recent annual report and should not be read as guidance.
