Modern slavery statement

This statement is made under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 for the financial year ended 31 December 2025. It was approved by the board on 26 March 2026.

Our business and supply chain

Northwind Industrial Group supplies engineered components, surface technologies and industrial services from manufacturing sites in the United Kingdom and continental Europe. The group buys metals, chemical intermediates, fabricated parts and site services from around 1,900 direct suppliers.

Steps taken in the year

  • Screened all new direct suppliers against the group's responsible sourcing standard before first order
  • Completed on-site audits at 34 suppliers in higher-risk categories, with corrective action plans agreed where findings were raised
  • Refreshed mandatory training for procurement and site management staff, with completion of 97 per cent
  • Maintained a confidential reporting line, available to employees and to workers in the supply chain, with no modern slavery reports received in the year

Commitments for the current year

The group will extend audit coverage to logistics and labour providers, and will include modern slavery indicators in the pre-qualification of all tenders above 250,000 pounds. Progress is reviewed by the board annually and reported in this statement.

Printed copies of this and prior-year statements are available from the company secretariat at the registered office.

Reporting concerns

Concerns can be raised in confidence at speakup@northwind-group.example. Reports may be made anonymously.
