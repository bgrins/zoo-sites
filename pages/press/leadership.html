Leadership

The board of Northwind Industrial Group plc and its executive leadership. Committee memberships are listed on the governance page.

Alistair Renshaw

Chair

Appointed to the board in 2020 and chair since 2022. Previously chief executive of a listed engineering services group, with earlier senior roles in power generation and rail infrastructure.

Marianne Holt

Chief Executive

Joined Northwind in 2015 as managing director of the flow control division and appointed chief executive in 2021. A chartered engineer, she began her career in process plant design.

David Okafor

Chief Financial Officer

Appointed in 2023 after twelve years in senior finance roles across the speciality chemicals sector, latterly as group financial controller of a FTSE-listed materials company.

Priya Nandra

Independent Non-executive Director; Chair of the Audit Committee from 1 August 2026

A chartered accountant with more than two decades of senior finance experience in the engineering and materials sectors. Joined the board in September 2024.

Colin Braithwaite

Independent Non-executive Director; Chair of the Audit Committee to 31 July 2026

Joined the board in 2017 and has chaired the audit committee since 2019. He steps down at the conclusion of his third three-year term.

Ruth Calloway

Company Secretary

Responsible for board support, listing obligations and the group's subsidiary governance framework. Joined Northwind in 2018.
