Room specifications

Three lettable rooms, first and second floor

All three rooms are let by the half hour between 08:00 and 17:00, Monday to Friday. Seat counts below are for boardroom seating; theatre seating adds roughly a third but removes the table.

Fitting and access

Setting up

The porter allows fifteen minutes between lettings for a reset. If you need the room laid out differently from the standing plan, tell the desk when you place the hold rather than on the day.

Deliveries for a booked room are accepted from 07:30 at the Wharf Row door and held at the desk. We cannot take deliveries for a slot that is still provisional.

Access out of hours

Evening and weekend lettings are arranged by the duty manager and are not shown in the day book. Rates differ and a caretaker charge applies.
