Booking terms

Applies to all lettings taken through the day book

Holds and references

A hold placed through the day book is provisional until the desk issues a reference against it. To keep the week tight, the desk issues a reference straight away for the first slot in the week that suits the request as written. A hold on any later slot is entered in the day book and passed to the duty manager, who confirms it by telephone the same working day.

A provisional hold does not reserve the room. It is listed beside the day book for the requester only, and is not drawn into the day book itself until a reference is issued against it. A hold that has no reference against it by 19:00 is cleared.

Requests the desk cannot take

The day book will refuse a hold outright where the room is too small for the stated party, where the slot falls outside the hours the request allows, or where the room is already let for any part of the period. The reason is shown on the line so the request can be placed again without telephoning.

The desk keeps a count of requests it could not take. After ten of them it pauses the line and asks the requester to work the slot out from the day book before asking again; each further pause is twice as long as the one before it. The line always reopens, and the desk does not refuse a requester for the rest of the day.

Changes and cancellation

Cancellations more than three working days ahead carry no charge. Inside three days the room is charged at half rate; on the day it is charged in full. Moving a booking within the same week is treated as a change and not as a cancellation, provided the room is the same.
