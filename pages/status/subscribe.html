Other ways to follow

The incident feed (Atom) carries each record from the incident history once it is resolved, and is the lightest way to watch it from a wiki or a chat integration. Teams on the Ridge plan can point a webhook at their own endpoint instead; the payload names the component, the state it moved to, the incident reference, and the update text, and it is retried for six hours before we give up on an endpoint.

Subscriptions cover platform state only. Notices that affect one account, such as an approaching quota or an expiring certificate, are sent to the technical contacts on that account and cannot be redirected here.

What we do with the address

An address on this list is used to send status notifications and nothing else. We do not add it to product announcements, we do not pass it to anyone outside Nimbrel Networks, and we delete it within thirty days of an unsubscribe. Asking to be removed does not require an account, and a request to noc@nimbrel.example is honored the same working day.

Status definitions sets out what each state means, and About this page explains how a state is decided.
