What this page is

This page is published as a courtesy so customers can see the state of the platform without opening a ticket. It is updated by the network operations center as described on About this page. Its figures are not the measure service credits are calculated from; the status definitions explain the difference.

Using the relay path check and the feed

You may run the relay path check and read the incident feed from your own monitoring, at a reasonable rate. You may quote incident records, with their reference, in your own reports to your customers.
