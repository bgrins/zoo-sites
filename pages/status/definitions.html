Status definitions

The vocabulary this page uses, and the service commitments each term is measured against. Where a customer agreement says something different, the agreement governs.

Component states

StateWhat it means
Operational The component is meeting its service objective from every vantage point we probe from. Individual requests can still fail; a component is not scored on single requests.
Degraded The component is answering, but slower than its objective or with a share of errors above the threshold set out below. Work is in progress and no customer action is required.
Congested Capacity on a path is oversubscribed and requests are queueing behind it. A congested path usually clears when spill capacity comes online, which is why this state is reported on a route rather than on the platform.

An incident record carries its own state, which tracks the response rather than the component: Investigating, Identified, Monitoring, then Resolved. A record reaches Monitoring when the fix is in place and we are still watching, and Resolved only after a full measurement window has passed clean.

The uptime figures beside each component on the status page are a trailing 90-day view and are not the measure a service credit is calculated from. Credits are calculated per calendar month, per component, on the account's own traffic.

Exclusions

Announced maintenance is excluded from the measurement when it was announced at least five days ahead and kept inside its published window. So is unavailability caused by something outside our control: a fault in your own network or a transit provider you chose, traffic beyond a contracted rate limit, a request rejected because a credential expired, or an outage caused by software you deployed to the edge yourself.

Beta components are excluded until they are announced as generally available. A beta component is labelled as such in the control panel and does not appear on this page.

Claiming a credit

A credit is not applied automatically. Send a claim to support@nimbrel.example within thirty days of the end of the month it relates to, naming the account, the component, the dates, and the incident reference from the history page if there is one. Where the claim concerns a route rather than a component, a probe code from a relay path check taken during the window will let us find the measurements without asking you to reproduce anything.

We answer a claim within ten business days. A credit is applied against future invoices and is not exchanged for a refund.
