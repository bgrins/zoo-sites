Privacy on this page

What the status page records about a visit, and what happens to a subscription address. The Nimbrel Networks customer agreement covers everything else.

Visits and relay path checks

The status page sets one cookie to keep the Recent checks list together for your visit. A relay path check records the network address it ran from, the relay it reached and the probe code it was given. We keep those records for seven days so that support can find a check you quote, and then delete them.

Subscription addresses

An address on the notification list is used to send status notifications and nothing else. It is not added to product announcements or passed to anyone outside Nimbrel Networks, and it is deleted within thirty days of an unsubscribe.

To see or remove what we hold, write to noc@nimbrel.example.
