Incident history / NE-4B9E1

Relay mesh congestion in the Aparra region

NE-4B9E1 2026-07-03 19:41 to 21:05 Resolved

Affected component: Relay mesh.

Updates

  1. Resolved2026-07-03 21:05

    Spill capacity has absorbed the load and paths in the Aparra region have measured clean for a full window. Relay path checks now report the region as operational.

  2. Monitoring2026-07-03 20:22

    Spill capacity is online and queueing on the two relays is falling. Checks from the region may still report congested paths for a short while.

  3. Identified2026-07-03 19:58

    A fiber cut in the Aparra region has moved traffic onto two relays, which are now oversubscribed. We are bringing spill capacity online.

  4. Investigating2026-07-03 19:41

    Relay path checks from the Aparra region are reporting congested paths. We are investigating.

Postmortem

A civil works crew cut a regional fiber route at 19:36. The four relays serving the region were designed to lose one route, but this cut took the routes feeding two of them, so their traffic moved onto the remaining two.

Spill capacity in the neighboring region is held for exactly this case, but bringing it online is a manual step, and it took 41 minutes to complete. Paths from the region were congested for most of that time.

Spill capacity for the region is now brought online automatically when two relays are lost at once, and the step has been added to the quarterly failover exercise.
