Incident history / NE-C214A

Elevated relay latency on trans-Atlantic routes

NE-C214A 2026-07-21 09:14 to 11:47 Resolved

Affected component: Relay mesh.

Updates

  1. Resolved2026-07-21 11:47

    The carrier has restored the segment and latency on trans-Atlantic routes has been at its normal level for a full measurement window. Routes are back on their usual paths.

  2. Monitoring2026-07-21 10:30

    Affected routes are steered onto the Vireo ring and latency is back inside budget. We are waiting for the carrier to restore the segment before moving traffic back.

  3. Identified2026-07-21 09:52

    A fault on a carrier segment serving the Halden relay is adding 80 to 120 ms. We are steering traffic onto the Vireo ring.

  4. Investigating2026-07-21 09:14

    Relay path checks on trans-Atlantic routes are reporting higher latency than usual. We are investigating.

Postmortem

A third-party carrier lost one of two fiber pairs on the segment that carries trans-Atlantic traffic into the Halden relay. Traffic stayed up on the second pair, but on a longer path, which added 80 to 120 ms for about two and a half hours.

Our probes saw the latency within four minutes, but the automatic reroute waits for packet loss rather than latency, so traffic stayed on the degraded path until an engineer steered it by hand 38 minutes later.

We have changed the reroute policy to act on a sustained latency breach as well as on loss, and we now take a second carrier into the Halden relay so a single segment fault no longer leaves it on one path.
