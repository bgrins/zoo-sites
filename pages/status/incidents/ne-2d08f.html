Incident history / NE-2D08F

Log streaming delivering with delay

NE-2D08F ongoing Monitoring

Affected component: Log streaming.

Updates

  1. Monitoring 

    Ingest is running on standby capacity and the backlog is draining. Delivery continues with a delay of up to four minutes. No records have been lost, and records already delayed will arrive in order.

  2. Identified 

    An ingest node in EU-West lost a storage volume and the remaining nodes are over their share. We are moving ingest for the point of presence onto standby capacity.

  3. Investigating 

    Log streaming deliveries from the EU-West point of presence are arriving late. We are investigating.
