Ticket terms

These terms apply to every ticket sold by a Verlan Transit kiosk. They sit alongside the Authority's conditions of travel, which are posted at every staffed office and on the notice frame beside each gate line.

Buying at a kiosk

  • A kiosk sells one ticket per purchase and loads it to the card you pay with. The card is the ticket: show it to an inspector on request.
  • The fare is the one quoted on the review screen at the moment you confirm. It includes the time-of-travel adjustment in effect then, and it is the amount charged.
  • A purchase is complete when the receipt screen shows a confirmation code. If the screen shows no code, check with the help point before buying again: a charge made without a code is reversed once the kiosk log confirms it.

Validity

  • A single is valid for one journey within the zones bought, starting within ninety minutes of purchase, with free changes at interchanges.
  • A day pass is valid for unlimited journeys within the zones bought until 04:30 the following morning.
  • Reduced tickets are valid only with a current Verlan concession card, which must be carried and shown on request.

Refunds and faults

Unused tickets are refundable for 28 days at any staffed office, to the card used to pay, on quoting the confirmation code. If a kiosk charges your card without showing a code, report it at the help point quoting the kiosk number shown in the header and the time; the charge is reversed once the kiosk log confirms it.

Penalty fares

Travelling beyond the zones on your ticket, or without a valid ticket, makes you liable to a penalty fare of £25.00 or twice the full single fare to your destination, whichever is greater.
