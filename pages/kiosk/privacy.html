Privacy at the kiosk

The Verlan Transit Authority, 55 Corridor House, Verlan Central VN2 4EJ, is responsible for the records described here.

What a kiosk records

For each purchase the kiosk records the ticket and zones sold, the fare charged, the time, the kiosk number, the confirmation code and the last four digits of the card. The card number itself goes to the payment processor and is never stored by the kiosk. The kiosk has no camera and asks for no name.

Why, and for how long

  • Purchase records: kept for two years, to handle refunds, inspections and disputed charges.
  • Journey taps on the card: kept for eight weeks, then reduced to anonymous counts used to plan services.
  • Fault logs from the kiosk: kept for ninety days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the purchase records held against a card by writing to the Data Protection Officer at the address above, or at tickets@verlantransit.example, quoting a confirmation code from that card. We answer within one month.
