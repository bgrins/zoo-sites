Time-of-travel adjustment

A network-wide adjustment is added to every base fare. It changes through the day with loading on the corridor, so printed figures are never the price charged: the kiosk quotes your exact fare on the review screen before your card is charged, and the receipt repeats the amount taken.

Reduced fares

Reduced fares apply to holders of a Verlan concession card: over-65s, under-18s in education, and registered assistance travellers. The kiosk may ask you to present the card to the reader when you confirm.

Refunds

Unused tickets bought at a kiosk are refundable for 28 days at any staffed office. Bring the card you paid with and quote the confirmation code printed on the receipt screen.
