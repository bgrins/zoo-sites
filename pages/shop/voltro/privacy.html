Privacy Notice

Last revised 2026-05-02.

What we collect

Order details, delivery addresses, and the pages you browse on Voltro. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never stored against your Voltro account.

How it is used

To fulfil orders, to prevent fraud, and to show you relevant listings. Aggregated, de-identified figures are shared with sellers about their own listings only.

Cookies

A session cookie keeps your cart together while you shop; it is essential and cannot be declined. Measurement cookies are optional — the banner on the storefront lets you accept or decline them, and declining does not change what you can buy.

Interest-based ads

Sponsored placements in listings are marked "Sponsored". They are selected from your browsing on Voltro only; we do not buy third-party browsing history. You can opt out of personalised placement by declining measurement cookies.

Your choices

Write to privacy@voltro.example to request a copy or deletion of your data. Requests are answered within 30 days.
