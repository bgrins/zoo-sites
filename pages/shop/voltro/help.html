Help

Most questions are answered fastest from the pages below. For anything else, the help desk is open every day, 8:00 to 22:00 Mountain Time.

Your orders

Check where an order stands from Orders & returns with the order number from your confirmation and the last name it ships to. Guest orders work the same way. To cancel an order or return an item, call the help desk.

Delivery and returns

Delivery windows, return labels and refund timing are covered in Delivery & returns. Monitors travel double-boxed; keep the packaging for the first 30 days.

Deals and promotions

Daily deal codes are published on the Deals of the Day page of the Voltro mobile site and are redeemed in the promotion box on the payment step of checkout. One code per customer per day.

Two baskets, two sellers

Monitors from the storefront go to your shopping cart and are sold by Voltro Retail. Desk Setup accessories are supplied by Voltro Business Supply, so they sit in a separate Desk basket; its checkout books delivery and emails an invoice to pay online.
