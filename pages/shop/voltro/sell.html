Sell on Voltro

Marketplace sellers list alongside Voltro Retail and keep their own pricing, stock and returns windows. Listings are marked with the seller of record.

Fees

A flat 8% referral fee per sale in the Electronics categories, billed monthly. No listing fees, no subscription. Optional fulfillment by Voltro is priced per unit by size band.

Requirements

A registered business, a returns address in-country, and specification sheets for every monitor listed. Catalog quality checks reject listings whose panel size, resolution or refresh rate cannot be verified.

Getting started

Write to sellers@voltro.example with your business registration and a sample product sheet. Accounts are approved within 3 business days, and your first listing can be live the same day.
