Delivery & Returns

Delivery

Items sold and shipped by Voltro Retail ship free. In-stock monitors leave the warehouse within 24 hours; accessories within 1 business day. Same-day pickup is offered at the Downtown counter for items flagged on the listing.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery in the original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving. Marketplace sellers set their own windows, shown on the listing.

Cancellations

An order can be cancelled from Your Orders until it enters packing. After that, refuse the delivery or start a return once it arrives. Daily deal discounts are restored to the item price on cancellation and cannot be re-redeemed.

Damaged or wrong items

Contact Customer Service within 48 hours of delivery with your order number. Panels with shipping damage are replaced ahead of the return.
