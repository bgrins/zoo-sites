Delivery & returns

Delivery

Items sold and shipped by Voltro Retail ship free. In-stock monitors leave the warehouse within 24 hours; accessories within 1 business day. Same-day pickup is offered at the Downtown counter for items flagged on the listing.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery in the original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving. For a prepaid return label, call the help desk on 1-303-555-0176 with the order number; the label is emailed to the address on the order. Marketplace sellers set their own windows, shown on the listing.

Cancellations

The help desk can cancel an order until it enters packing, and Orders & returns shows whether it has. After that, refuse the delivery or start a return once it arrives. Daily deal discounts are restored to the item price on cancellation and cannot be re-redeemed.

Damaged or wrong items

Contact Help within 48 hours of delivery with your order number. Panels with shipping damage are replaced ahead of the return.
