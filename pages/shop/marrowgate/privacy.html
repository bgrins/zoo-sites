Privacy policy

Who we are

Marrowgate Retail Group runs the Marrowgate stores and this website from 1200 Ferrand Boulevard, Skellbourne, MI 48000. This policy was last updated on 2026-04-01.

What we collect

  • What you put in your basket, the orders you place, and the store you pick up from.
  • Your name, email, phone number and delivery address when you check out.
  • The email address you give us for a stock alert, kept only until the alert is sent.
  • My Marrowgate membership details and receipts, whether you join online or at a till.
  • A session cookie that keeps your basket together. We do not set advertising cookies.

How we use it

To fill and deliver orders, to hold pickup orders at your store, to send the stock alerts you ask for, and to answer you when you contact customer service. We do not sell personal information, and we share delivery details only with the crew that delivers your order.

Card payments

Cards are taken at the till or by the delivery crew on a Marrowgate card terminal. Card numbers never pass through this website.

Keeping and deleting

Order records are kept for seven years for tax purposes. Stock alert addresses are deleted when the alert is sent or after 90 days, whichever is sooner.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email privacy@marrowgate.example or call customer service on 1-616-555-0137. We answer within 30 days.
