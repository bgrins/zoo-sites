Trade-in and recycling

Trade-in

Working monitors, laptops and tablets can be traded at the customer service desk for store credit. Bring the power cable; quotes are made on the spot and are valid for 14 days.

Recycling

Displays carry a state electronics recycling levy at the till, shown as its own line in your order summary. The levy funds free take-back: any brand of display is accepted for recycling at door 2, no purchase required, limit two per visit.

Data

Storage devices are wiped to NIST 800-88 standard before recycling. Ask for the wipe certificate at drop-off if you need one for your records.
