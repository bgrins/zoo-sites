Basket help

Adding items

Use Add to basket on a listing or product page. The basket keeps one line per SKU and adds quantities together.

Promotion codes

Codes are entered in the basket, before the payment step. One code per order; a new code replaces the old. Current codes and their terms are published on the offers page.

Recycling levy and tax

A state recycling levy is added per display and shown as its own summary line. Estimated tax is calculated on the discounted subtotal.

Payment

Choose Continue to checkout in the basket, then pick up at your store or have the order delivered. Pickup orders are paid at the till when you collect; delivered orders are paid by card to the delivery crew. Nothing is charged on this website.
