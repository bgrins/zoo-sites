Terms of sale

Ordering online

An online order reserves the items for pickup at your chosen store or books them for delivery. The sale is made when you pay: at the till when you collect, or by card to the delivery crew at your door. Nothing is charged on this website.

Prices

Online prices are the same at every Marrowgate store and exclude sales tax. A state electronics recycling levy is added for each display and shown as its own line. If a price changes between your order and your payment, you pay the lower of the two.

Promotion codes

One code per order, entered in the basket. Each code carries the terms printed on the offers page. An expired code cannot be reinstated.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup orders are held for five days. Delivery is free within our delivery area and is booked for the next available day; the crew calls ahead.

Returns and price match

See the return policy and the price match guarantee.
