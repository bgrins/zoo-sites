Return policy

Return window

Most products: 30 days. Monitors and other displays: 15 days, original packaging required. Opened software and gift cards are not returnable.

How to return

Bring the item and your receipt or order number to any Marrowgate store, or start a return from Order status to get a prepaid label. Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 days of the item arriving back.

Open-box and clearance

Open-box items follow the same windows and are refunded at the price paid. Clearance items marked final sale at the shelf cannot be returned.

Refurbished stock

Refurbished monitors carry a 90-day limited warranty and the standard 15-day return window.
