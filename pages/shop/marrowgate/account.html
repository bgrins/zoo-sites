  • Free shipping on orders over $35 on Member Mondays
  • Order history and e-receipts under one account
  • Stock alerts for sold-out items, by email
  • Extended 45-day returns on most products (displays stay at 15 days)

Alerts and order history need a signed-in session; browsing, baskets and promotion codes work without one.
