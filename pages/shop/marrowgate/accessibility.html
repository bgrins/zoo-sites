Accessibility

On this site

Listing controls, refinements and basket actions are labelled for screen readers, and every price is exposed as plain text alongside the styled tag. If a page does not work with your assistive technology, tell us: access@marrowgate.example.

In store

All stores have step-free entrances, accessible tills, and staff-assisted shopping by appointment through customer service. Service animals are welcome.

Feedback

We review accessibility reports weekly. Include the page address and the software you were using; we reply within five business days.
