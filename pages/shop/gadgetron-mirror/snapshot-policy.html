Policy

This node serves the most recent complete snapshot taken from the primary store, identified in the strip above by its capture time. It never mixes data from two snapshots: a department is either rebuilt in full from the current snapshot or held back as pending.

A snapshot is taken shortly before every planned maintenance window. Prices and warehouse status reflect the primary store at capture time and are not updated while the node is serving, which is why order queueing is disabled here rather than risk selling against stale stock.

When a window closes, this node drains its readers and returns to standby. Nothing entered on the mirror carries over, because nothing can be entered on the mirror.

Questions about a specific price in the snapshot: the support desk can read the live warehouse figure by phone.
