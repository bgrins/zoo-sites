Bulk quote

For 10 units or more of any part number, the buying desk quotes tiered pricing off the spec index list price. Quotes are answered within one business day and held for 14 days.

Part numbers are shown in the Model column of the catalog index. Sold-out part numbers cannot be quoted; see the approved substitution list.
