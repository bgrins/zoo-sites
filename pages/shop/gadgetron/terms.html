Terms of sale

The terms on which Gadgetron Trading Co. sells from the spec index. Marketplace listings are sold by their seller of record on the seller's own terms; see the marketplace seller terms.

Orders

An order list becomes an order when the buying desk confirms it by email and the pro forma invoice is paid, or, for trade accounts, when the desk confirms it against net terms. A sold-out part number cannot be ordered; the desk accepts only the alternate named on the approved substitution list.

Prices

List prices are per unit in USD and exclude sales tax and the recycling levy. The price engine refreshes throughout the day; the price that applies is the one on the confirmed order. Obvious pricing errors are corrected before confirmation, never after.

Delivery

Confirmed orders ship from the warehouse on the next carrier run after the 15:00 cut-off. Freight-class items are booked with the carrier by the desk. See the warehouse policy for cut-off times and partial shipments.

Returns

Returns follow the return window and restocking fees in the warehouse policy. Open-box units carry a 90-day limited warranty.
