Support desk

One desk answers the catalog, the price engine, the warehouse and the marketplace. It reads live warehouse figures, so it can settle a question the catalog cannot.

Have the part number to hand for a catalog question, or the order reference from your confirmation for anything already queued. The desk cannot look an order up by name alone, because a trading name often covers several buyers.

What the desk can settle

  • Live stock for a part number the catalog shows as sold out online, including whether an approved alternate is in the warehouse today.
  • Whether a freight-class item makes the next scheduled carrier run.
  • A price the engine and a marketplace listing disagree on, which is nearly always a listing the seller has not republished yet.
  • A return started inside the window on the warehouse policy, including the restocking fee that will appear on the credit note.
  • Splitting a confirmed list into more than one shipment, where the carrier has not collected it yet.

What it cannot do

  • Take a backorder. The warehouse refuses a sold-out part number at the order list rather than holding it open.
  • Honour a price from a stale page or from an older mirror snapshot.
  • Change the terms a marketplace seller publishes on their own listing. The desk will open the dispute and stay on it, but the seller's window governs.

Escalating

Ask the agent to raise the call to a desk supervisor if a question has been open for more than two business days. Supervisors work the same hours as the desk and can commit the warehouse to a date, which a first-line agent cannot. A trade account can escalate through its account manager instead, which is usually quicker during an accounting close.

During a service incident

When the primary store is offline the desk stays open on the same number and extends to seven days a week for the duration. Read-only catalog pages are served from the mirror node, which takes no orders; the desk takes orders by phone against the live warehouse figure instead. Open and recent incidents are listed on the service status history.
