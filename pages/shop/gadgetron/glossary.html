Spec glossary

Definitions for the columns of the monitor spec index.

Diag in

Diagonal of the visible panel in inches, measured corner to corner.

Native res / Class

Native resolution in pixels, and its class label: FHD is 1920x1080, QHD is 2560x1440, UHD-4K is 3840x2160. Running below native resolution softens the image.

Panel

All rows in the current index are IPS: wide viewing angles and stable colour, at the cost of contrast versus VA panels.

Hz

Maximum refresh rate at native resolution. 60 Hz suits office and video work; 144 Hz and up is aimed at gaming and motion-critical work.

Rating and reviews

Mean customer rating out of 5, with the count of reviews it is drawn from. Ratings pool Gadgetron direct and marketplace purchases of the same part number.

Stock

Warehouse status at the last index refresh; see the status codes.
