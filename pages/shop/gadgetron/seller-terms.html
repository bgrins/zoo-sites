Marketplace seller terms

Terms for third-party sellers listing on the Gadgetron spec index.

Listing requirements

Every listing binds to a manufacturer part number and the manufacturer specification sheet. Sellers may not alter specification fields; only price, stock and lead time are seller-controlled.

Seller of record

Gadgetron is not the seller of record for marketplace items. The seller's trading name appears on the order confirmation and the invoice.

Performance

  • Orders confirmed within 1 business day, shipped within 3.
  • Stock accuracy of 98% or better, measured weekly.
  • Returns honoured at least to the warehouse policy baseline.

Fees

8% commission on the line price, invoiced monthly. No listing or subscription fees.

Delisting

Listings that miss performance floors two weeks running are suspended from the index until the seller passes a review with the marketplace desk.
