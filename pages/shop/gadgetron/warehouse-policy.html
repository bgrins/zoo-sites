Warehouse policy

Operational terms for orders fulfilled from the Gadgetron warehouse. Marketplace sellers publish their own terms on the listing.

Warehouse status codes

Each catalog row carries one status. In stock: pickable now, queueable on an order list. Sold out online: not queueable; an approved alternate may be published in the substitution list. In-store collection only: sold at the counter, never shipped.

Backorder terms

The warehouse does not take backorders. A sold-out part number is refused at the order list rather than held open, so a queued list is always shippable in full.

Cut-off times

Order lists confirmed by 15:00 ship the same business day. Lists confirmed later ship the next business day. Freight-class items ship on the next scheduled carrier run.

Partial shipments

Lists ship complete by default. Split shipment can be requested at confirmation; each shipment carries its own tracking and delivery note, with no extra freight charge inside the same service level.

Return window

30 days from delivery for stock items in resalable condition. Monitors: 15 days, original packaging required. Marketplace items follow the seller's stated window.

Restocking fees

None for unopened stock items. Opened monitors: 10% of the line price. Special-order and freight-class items: 15%. Fees are shown on the credit note before you confirm the return.

Net terms

Approved trade accounts settle net 30 from invoice date. Applications go through your account manager; see account access.

Tax exemption

Exemption certificates are filed against the trade account before ordering. Tax charged before a certificate is filed is not refundable at the desk.
