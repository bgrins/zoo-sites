Approved substitution list

3 active substitutions

When a part is sold out online, the buying desk publishes the alternate it will accept in its place. Only the alternate named in the table below may be ordered against a sold-out part number. The list is revised every Monday.

Sold-out part numbers and the alternate approved for each. One approved alternate per part.

Rows 1-3 of 3. Alternates are matched on panel class and connector set, not on price; an alternate may cost more than the part it replaces.

How a substitution is applied

Order the approved alternate's own part number. The desk does not convert a sold-out line for you, and a sold-out part number cannot be added to an order list.

Price is the alternate's list price on the day of the order. No price protection is carried over from the sold-out part.

Alternates that are not approved

Buyers frequently propose the ScreenCraft SC-27U HDR against PixelForge PF-27 because the panel class matches. SC-27U HDR is not an approved alternate and the desk will reject the line at goods-in.

Likewise the ClaritySee CS27-4K Pro is not accepted against any open-box part number, and the PixelPeak P27U Value is not accepted against PixelForge PF-27.

Escalation

To propose an alternate that is not on the list, raise a buying desk ticket with the manufacturer datasheet attached. Decisions are published in the following Monday revision.
