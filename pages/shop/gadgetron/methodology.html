Price engine methodology

How the Price USD column on the spec index is produced.

Sources

Gadgetron direct pricing comes from the warehouse system. Marketplace offers are polled hourly; the engine lists the lowest current offer from a seller in good standing.

What the listed price includes

Prices are per unit in USD, excluding tax and the recycling levy. The levy and tax are itemised on the order list before confirmation.

Refresh cadence

The index refreshes hourly on the hour. A price shown on a queued order list line is honoured for 24 hours from the time it was queued.

Errors

Obvious feed errors (a misplaced decimal, a currency mismatch) are corrected on the next refresh, and affected order lists are re-priced with the buyer notified before shipment.
