Privacy notice

What the spec index and the price engine record about a visit, how long each record is kept, and who inside Gadgetron Trading Co. can read it.

What this store records

Browsing the catalog needs a session cookie and nothing else. The cookie identifies the order list you are building and the filters you have applied, so a reload does not empty either. It carries no name, no address and no account number, and it expires when the browser session ends.

Access logs record the path requested, the response code, the referring page inside this store, and a coarse network region. They exist so the warehouse team can size the price engine for the hours it is actually busy.

Order lists and quotes

An order list is held against your session until it is confirmed. Once it is confirmed, the part numbers, quantities and delivery address move into the warehouse system, where they sit with the order record for as long as the return window and the accounting year require. A bulk quote is held the same way, against the trade account that asked for it.

Trade accounts

A trade account holds the details its account manager needs: the trading name, the billing and delivery addresses, the named buyers who may confirm a list, any exemption certificate on file, and the settlement history. Sign-in attempts are logged with their outcome. Buyers see their own account only; account managers see the accounts assigned to them.

Marketplace listings

Marketplace sellers publish their own listings and set their own terms. When a marketplace item is queued, the seller receives the part number, the quantity and the delivery address, and nothing else about the buyer. Seller messaging is retained for the length of the seller's stated return window so a dispute can be read back in full.

Retention and deletion

  • Session cookies: cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Access logs: rotated after 30 days.
  • Unconfirmed order lists and quotes: discarded after 14 days of no activity.
  • Confirmed orders and credit notes: kept for the accounting year and six years after.
  • Closed trade accounts: reduced to the settlement record after 90 days.

Who else sees it

Carriers receive the delivery address and the parcel weights. Payment processors receive the amount and the reference, never the catalog rows. Nothing on this store is sold to anyone, and no advertising network is given access to the catalog session.

Asking what is held

Write to privacy@gadgetron.example to see, correct or delete what this store holds about you. Include the trading name if the request concerns a trade account. Questions about an order in flight are faster through the support desk, which can read the order record directly.
