What the closed incidents were

  • MB-3-1174: a planned migration of the warehouse database onto the new pricing cluster, 04:00 to 09:30 (UTC-05). While it ran, every catalog path on the primary store answered with a maintenance notice, and read-only pricing was served from mirror node MB-3, built from a snapshot taken at 03:50 just before the window opened. The mirror took no orders; the support desk took them by phone against the live warehouse figure. The mirror returned to standby when the window closed.
  • MB-3-1121: a feed lag left the price engine quoting figures the warehouse had already superseded. Quotes issued during the lag were honoured at the lower of the two figures. The mirror node was not engaged.
  • MB-3-1087: a carrier interface fault delayed order confirmation mail by up to nine hours. Shipments themselves were unaffected and no cut-off was missed.
  • MB-3-1042: seller messaging stopped delivering for part of a day. Return windows that fell inside the outage were extended by the same number of days for every affected listing.
  • MB-3-1015: a planned rebuild of the spec index. Filters and sorting were unavailable; the catalog rows themselves stayed readable throughout.

How a window is announced

Planned windows are posted here at least five business days ahead and repeated on the catalog ticker for the two days before they open. Trade accounts with net terms are notified by their account manager as well, because a window that crosses a cut-off moves that day's shipping.

An unplanned interruption is logged here once operations has confirmed it, which is usually inside the hour. The row is updated rather than replaced as the picture changes, so the history reads as what was known at the time rather than as a tidy retelling.

Service credits

A planned window inside the announced hours carries no credit. An unplanned interruption that stops a trade account from confirming a list before the 15:00 cut-off carries one, applied to the next invoice; ask the account manager to raise it. Credits are not paid on marketplace listings, since the seller of record is not Gadgetron.
