Complaints & Appeals

Customer Complaint Procedure

Adopted by the Board of Commissioners under borough ordinance. Every account holder is entitled to have a disputed charge, a service failure or the conduct of a member of staff reviewed at three successive levels, without charge, and to have water service maintained while a disputed amount is under review.

Level One — The Counter

Raise the matter with the clerk on duty at the Works Yard office, or by telephone to customer service on (802) 555-0122 during counter hours. Most complaints are resolved here: a misread register, a payment credited to the wrong premises, an estimate that turned out badly, a missed final reading. The clerk records the complaint on the account, so a later escalation does not start from nothing.

Bring or quote the service account number, the billing period in dispute and the amount you consider owing. Where the complaint concerns a reading, ask for a re-read; a register found to have been misread is corrected at no charge.

Level Two — The Superintendent

An account holder dissatisfied with the counter's answer may ask in writing for review by the Superintendent of Works. Address the request to the Works Yard, 4 Aqueduct Road, marked for the Superintendent's attention, within thirty days of the counter's decision.

Acknowledgement Within five business days of receipt.
Written decision Within twenty business days, stating the finding and the reason for it.
Meter testing Ordered where accuracy is in dispute. The test charge on the Rates & Charges page is waived and refunded where the meter is found to over-register.

Level Three — The Board of Commissioners

The final review is by the five-member Board, which sits on the second Tuesday of each month in the Works Yard meeting room. A written appeal must reach the Clerk of the Board ten days before the session at which it is to be heard. The appellant may appear in person, may bring a representative, and may put questions to the Superintendent on the record.

The Board's determination is issued in writing and entered in the minutes, which are available for inspection at the counter. Nothing in this procedure limits recourse to the state utility regulator or to the courts.

Service During a Dispute

Water service is not discontinued over an amount properly in dispute while a review is pending at any level, provided that the undisputed portion of the account is kept current. Where the dispute is resolved against the account holder, the balance falls due twenty-one days from the date of the written decision and no late charge is applied for the period under review.

Sewer service is never discontinued for non-payment; unpaid sanitary charges are certified to the Borough for collection with the property tax.

Emergencies Are Not Complaints

A main break, a flooded basement, a sewer backing up or water that has stopped altogether should go to the duty line, not through this procedure. Call customer service and ask for the distribution office; outside counter hours the same number reaches the on-call operator.
