Replacing an Estimated Reading

A bill issued on an estimated reading (code E on the bill) is recalculated from a reading you took on the day of the estimate. Enter the reading from the black digits of the register, in hundreds of cubic feet; ignore the red digits and the sweep hand.

A bill issued on an actual reading (code A) or on a reading you supplied (code C) cannot be changed here. To dispute one, call customer service or write to the billing clerk.
