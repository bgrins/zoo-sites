Rules and Regulations of Service

Water and sewer service is supplied under the rules adopted by the Board of Commissioners. This page summarizes the rules that most often affect an account holder; the full rules are available for inspection at the counter.

Application for Service

Service is supplied to the owner or occupant of the premises who applies for it and accepts responsibility for the charges. A new occupant applies through the Transfer of Service desk; service continues without interruption while a transfer is processed.

Responsibility The account holder of record is responsible for all charges up to the final reading taken at a transfer or a close of service.
Access to meters Authority staff must be able to reach the meter during daylight hours for reading, testing and replacement.

Charges and Payment

Charges are billed at the rates on the Rates & Charges page and are due as set out under Billing & Payment. A disputed charge is handled under the complaint procedure, and service is maintained while it is under review.

Interruptions and Discontinuance

The Authority may interrupt service without notice for an emergency repair, and with at least forty-eight hours’ notice for planned work. Water service may be discontinued for an undisputed balance more than sixty days past due, after written notice to the account holder and the occupant. Sewer service is never discontinued for non-payment.

The use of this website is governed by the same rules. A request submitted through the website is received when the Authority confirms it with a reference number.
