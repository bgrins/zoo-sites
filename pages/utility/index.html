Accounts and Service

The Authority supplies metered water and sanitary sewer service to approximately 6,400 accounts in the Borough of Grelsby and the Tannery Flats service district. Meters are read on a bimonthly route cycle and bills are issued within ten days of the reading.

Moving in or out Use the Transfer of Service desk to move an account to a new occupant. A final reading is scheduled automatically.
Your bills Bills for the last twelve months, and a form for submitting your own meter reading, are in My Account.
Current tariffs The quarterly schedule of rates and charges is published on the Rates & Charges page.
Counter hours Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., at the Works Yard office.

Board of Commissioners

The Authority is governed by a five-member board appointed by the Borough Council. Regular sessions are held on the second Tuesday of each month in the Works Yard meeting room; minutes are available for inspection at the counter.
