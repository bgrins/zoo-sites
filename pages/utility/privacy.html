Privacy Statement

Adopted by the Board of Commissioners

This statement describes the customer records the Authority keeps, why it keeps them, and when they may be released. It applies to records held at the Works Yard office and to requests made through this website.

Records We Keep

Account records Name of the account holder, service address, mailing address, telephone number, meter number and size, and the reading and payment history of the account.
Service requests Transfers of service, final-reading requests and complaints, with the date received and the clerk who recorded them.
Website requests What you enter on a request form, and the date and time it was received. The site sets one cookie to keep a request together while you complete it; it is not used for advertising.

How Records Are Used

Customer records are used to read meters, issue bills, credit payments, schedule final readings and answer complaints. The Authority does not sell customer records and does not use them for any purpose other than the operation of the water and sewer system.

Records are shared only where the operation of the system or the law requires it: with the Borough for the collection of certified sewer charges, with the state drinking water program for sampling at a customer tap, and in answer to a court order.

Public Records Requests

Board minutes, the adopted schedule of rates and water quality results are public and may be inspected at the counter. Individual account records are not released to anyone but the account holder, or a person the account holder authorizes in writing, except as required by law.

To see or correct the records held on your account, bring the service account number to the counter, or write to the Works Yard, 4 Aqueduct Road, marked for the Records Clerk. Requests are answered within ten business days.
