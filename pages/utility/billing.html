Billing & Payment

Bills and Due Dates

The Authority reads meters on a bimonthly route cycle and issues each bill within ten days of the reading that produced it. A bill therefore covers two months of consumption, one fixed service charge for the meter size on the premises, and the sanitary sewer surcharge computed from the water charge. The Rates & Charges page carries the schedule the Board has adopted for the current quarter, and every bill issued in the last twelve months can be opened in My Account.

Payment due Twenty-one days from the date printed on the bill.
Late charge One and one-half percent per month on the unpaid balance, applied at the next billing run.
Part payment Accepted at the counter and credited to the oldest balance first.

How to Pay

Four methods are open to every account holder. Whichever you choose, quote the service account number printed at the top of the bill so the payment is credited to the right premises rather than held as an unapplied receipt.

At the counter Works Yard office, 4 Aqueduct Road, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Cash, check and money order are accepted; the counter does not hold card facilities.
By mail Check or money order to the Works Yard address, with the return portion of the bill. Do not send cash by mail.
Night depository The steel box beside the Aqueduct Road gate is emptied at 8:30 a.m. each business day. Payments left after that hour are posted with the following day's receipts.
Automatic bank payment Your bank’s bill-pay service can send a set amount on a set day of the month. The counter will tell you what payee details to use; the Authority does not draw on customer accounts itself.

Budget Arrangements

An account holder who expects difficulty with a bill should speak to the billing clerk before the due date rather than after it. The Authority will spread an unpaid balance over as many as six bimonthly cycles where the account has no prior default, and no late charge accrues on an instalment kept to its terms. Arrangements are recorded at the counter and confirmed to the account holder in writing.

Estimated Readings

Where a meter cannot be read — a locked yard, an obstructed pit, a frozen register — the route clerk records an estimate based on the same period in the prior year, and the bill is marked accordingly. The next actual reading reconciles the account in full. An account holder who read the meter on the day of the estimate may submit that reading in My Account, and the bill is recalculated from it. Persistent inability to read a meter is referred to the distribution office for an access appointment.

Closing a Bill on a Move

An account holder leaving the premises should not simply stop paying. Use the Transfer of Service desk, which schedules the final reading and issues the closing bill from it. Consumption after the final reading belongs to the incoming occupant, and the departing holder's account number is retired once the closing bill is settled.
