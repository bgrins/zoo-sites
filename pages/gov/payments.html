NOTICE: The electronic payment portal operates during the general filing season (January 20 through April 15) and for thirty days after. It is now closed until the season reopens. Payments by mail and at the counter are accepted year-round.

Electronic payment. During the filing season, the portal accepts payment of filing fees and assessed amounts by electronic funds withdrawal for Combined Declarations (Forms CD-1, CD-1EZ and CD-2) filed electronically. Vehicle declarations (the RV series) are paper-only filings and cannot be paid through the portal.

Payment by mail. Send a check or money order payable to Commonwealth Treasurer together with the form it pays, to the filing address stated in that form's instructions. Write the form number and your account number on the face of the check. Do not send cash through the mail. A payment mailed without its form is posted to the account unapplied and may delay processing.

Payment at the counter. Any office listed under Office Locations accepts check, money order, or cash at the window during counter hours. The Millbrook satellite office handles payments and account inquiries only.

Returned items. A check returned unpaid by the bank is subject to a $28.00 returned-item charge in addition to any late-filing surcharge that accrues while the payment is unresolved.

Amounts due are computed by the filer. Consult the Schedule of Filing Fees and the applicable form instructions.
