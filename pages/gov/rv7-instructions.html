BUREAU OF CIVIC REVENUE
FORM RV-7 INSTRUCTIONS
[ Main Page ] [ All Forms ] [ Form RV-7 ] [ Schedule of Filing Fees ]

These instructions cover who must file, how to compute the declared value, and the late-filing penalty schedule. The annual deadline is June 12 (see the Form RV-7 page).

The printed booklet (12 pages) contains the same instructions and a worksheet copy of the form. Copies are available at any Bureau office.

Who must file

File one Form RV-7 for each registered household vehicle that you kept in the Commonwealth for more than 180 days of the declaration year. Count every day the vehicle was garaged at an address in the Commonwealth, including days it was out of service.

Do not file Form RV-7 for a vehicle registered to a business or used for hire; those vehicles are declared on Form RV-9. A vehicle sold, scrapped or moved out of the Commonwealth before its 181st day in the declaration year is not declared.

Line-by-line instructions

Part A, Filer. Print your name, your Bureau account number and your mailing address. If you do not have an account number, leave the box blank; one is assigned when the declaration is opened.

Line 1. Enter the vehicle identification number exactly as it appears on the registration card, including any leading zeros.

Line 2. Enter the Commonwealth registration plate number.

Line 3. Enter the model year, make and body style.

Line 4. Enter the date you acquired the vehicle and the number of days it was kept in the Commonwealth during the declaration year.

Line 5. Enter the purchase price shown on the bill of sale. For a vehicle received as a gift or inheritance, enter the value stated on the transfer of title.

Line 6. Multiply Line 5 by the percentage in Table 1 for the vehicle's age and enter the result, rounded to the nearest whole dollar. This is the declared value.

Line 7. Enter the base filing fee for Form RV-7 from the Schedule of Filing Fees.

Line 8. If you are filing after the deadline, enter the late-filing surcharge. See "Late filing" below.

Line 9. Add Lines 7 and 8. Pay this amount by check or money order payable to Commonwealth Treasurer, and enclose it with the declaration.

Part B, Declaration. Sign and date the declaration on page 2. An unsigned declaration is returned unprocessed.

Computing the declared value

The vehicle's age is the declaration year minus its model year. Use the percentage for that age from Table 1.

Table 1. Age of vehiclePercentage of Line 5
Current model year or newer90%
1 year78%
2 years66%
3 years55%
4 years45%
5 years36%
6 years or older28%

If you believe Table 1 overstates the value of a damaged vehicle, file the declaration as computed and attach a written statement with a repair estimate. The Office of Valuation Review will reply by mail.

Late filing

A declaration postmarked after the deadline is subject to the late-filing surcharge set out in footnote † of the Schedule of Filing Fees. The Bureau does not compute the surcharge for you; enter it on Line 8. A declaration filed late without the surcharge is accepted, and the unpaid surcharge is billed by mail.

To ask that a surcharge be waived for reasonable cause, file Form PR-19, Penalty Reconsideration Request, after the declaration has been accepted.

Where to file Form RV-7

An original declaration is opened by the Declarations Unit. Mail the signed declaration to:

Bureau of Civic Revenue, Declarations Unit

PO Box 4410, Statehouse Plaza Station

Do not send an original declaration to the amendments annex; see Form RV-7A Instructions for amended filings, which use a different box and a different deadline.
