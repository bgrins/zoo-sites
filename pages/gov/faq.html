FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q. When is my declaration due?
A. Deadlines vary by form. Consult the individual form page under Forms & Publications.

Q. I filed Form CD-1 but have not received a confirmation letter.
A. Allow six (6) weeks for paper filings. Electronic filers receive a confirmation number immediately.

Q. Can I file my vehicle declaration at a satellite office?
A. Yes. See Office Locations for hours.

Q. Is there a penalty for filing late?
A. Yes. Each form's instructions include the applicable penalty schedule.

Q. The document I requested did not download.
A. Some archived documents are being migrated and are temporarily unavailable. Please try again during the next filing season or request a copy by mail.

Q. Do I need Form RV-9 for the family car?
A. No. Form RV-9 is for commercial vehicles only. Household vehicles use the residential declaration; see Forms & Publications.
