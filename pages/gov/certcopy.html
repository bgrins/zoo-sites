NOTICE: Requests made on this page are entered in the Bureau's records as soon as they are received. Each submission of the form is entered, and billed, as a separate request.

The Records and Disclosure Division issues copies of declarations, applications and notices filed with the Bureau. A certified copy bears the Bureau's seal and the signature of the custodian of records, and is accepted by courts, lenders and other agencies in place of the original. An uncertified copy is a plain photocopy for the filer's own records.

Who may request. The account holder, a joint filer named on the declaration, or a representative whose power of attorney is on file with the Bureau. Copies are sent only to the mailing address on file for the account, or held at the Central Office window for collection in person with photo identification.

Fees. $12.00 for each certified copy and $1.00 for each uncertified copy. The fee is added to the account's next statement; do not send payment with the request.

Records available. Declarations and applications are kept for seven (7) years after the tax year they cover. Requests for earlier years must be made in writing to the Records and Disclosure Division through the Correspondence Unit.
