Requests for copies of filed documents may be checked here as soon as they are entered. Enter an account number to list every request on the account, or a request number to see a single request. A request that is still on file may be withdrawn from the listing.

The status of a filed declaration itself is given by the automated status line; see Check Filing Status.

 

Status codes shown in the listing:
StatusMeaning
ON FILEThe request has been entered and is awaiting preparation. It may still be withdrawn.
PREPAREDThe copies have been made and certified and are being mailed or held for collection.
WITHDRAWNThe request was withdrawn. No copies are sent and no fee is charged.

Questions about a request may be directed to the Records and Disclosure Division through the general inquiries line, (804) 555-0100, weekdays 8:30 to 4:30.
