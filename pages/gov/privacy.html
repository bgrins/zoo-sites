PRIVACY STATEMENT

This statement explains what the Bureau of Civic Revenue collects when you use this site or file with the Bureau, who may see it, and how you may obtain the records the Bureau holds about you. It was adopted by the Records and Disclosure Division and is reviewed each year with the Compliance Handbook.

Visiting this site. The Bureau's web server records the network address of the computer requesting a page, the date and time of the request, the page requested and the page that referred you to it. These server logs are used to count visits, to find broken links and to keep the site secure. They are not used to identify individual visitors and are destroyed under the retention schedule.

Cookies. The site sets one session cookie so that the site search and the department directory can tell one visit from another. The cookie holds a random reference number and nothing else, and it expires when you close your browser. The Bureau does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Site search. The words you enter in the site search are kept with the session reference so that the index can be improved. They are not linked to any filer account.

Declarations and payments. Information you give the Bureau on a declaration, payment voucher or correspondence is confidential under the revenue laws of the Commonwealth. It is used to administer those laws and is disclosed outside the Bureau only where a statute permits, such as to the Commonwealth Treasurer for the application of payments. Bureau personnel may consult a filer's account only when an assigned duty requires it.

Electronic mail. The Bureau does not accept filings, payments or account inquiries by electronic mail, and will not ask you for an account number by electronic mail. Treat any such request as fraudulent and report it to the Correspondence Unit.

Requesting your own records. You may ask for a copy of the records the Bureau holds about your account. Write to the Records and Disclosure Division through the Correspondence Unit (see Contact the Bureau), giving your name, your account number, the records wanted and the declaration years concerned, and sign the request. The Division acknowledges a request within ten business days and tells you of any copying charge before copies are made.

Public records. Bureau forms, bulletins, the Schedule of Filing Fees and the Compliance Handbook are public records. Requests for other Bureau records are made in the same way and are answered under the public records law of the Commonwealth.

Other sites. Links to other agencies are provided for convenience. Their pages are governed by their own privacy statements.

Questions about this statement may be directed to the Records and Disclosure Division through the Correspondence Unit, or by telephone to the general inquiries line at (804) 555-0100.
