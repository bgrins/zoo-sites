By telephone. General inquiries: (555) 014-2200, Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Please have your account or confirmation number ready. Callers using a text telephone may dial the TTY line at (555) 014-2210 during the same hours.

By mail. Written correspondence should be addressed to:

Bureau of Civic Revenue
Correspondence Unit
Revenue Building, Statehouse Plaza

Do NOT mail declarations or payments to the correspondence address. Each form's instructions state the correct filing address for that form; see Forms & Publications.

In person. Window service is available at the Central Office and at the satellite offices listed under Office Locations. For the direct extension of a specific service desk, consult the Department Directory.

About this site. Reports of a broken link or an out-of-date listing should be directed to the webmaster through the Correspondence Unit at the address above, or by telephone. Please include the address of the page concerned. The Bureau does not accept filings, payments, or account inquiries by electronic mail.
