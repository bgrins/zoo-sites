Records and Disclosure Division

Keeps the filing archive and the retention schedule, issues certified copies, publishes Bureau forms, and answers public records requests. The offices of this division are listed below.

  • Office of the Filing Archive — Retention, retrieval and certified copies.
  • Office of Disclosure Requests — Public records requests and redaction review.
  • Office of Forms Control — Form numbering, revision control, print orders and alternate formats.
