Office of Payment Processing

Lockbox, counter and electronic remittance. The sections of this office are listed below.

  • Lockbox Operations Section — Opening and posting of mailed remittances.
  • Counter Receipts Section — Window payments and same-day receipting.
  • Returned Items Section — Checks returned unpaid, and the returned-item charge.
