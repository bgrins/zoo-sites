Office of Delinquency Review

Aged balances, liens and write-off recommendations. The sections of this office are listed below.

  • Aged Balances Section — Balances past the second notice cycle.
  • Lien Preparation Section — Preparation, recording and release of revenue liens.
  • Write-Off Review Section — Uncollectible balances recommended for write-off.
