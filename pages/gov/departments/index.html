Department Directory

The Bureau is organised into four divisions. Each division listing continues to its offices, sections and public service desks. Counter hours and direct extensions are published on the desk pages.

  • Assessment Standards Division — Ground works, site permits, valuation methodology and field inspection.
  • Permits and Clearances Division — Occupancy clearances, street cuts, signage and temporary use consents.
  • Collections and Remittance Division — Payments and instalments, delinquency and write-off review.
  • Records and Disclosure Division — Filing archive, certified copies, disclosure requests and retention.
Directory listings are maintained by the Records and Disclosure Division. Rev. 03/25. Report a wrong extension to the webmaster. Bureau home page.
