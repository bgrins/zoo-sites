Department Directory

The Bureau is organized into four divisions. Each division listing continues to its offices, sections and public service desks. Counter hours and direct telephone lines are published on the desk pages.

  • Assessment Standards Division — Maintains the assessment manuals and valuation tables, reviews declared values, and inspects sites in the field, including excavation and ground works.
  • Permits and Clearances Division — Issues certificates of occupancy and change-of-use consents, and licenses street furniture, signage and temporary uses of public space.
  • Collections and Remittance Division — Receives and posts payments, administers installment agreements, and follows up delinquent balances through notices, liens and write-off review.
  • Records and Disclosure Division — Keeps the filing archive and the retention schedule, issues certified copies, publishes Bureau forms, and answers public records requests.
Directory listings are maintained by the Records and Disclosure Division. Rev. 03/25. Report an incorrect listing to the webmaster through the Correspondence Unit.
