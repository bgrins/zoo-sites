Office of Temporary Use

Short-term consents for events, filming and seasonal uses of public space. The sections of this office are listed below.

  • Event Staging Section — Staging areas, barricades and street closures for permitted events.
  • Seasonal Consents Section — Outdoor seating, holiday markets and other seasonal uses.
