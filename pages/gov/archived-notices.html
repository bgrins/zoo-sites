ARCHIVED NOTICES (1998-2020)

Notices below are retained for reference under the records schedule. They no longer have effect. For bulletins currently in force see Announcements & Bulletins.

NOTICE 2020-12 (December 2020): Form RV-3 is withdrawn. Residential vehicle declarations for declaration year 2021 and later are filed on Form RV-7. Declarations on Form RV-3 postmarked after December 31 will be returned unprocessed.

NOTICE 2002-04 (March 2002): The Bureau's public counter relocates from the ground floor of the Revenue Building to the 4th floor of 1 Assessment Plaza effective April 8. The drop box remains at the Revenue Building.

NOTICE 2001-09 (August 2001): Confirmation letters for paper Combined Declarations are delayed by the records system conversion. Filers should allow ten (10) weeks before inquiring.

NOTICE 2000-01 (January 2000): All Bureau systems completed the century date change without interruption. Deadlines published for the 2000 filing season are unchanged.

NOTICE 1999-06 (May 1999): This site now publishes the full forms index. Filers may download frequently requested forms rather than requesting them by mail. Forms by mail continue to be available through the Correspondence Unit.

NOTICE 1998-02 (June 1998): The Bureau of Civic Revenue establishes its official site on the Commonwealth network. Comments on the site may be directed to the webmaster through the Correspondence Unit.
