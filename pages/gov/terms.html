This site is published by the Bureau of Civic Revenue as a public service. By using it you accept the conditions below.

1. Information only. Pages on this site summarize the Bureau's forms, deadlines and procedures. They do not replace the revenue laws of the Commonwealth, the instructions printed with each form, or a written determination issued to you by the Bureau. Where a page and a form's instructions differ, the instructions govern.

2. Forms. A form printed from this site may be filed in the same way as a form obtained at a Bureau office, provided it is the current revision shown under Forms & Publications. Superseded revisions are returned unprocessed.

3. No filings through this site. Declarations, payments and correspondence are not accepted through this site or by electronic mail. The electronic payment portal is a separate service and is governed by the conditions shown when it is open.

4. Reuse of Bureau material. Text and forms published by the Bureau may be copied and distributed without permission, provided they are not altered and are not presented as a current form after they have been superseded. The Bureau's seal may not be reproduced except by the Bureau.

5. Availability. The site is taken off line for maintenance from time to time, usually overnight. A deadline is not extended because the site was unavailable; paper forms remain available at every office listed under Office Locations.

6. Links. Links to other agencies are provided for convenience. The Bureau is not responsible for the content of other sites.

7. Accuracy. The Bureau takes care to keep this site accurate but does not warrant that every page is current. Report an error to the webmaster through the Correspondence Unit, giving the address of the page concerned.

See also the Privacy Statement and the Accessibility page.
