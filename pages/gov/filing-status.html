The status of a filed declaration may be checked by automated telephone inquiry.

Automated status line: (804) 555-0102. Available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Have ready: the form number you filed, the confirmation number from your acceptance notice (electronic filers) or the date of mailing (paper filers), and the last four digits of your account number.

Processing times. Electronic filings post to the status line within two (2) business days of transmission. Paper filings post when the declaration is opened and keyed; allow six (6) weeks from the date of mailing before inquiring.

Status codes read by the automated line:

CodeMeaning
RECEIVEDThe declaration has been opened and keyed.
IN REVIEWThe declaration is under routine review. No action is required.
ACCEPTEDA confirmation letter has been ordered. Allow two weeks for delivery.
RETURNEDThe declaration was incomplete and has been returned by mail with a deficiency notice.

If the automated line does not recognize your confirmation number, wait one business day and try again before calling the general inquiries line at (804) 555-0100.

Status inquiries over the counter are handled at any office listed under Office Locations.
