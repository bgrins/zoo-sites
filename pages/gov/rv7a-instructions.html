FORM RV-7A INSTRUCTIONS
[ Main Page ] [ All Forms ] [ Form RV-7 Instructions ]

Form RV-7A is the AMENDED residential vehicle declaration. File it only to correct a declaration already accepted for the same declaration year. An original declaration is filed on Form RV-7 and is subject to a different deadline and a different filing address; the two forms are processed by different units and must never be combined in one envelope.

Line 1: Enter the confirmation number printed on the acceptance notice for the declaration you are amending. An amendment without a confirmation number is returned unprocessed.

Line 2: Enter the vehicle identification exactly as it appeared on the original declaration, even if the registration card has since been reissued.

Line 6: Enter only the corrected figures. Lines left blank are treated as unchanged from the accepted declaration.

Where to file Form RV-7A

Amended declarations are opened at the annex, not at the Declarations Unit. Mail the signed amendment to:

Bureau of Civic Revenue, Amendments Section

PO Box 7 A, Substation Annex

Amendments delivered to the Declarations Unit address are re-routed by internal mail and are date-stamped on arrival at the annex, which may place an otherwise timely amendment outside the amendment window.

Do not use this form for an original declaration. See Form RV-7 Instructions for original filings.
