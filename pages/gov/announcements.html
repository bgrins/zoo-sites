BULLETIN 26-16: HOLIDAY CLOSURES. All Bureau offices, including the satellite offices, will be CLOSED on Monday, October 12; Wednesday, November 11; Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27; and Friday, December 25. The monthly records-processing closure on the first Monday of each month continues. A deadline that falls on a closure day moves to the next business day.

BULLETIN 26-14: REMINDER — FORM RV-9 COMMERCIAL VEHICLE DECLARATIONS ARE DUE JULY 15. Commercial filers should NOT use the residential RV-7 schedule.

BULLETIN 26-11: CD-1 EXTENSION REQUESTS (FORM CD-1X) postmarked by April 15 extend the general filing season to OCTOBER 15.

BULLETIN 26-09: RV-7A AMENDED DECLARATIONS for the prior year are accepted through JULY 31 without a reconsideration request.

BULLETIN 26-02: The Harborview satellite office has moved to 219 Pier Road, Suite 3. Hours unchanged.

BULLETIN 26-01: The Schedule of Filing Fees for the current declaration year is adopted, effective January 20. Expedited review of Combined Declarations remains available for an additional fee shown in the Schedule.
