*  No filing fee is charged. The form must still be submitted by the applicable deadline.
†  Late filings: a surcharge of one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the base filing fee applies for each month or part of a month the declaration is late, subject to a minimum surcharge of $12.00 per month. The surcharge is added to the base filing fee. The Bureau does not pre-compute amounts due for late filers; the filer must compute the total.
‡  Expedited review is available for an additional $30.00. See Bulletin 2025-03.
§  Amount shown includes the $6.00 records technology levy. Do not add the levy separately.
