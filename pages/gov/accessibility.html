The Bureau is committed to making its services available to all residents of the Commonwealth.

Documents in alternate formats. Any Bureau form, instruction booklet, or bulletin is available in large print or on audio cassette on request. Requests are filled by the Records and Disclosure Division within ten business days; call (555) 014-2200 or write to the Correspondence Unit (see Contact the Bureau).

Telephone access. A TTY line for callers using a text telephone is available at (555) 014-2210, Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

Building access. The Central Office public counter at 1 Assessment Plaza is served by a street-level entrance and elevators to the 4th floor. Satellite office accommodations vary; call ahead using the numbers listed under Office Locations.

Assistance completing a form. Counter staff at any office will read a form aloud and record a filer's responses on request. Staff may not provide advice on the amounts to be declared.

About the pages on this site. Pages on this site are published in standard HTML and are usable with text-only browsers. Where a document is offered only in Portable Document Format, a paper copy may be requested at no charge.
