General filing season for Combined Declarations (Forms CD-1, CD-1EZ, CD-2) opens January 20 and closes April 15.

NOTE: Vehicle declarations (the RV series) and business registrations (the SB series) follow their own deadlines. Consult the individual form pages via Forms & Publications for those dates. This page lists only the general season and the rules that apply to every Bureau deadline.

Extensions. A Combined Declaration filer who cannot file by April 15 may file Form CD-1X, Extension Request, postmarked by April 15. An accepted extension moves the filing deadline for that declaration to October 15. An extension of time to file is not an extension of time to pay: any amount due must still be remitted by April 15, with Form ES-40.

Weekends and holidays. A deadline that falls on a Saturday, a Sunday or a Bureau holiday moves to the next business day. Offices are also closed for records processing on the first Monday of every month, and a deadline that falls on that day moves in the same way.

Bureau holidays. The Bureau observes the legal holidays of the Commonwealth: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and Christmas Day. Closure dates for the current year are announced by numbered bulletin under Announcements.

Postmarks. A paper filing is timely if its postmark, or the date recorded by a private delivery service, falls on or before the deadline. Filings delivered by hand are date-stamped at the counter or on removal from the drop box.
