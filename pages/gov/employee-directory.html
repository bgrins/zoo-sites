The printed employee directory is no longer reproduced on this site. Under the records policy adopted by the Records and Disclosure Division, individual staff extensions and room assignments are not published.

Callers are directed to the listing appropriate to their inquiry:

  • Direct telephone lines for public service desks are published on the desk pages of the Department Directory.
  • General inquiries: (804) 555-0100. See Contact the Bureau.
  • Office window hours and locations: Office Locations.

A request for the name and title of the official responsible for a particular determination may be made in writing to the Records and Disclosure Division through the Correspondence Unit.
