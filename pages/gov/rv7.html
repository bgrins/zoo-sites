FORM RV-7: RESIDENTIAL VEHICLE ANNUAL DECLARATION
<< Home | All Forms

Form RV-7 must be filed each year by every resident who kept a registered vehicle in the Commonwealth for more than 180 days of the declaration year.

Filers should note that declarations submitted before the assessment cycle opens on May 1 cannot be processed. The annual filing deadline for Form RV-7 is June 12; if June 12 falls on a weekend or Bureau holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Late filings are subject to the penalty schedule in the instructions. Vehicle registration renewals are a separate process administered by the Registry and are due in your birth month; they are not affected by this declaration.

Downloads:

  • Form RV-7 Instructions (12 pages)

Do not use Form RV-9 (Commercial) for household vehicles.
