Send a parcel

Sending with Corvane takes four steps. Parcels enter the network either through a driver collection or over the counter at a drop point, and the tracking number is issued the moment we take the parcel.

  1. Check the item can travel. Review the prohibited items list before packing.
  2. Pack and weigh it. Follow the packaging guide; the charged weight is the greater of actual and volumetric weight.
  3. Price the journey. Look up the destination zone and price on the rates and zones card.
  4. Hand it over. Book a collection or take the parcel to a drop point and pay at the counter.

Choosing a service

Express 24 aims for the next working day by 18:00 and carries the higher compensation cover. Ground Economy moves on the 2-4 working day network and suits non-urgent parcels. Depot collect sends the parcel to a depot counter of the receiver's choice, where it is held for 7 days from arrival.
