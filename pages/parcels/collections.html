Book a collection

A driver collects from your door on the next round with space, usually the next working day. Collections booked before 20:00 join the following day's rounds; morning and afternoon windows are confirmed by the depot.

What the driver needs

  • Parcels packed and sealed to the packaging guide, each under 30 kg.
  • Labels attached if pre-paid; otherwise the driver labels and the fare is charged to the booking card.
  • Someone at the address through the confirmed window to hand the parcels over.

Good to know

  • Collections in the northern zone close 90 minutes early this week while the Marbeck sort hall is re-laid.
  • Business accounts get a standing daily collection window; see business accounts.
  • No one in when the driver calls? The round moves on and the booking rolls to the next working day.
