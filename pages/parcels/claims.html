Claims

Claims desk open 08:00-18:00

Claims are made by the sender, as the party who contracted with Corvane. Receivers should ask the sender to raise the claim and can pass photographs and packaging to them as evidence.

When a claim can open

Claim typeOpensCloses
DamageOn delivery14 days after delivery
Part lossOn delivery14 days after delivery
Loss10 working days after the last scan90 days after collection

How to claim

  1. Call the claims desk on 0808 157 0420 (option 3) or write to claims@corvane-parcel.example quoting your tracking number.
  2. The desk issues a claim reference and an evidence checklist within one working day.
  3. Send proof of value, photographs of the item and packaging, and the receiver's statement where relevant. Keep all packaging until the claim settles.
  4. Assessment normally completes within 10 working days of full evidence arriving; settlement is paid to the sender's original payment method.

Business accounts

Account holders raise claims through their named account handler instead of the public desk. Settled claims appear as credit lines on the next weekly invoice.
