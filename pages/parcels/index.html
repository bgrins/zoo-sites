Scan glossary

Network scanParcel read at a hub or depot belt
Depot holdParcel stopped at a depot pending action
Pre-adviceShipment registered, parcel not yet with us
Final scanLast scan on the parcel's journey

Scan times are local to the site that made the read. Depot holds are worked through in arrival order once the blocking reason is cleared by the sender or the receiver.
