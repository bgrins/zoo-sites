Rates and zones

Rate card 2026-2 · prices in GBP, ex VAT

Pricing is set by zone and by charged weight. The zone is fixed by the delivery postcode; the charged weight is the greater of the actual weight and the volumetric weight (length x width x height in cm, divided by 5,000).
