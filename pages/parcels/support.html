Common questions

  • Where is my parcel? Use the tracking page. A number that has just been issued may not resolve until the parcel is first scanned.
  • The parcel shows a depot hold. Holds are worked through in arrival order once the blocking reason is cleared by the sender or the receiver; the service team can tell you what is needed.
  • Delivery was missed. Redelivery can be booked once a parcel reaches its delivery depot. After 3 failed attempts the parcel is returned to sender.
  • Something arrived damaged or not at all. The sender should open a case with the claims desk.
  • How much will sending cost? See rates and zones, and the packaging guide for how charged weight is worked out.

Complaints

If the service team cannot resolve a problem, ask for it to be logged as a formal complaint. Complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days and answered by a duty manager within 10. Unresolved complaints can be escalated in writing to the registered office.
