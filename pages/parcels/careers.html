How to apply

Email a CV and a short note on the role you want to careers@corvane-parcel.example with the role title in the subject line. Depot roles include a paid trial shift; driver roles require a full licence held for at least two years. We reply to every application within five working days.

Working here

  • Paid overtime at 1.5x after contracted hours.
  • Free depot collect for personal parcels at any counter.
  • Depot-to-office moves are common; most planners started on a belt or a round.
