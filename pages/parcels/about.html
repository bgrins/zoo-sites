About Corvane

Corvane Parcel Network is a regional parcel carrier built around one idea: a small, dense network run well beats a sprawling one run thin. We carry parcels up to 30 kg through five depots and a single national sort hall at Marbeck.

The company started in 2009 as Corvane Couriers, a two-van same-day operation working out of a yard in Halston. The overnight network opened in 2014 when the Marbeck sort hall came online, and the current five-depot layout was completed with the Seddon Vale depot in 2021. Corvane remains privately held and management-owned.

How the network runs

Every parcel is scanned at each belt and each vehicle load, which is why tracking on this site reflects real network reads rather than estimates. Rounds are planned nightly at Marbeck from the day's collection manifests, and depot teams own their own service recovery: the depot that holds a parcel is the depot that clears it.
