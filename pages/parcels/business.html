Open a business account

From 20 parcels a week

A Corvane business account replaces pay-as-you-go sending with contracted rates, a daily driver collection from your premises and one weekly invoice. Accounts are opened by our sales team, not online.

How to apply

Email sales@corvane-parcel.example with your company name, registered number, collection postcode and an estimate of weekly parcel volume. The sales team replies within two working days with a rate card and a draft agreement. Accounts go live once the agreement is signed and a direct debit is in place.

What is included

  • A scheduled daily collection window agreed with your local depot.
  • Label printing from your account, with addresses validated at entry.
  • Named account handler at the Marbeck sort hall for service queries.
  • Claims handled through your handler rather than the public claims desk.

Business pricing

Contracted rates are set against your weekly volume band and reviewed twice a year. Bands below are indicative discounts from the public rates and zones card; your rate card is confirmed in the account agreement.

Invoices

Business accounts are invoiced weekly in arrears. Each invoice consolidates every consignment collected in the invoice week, itemised by service, zone and weight band, with adjustments and claim credits shown as separate lines.

  • Invoices are issued each Monday and settle by direct debit 14 days later.
  • PDF and CSV copies are available after signing in.
  • Invoice queries go to your account handler within 30 days of issue.

API access

Account holders can integrate their warehouse or storefront systems directly with the Corvane network. The API covers consignment creation, label retrieval, collection manifests and status webhooks, authenticated with per-account keys issued by your handler.

  • Keys are scoped to your account and can be rotated from the account area.
  • Webhooks push scan events to your endpoint as they reach the network core.
  • Integration documentation is provided with your welcome pack.

To request API access on an existing account, email sales@corvane-parcel.example quoting your account number.
