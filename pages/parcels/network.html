Network map

Hub and spoke, one sort hall

The Corvane network is a hub-and-spoke layout. Every overnight parcel moves through the Marbeck sort hall; five delivery depots run the local rounds and feed collections back to Marbeck on evening trunks.

How a parcel moves

  1. Collected on a local round or dropped at a counter, and scanned into the origin depot.
  2. Trunked to Marbeck on the evening departure and sorted overnight to the destination depot.
  3. Trunked out before 05:00 and scanned onto a delivery round, or onto the depot collect shelf.

Each depot runs its own delivery rounds, named for the district they start in: Denhollow and Sallow Cross run out of Halston depot, and the Marbeck corridor rounds run from the sort hall itself.

Island postcodes in Zone 4 add a sailing from the Seddon Vale gateway, which is why their delivery standard runs a day longer. Trunk schedules are planned so that a parcel collected by 17:00 in any zone reaches Marbeck the same night.

Counter addresses and opening hours are listed on the drop points page. Current service disruptions are posted on the tracking page service advisory panel.
