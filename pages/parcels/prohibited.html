Prohibited and restricted items

Prohibited items may not travel on the Corvane network under any circumstances. Restricted items may travel only under the stated conditions. Counters refuse listed goods, and parcels found to contain them in the network are stopped at the nearest depot.

Prohibited

  • Explosives, fireworks, flares and ammunition
  • Flammable liquids and gases, including lighter refills and aerosol paint
  • Corrosives, oxidisers and toxic substances
  • Firearms and firearm parts
  • Illegal drugs and controlled substances
  • Living creatures of any kind
  • Loose lithium batteries and damaged battery-powered devices
  • Cash, bullion and bearer instruments
  • Human or animal remains

Restricted

ItemCondition of carriage
AlcoholSealed retail packaging, under 24% alcohol by volume, sender and receiver over 18
Perfume and aftershaveRetail packaging, maximum 500 ml per parcel, Ground Economy only
Lithium batteries installed in a deviceDevice switched off and protected against activation
Sharp tools and bladesCutting edges sheathed and boxed, receiver over 18
Perishable foodExpress 24 only, packed to survive 48 hours in transit
Glass and ceramicsPacked to the packaging guide; cover limited unless double-boxed

Sending a prohibited item breaches the terms of carriage: cover is void, disposal or return costs are charged to the sender, and dangerous goods are reported to the relevant authority. Unsure about an item? Ask the service team via the support page before handing it over.
