Privacy notice

Last revised 12 May 2026

This notice explains what personal data Corvane Parcel Network Ltd collects when you send, receive or track a parcel or use this site, and what we do with it. The data controller is Corvane Parcel Network Ltd, Unit 9, Marbeck Distribution Park, Halston HL4 2QR.

What we collect

  • Consignment data: sender and receiver names, addresses, contact details, and the scan history of each parcel.
  • Account data: sign-in email, billing details and invoice history for account holders.
  • Service records: calls, emails and claims correspondence with the service team.
  • Site data: server logs of page requests, and the preferences described in cookie settings.

Why we use it

  • To perform the contract of carriage: moving, delivering and returning parcels, and answering tracking queries.
  • To meet legal obligations, including retention of consignment records and reporting of dangerous goods.
  • For legitimate interests: securing the network, preventing fraud and planning depot capacity.

Sharing and retention

Consignment data is shared with partner counters only as needed to hand over a parcel, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data. Consignment records are kept for 15 months from the last scan; account and invoice records for 7 years; service recordings for 6 months.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion where we no longer need it. Write to privacy@corvane-parcel.example or to the registered office marked for the data protection lead. We answer within one calendar month, and you may complain to the supervisory authority if you are unhappy with the outcome.
