Terms of carriage

Edition 2026-1, in force from 1 Mar 2026

These terms govern every consignment handed to Corvane Parcel Network Ltd. By handing over a parcel, the sender accepts them on their own behalf and on behalf of anyone with an interest in the contents.

1. The contract

The contract of carriage is between Corvane and the sender. It begins when a parcel is scanned into the network and ends on delivery, on collection from a depot counter, or on return to the sender. Receivers acquire no rights under the contract except where the law provides otherwise.

2. Acceptance

We may refuse or stop any parcel that exceeds 30 kg or 150 cm on the longest side, is packed contrary to the packaging guide, or contains anything on the prohibited and restricted list. The sender warrants the accuracy of the declared contents, weight and addresses.

3. Delivery

Delivery is made to the address on the label, to a neighbour or safe place where the sender permitted it, or to a depot counter for depot collect services. Delivery standards published on the rates page are targets, not guarantees, unless a guaranteed service is expressly bought. After 3 failed delivery attempts the parcel is returned to the sender at the applicable return rate.

4. Charges

Charges follow the rate card in force at handover, based on zone and charged weight. Underdeclared parcels are re-weighed and re-cubed in the network and the difference, plus the address correction or oversize surcharges where they apply, is charged to the sender.

5. Liability

Our liability for loss or damage is limited to the included cover for the service used, or the extended cover bought at handover, and never exceeds the lower of the repair cost, the replacement cost and the declared value. We are not liable for indirect loss, loss of profit, or delay on non-guaranteed services. Claims must follow the process and time limits on the claims page.

6. Exclusions

No cover applies to prohibited items, to restricted items carried outside their stated conditions, to fragile goods packed outside the packaging guide, or to inherent defects in the goods themselves.

7. Undeliverable parcels

Parcels that cannot be delivered or returned are held for 90 days from the last scan. After that we may open them to identify an owner and, failing that, dispose of or donate the contents.

8. General

These terms are governed by the law of the registered office's jurisdiction. If any clause is found invalid the remainder stands. Notices to Corvane go to the registered office: Unit 9, Marbeck Distribution Park, Halston HL4 2QR.
