Drop points

6 depot counters · 241 partner sites

Drop points take parcels into the network without a collection booking. Depot counters weigh, price and label the parcel for you; partner counters and lockers accept parcels that already carry a Corvane label.

Partner counters and lockers

Newsagents, convenience stores and forecourt lockers across all four zones accept pre-labelled parcels up to 15 kg and 60 cm on the longest side. Larger or unlabelled parcels must go to a depot counter or a booked collection. Partner sites hand parcels to a Corvane driver on the next scheduled sweep, usually within one working day.

Depot collect parcels are held at the chosen counter for 7 days from arrival, then returned to sender. Bring photo identification matching the receiver name on the label.

Before you go

  • Check the prohibited items list; counters refuse restricted goods.
  • Card payment only at depot counters; partner sites take labelled parcels at no charge.
  • Collections in the northern zone close 90 minutes early while the Marbeck sort hall floor is re-laid.
