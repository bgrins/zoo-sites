The service

What Halbeck provides
Hosted analytics over the data your connectors send: ingestion, storage, dashboards, exports and alerting, targeted at 99.9% monthly availability outside announced maintenance.
Your data
Connected data remains yours. We process it only to provide the service and delete it on the schedule in your data processing agreement when the workspace closes.

Obligations

Acceptable use
Workspaces may only analyse data the customer is entitled to process. Reselling access, probing other tenants, or sending data you lack a lawful basis for are grounds for suspension.
Accounts

Termination
Either party may terminate at the end of the committed term with thirty days notice; exports remain available for thirty days after closure.
