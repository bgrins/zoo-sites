Support

The Scale plan includes support from the analytics engineering team on business days, 09:00-17:30 UK time, with a first response inside one business day.

Before you write

Data looks wrong
Check Data sources first: a held-back connector shows as a gap, not a zero. Then compare the panel's table view with its CSV export; they should match to the seat.
Panel will not load
Check the status page for platform incidents before reporting.
Access problems
Access is granted through your directory group; a workspace admin (see Members) can confirm the mapping.
