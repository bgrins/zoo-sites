Docs

The short guide to reading a Halbeck workspace. Product-wide documentation; nothing here is specific to Northgate Media.

Reading a dashboard

Views
Every series panel offers a chart and a table of the same data; the toggle is in the panel header. The table is the citable form — figures in it match exports exactly.
Exports
Export CSV in a panel header downloads the series behind that panel. Scheduled exports are configured per saved view.
Freshness
A panel whose source has not landed a batch inside its window is held back rather than shown stale; see Data sources for connector health.

Conventions

Closed periods
Monthly figures settle three days after month end and are never restated. Late-arriving events land in the period they were received.
Timezone
All periods close in the workspace timezone, set by an admin.
Deduplication
Member-based metrics count a person once across all connected workspaces.
