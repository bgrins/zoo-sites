Halbeck / Privacy notice

Privacy notice

Last updated 12 February 2026. This notice covers people who use the Halbeck console and people whose activity appears in a customer's connected data.

Console users

What we hold
Name, work email, role, and an access log of sign-ins and exports kept for two years. We use it to run the service and for nothing else.
Where it lives
In the region your organisation selected, with encrypted backups in the same jurisdiction.

Analysed data

Controller
Your organisation controls the data its connectors send; Halbeck processes it under the data processing agreement and never uses it for its own purposes.
Retention
Raw events follow the retention set per source (see Data sources); aggregates persist until the workspace is deleted.
Requests
Subject access or deletion requests about analysed data go to the controlling organisation; requests about console accounts go to privacy@halbeck.example and complete within thirty days.
