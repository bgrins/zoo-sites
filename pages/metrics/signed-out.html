Signing out of Halbeck

Skelvane Media signs in to Halbeck through Ondrexa SSO, so a Halbeck session lasts as long as the Ondrexa session behind it. Scheduled exports and alert emails carry on whether or not you are signed in.

Ending your session

Your own computer
Sign out from the Ondrexa portal. Halbeck asks you to sign in again the next time you open the workspace.
Shared computer
Sign out of Ondrexa, then close every browser window, so neither session outlives your visit.
Staying signed in
Return to Seat usage.
Trouble signing in
Access comes from your directory group; a workspace admin can check the mapping, or contact support.
