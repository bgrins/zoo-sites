Putaway

Receipt licences waiting to be moved from the Bay A marshalling lanes into storage locations.

The putaway queue is clear. Every receipt licence released by receiving this week has been put to location and confirmed by scan.

Routing rules

  • Heavy and palletised goods go to ground-level bulk locations in Bays B and C.
  • Fast-moving lines are directed to pick faces in Bays D to F, nearest the despatch lanes.
  • Slow movers and overstock go to upper rack levels in Bay G.
  • A move is complete only when the destination location has been scanned; unconfirmed moves block the licence and appear on the shift handover report.

Suggested locations come from the bin map; deviations must be scanned to the actual location so records stay true.
