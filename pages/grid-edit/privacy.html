Privacy notice

How Binthral handles personal information about the people who use it. Last reviewed January 2026.

What Binthral records about you

  • Your operator sign-in, the terminal used, and session times.
  • Actions taken under your sign-in: receipts, moves, count adjustments, and despatch confirmations, each with a timestamp.
  • Your name and role as held in the depot staff roster.

Why it is recorded

Stock records are ledger entries. Attributing each entry to an operator is how the depot evidences its counts to auditors and investigates discrepancies. This processing is carried out under the company's legitimate interest in accurate stock control.

Retention and access

Action logs are kept for six years in line with the audit retention schedule, then deleted. You may ask the stock desk for a copy of the log entries attributed to your sign-in.

Who to contact

Privacy questions go to the data protection lead at head office, via the stock desk on 0161 496 0142 or through your shift manager.
