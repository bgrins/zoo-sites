Terms of use

Conditions for using Binthral at any Brantmoor Depot site. Last reviewed January 2026.

Authorised use

Binthral is provided for the company's warehouse operations only. Access is limited to staff and approved contractors with a personal sign-in issued by the stock desk.

Your sign-in

  • Do not share your sign-in or leave a terminal signed in unattended.
  • Every entry made under your sign-in is attributed to you in the ledger.
  • Report a suspected compromised sign-in to the stock desk immediately.

Stock records

Records in Binthral are the company's books of account for stock. Deliberately entering a figure you know to be wrong, or posting a count you did not perform, is treated as a disciplinary matter under the company handbook.

Availability

The system is maintained overnight and may be briefly unavailable during the maintenance window. Paper fallback sheets for counts are held at the stock desk.
