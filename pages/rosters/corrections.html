Corrections and enquiries

The Office of the Registrar answers questions about the staff register and takes in corrections to it. A published roster is not amended after the fact; a correction is entered in the personnel system of record and appears in the next roster, so the sooner a matter reaches the Registrar the sooner it is put right in print.

Raising a correction

Write to registrar@ferncliff-institute.example. Say which programme year's roster the entry appears in, give the entry as it is printed, and give the wording it should carry instead. Where the correction concerns somebody else's entry, say how you come to know of it; the Registrar checks every correction against the establishment record and with the head of the department concerned before entering it, and an unattributed report takes longer.

Corrections received before the first of August are carried into the roster published that September. Anything arriving later is held for the year after, since the extract is closed and read against the establishment record through August.

Matters the Registrar cannot settle

Three come up often enough to set out here. A change of working title is a matter for the head of department, who notifies Data Services; the Registrar records the change but cannot make it. An extension is allocated by the field station switchboard and changes whenever somebody moves bench or office, so a wrong extension is corrected by telephoning the switchboard rather than by writing to the Registrar. A visiting appointment is not an entry in the register at all and cannot be added to one, however long the visit ran; the Grants Officer records it in the annual report instead.

Names

The register prints the name a colleague asks the Institute to use. A change of name is entered on request, without documentation and without explanation, and takes effect in the next roster; earlier rosters keep the earlier name, since they are a record of a particular morning. Several colleagues here share a surname, so please quote a full name in any correspondence and add the department if you know it.
