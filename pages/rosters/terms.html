Terms of use

These terms cover the use of the Institute's website and of the material published on it, including the staff rosters, the register notes, and the Technical Notes series.

Citing published material

Rosters and Technical Notes may be quoted and cited freely for research and correspondence, with the citation given at the foot of each page. They may not be republished in full, or combined into a directory of staff, without the written permission of the Registrar.

Accuracy

A roster records appointments as they stood on the day it was compiled. It is not a current directory, and the Institute does not undertake to keep it so; for the current extension of a colleague, telephone the switchboard.

Contact details

Email addresses and extensions published here are for Institute business. Please do not add them to mailing lists.

Changes

The Registrar may revise these terms at the start of any programme year. The current version is always the one on this page.
