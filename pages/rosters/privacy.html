Privacy notice

This notice explains how the Ferncliff Institute for Applied Systems handles personal information about its staff, visiting scholars, and people who write to it. The Institute is the data controller; questions go to the Office of the Registrar at registrar@ferncliff-institute.example.

Staff rosters

Each published roster carries a colleague's name, working title, department and internal extension, as held on the day of compilation. The Institute publishes these because callers and partner institutions need to reach the right person. Rosters are a record of a particular day and are not amended afterwards; a change of name or title appears in the following year's roster, as set out under corrections and enquiries.

A colleague who would prefer not to appear on a published roster should tell the Registrar before the first of August. The entry is then held in the personnel system of record but left out of the published extract.

Enquiries and correspondence

Letters and email to the Institute are kept for as long as the matter is open and for two years after, and are then destroyed unless they form part of the archive under the records schedule.

This website

The website sets a single session cookie and keeps no record of individual visitors beyond the server's routine access log, which is cleared after thirty days.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the information the Institute holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or object to its use. Write to the Registrar; requests are answered within one month.
