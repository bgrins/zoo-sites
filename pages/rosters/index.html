About the Institute

The Ferncliff Institute for Applied Systems operates a small coastal field station, a materials laboratory, and an optics bench shared with partner institutions. Work at the Institute is organised in six departments: Administration, Applied Optics, Data Services, Field Operations, Materials Lab, and Public Programs.

The Institute is supported by programme grants and by the Ferncliff Trust. Our facilities are open to visiting researchers by arrangement; see the visiting scholars section below.

Staff rosters

The Institute publishes a staff roster once a year, at the start of the programme year in September. Each roster lists everyone holding an appointment on the day it was compiled, together with their working title, their department, and their internal extension. Rosters are published as-is and are not revised after the fact; corrections are carried forward into the next year's roster instead.

  • Staff roster, programme year 2025 — compiled 1 September 2025, arranged by department.
  • Staff roster, programme year 2026 — compiled 1 September 2026, arranged alphabetically.

Rosters for programme years before 2025 are held in the Institute archive and are available from the Archivist on request. Extensions are internal to the field station switchboard; dial the main number and then the extension.

Research programmes

Coastal instrumentation

Long-running deployment of tide, turbidity, and salinity instrument strings along the Ferncliff reach, with a rolling calibration schedule maintained by the Applied Optics and Field Operations departments.

Materials in a marine environment

Bench and immersion testing of fastenings, housings, and coatings used on the instrument strings. The Materials Lab keeps a specimen library going back to the founding of the station.

Data stewardship

Data Services curates the instrument archive, publishes quarterly data releases, and maintains the personnel system of record from which the staff rosters above are drawn.

Visiting scholars

The Institute hosts two or three visiting scholars each programme year. Visiting appointments are arranged through the Grants Officer and are not listed on the staff roster; visitors appear instead in the annual report as guests of the hosting department.

A note on names. Several colleagues at the Institute share a surname. Please quote the full name and the extension when you forward internal correspondence.
