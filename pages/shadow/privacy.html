PN-01 / PRIVACY AND MONITORING
What this console records about the people who use it, who sees it, and for how long. Metronome Systems Ltd is the controller for everything on this sheet.

What is recorded

Each clearance entry, granted or rejected, is recorded with the console identifier, the time and the shift line that keyed it. Door holds, lamp states and self-test results are recorded against the plant, not against a person. The console records the code you key only as granted or rejected; it never stores the code itself.

Why

To keep an access record for the stage that site engineering and the facilities duty manager can audit, and to investigate a fault or an incident. Records are not used to measure how fast or how often an operator works.

Who sees it, and for how long

The operations desk and the duty engineer see the on-screen event log for the current shift. The weekly extract goes to the access records office and is held for two years, then deleted. An extract that names another operator's entries needs the facilities duty manager's authority.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the entries recorded against your shift lines, and ask for a wrong entry to be corrected. Write to access.records@metronome-systems.example; the office answers within one month. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the data protection regulator.
