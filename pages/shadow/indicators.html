RC-04 / WHAT THE CONSOLE IS TELLING YOU
Lamp meanings and event log tags for the clearance module. Print side is laminated and lives on the console frame; this copy is the one that gets corrected.

Lamp strip

The strip under the title bar reads left to right in the order the plant is wired, not in order of severity. A dark lamp is a real state, not a fault: the module drives every lamp on self-test, so a lamp that never lights during a shift is worth a call.

bus 24v
Green. Control bus supply is present and within tolerance. Amber means the supply is running from the standby rectifier; the console still works, and the plant room wants to know. Dark means no bus, and nothing on this console is live.
interlock
Green. The interlock chain is proved and a clearance may be keyed. Dark means a link in the chain is open, most often a door leaf; the keypad will take a code and then refuse it.
stage two armed
Amber. Stage two is armed and waiting on a clearance entry. Green means clearance is already in force for the current shift. Dark means the stage is down for maintenance and no entry will be accepted.
door held
Red. The numbered door has a leaf open past its dwell time. The lamp clears itself when the leaf closes. Held doors are logged, and a door held for more than five minutes raises a call to the operations desk without an operator doing anything.
maint bypass
Dark in normal running. Amber means a duty engineer has bypassed part of the chain under a permit. Nothing may be keyed while bypass is up, and the permit number belongs on the shift sheet.

Event log tags

shift rotation
The operations desk has reissued clearance codes. Anything issued before this entry has rolled off.
selftest
The module exercised its lamps, its keypad and the interlock chain. One is expected per shift, shortly after rotation.
door
A door state change worth recording, with the numbered door and the duration it was held, and the closure that ended a hold.
stage two
The stage armed, cleared or dropped. A drop always carries a reason.
keypad
A rejected clearance entry, with the number of rejections so far this shift. Granted clearances go to the site access record, not to this log.
duty engineer
The console paged the duty engineer after a third rejection in the shift.
