TU-03 / TERMS OF USE FOR THIS TERMINAL
The conditions every operator accepts by keying at FAC-CONSOLE / 07. They sit alongside the standing procedures, and a procedure sheet wins where the two differ.

Who may use the console

Only operators named on the current shift sheet, and duty engineers under a permit. A visitor never keys, even under escort, and an operator never keys on behalf of someone who is not on the sheet.

Codes

Stage codes are issued by the operations desk for one shift and belong to the site. They are not shared, written down or sent by message, and a code that has rolled off is never reused. Keying variations of a code to see what the keypad accepts is a breach of these terms, whatever the outcome.

Faults and incidents

Report anything the console does that the indicator reference does not explain. Do not open the panel, bypass a lamp or clear a log; those are duty engineer tasks under a permit. The escalation sheet says who to call and in what order.

Monitoring

Use of the console is recorded as described in the privacy and monitoring notice. Records may be used in an investigation and, where the investigation finds a breach of these terms, in a disciplinary process.
