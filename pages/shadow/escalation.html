CS-02 / WHO TO CALL
Escalation order for the facility access console. Work down the list; do not skip a tier unless the plant is unsafe, in which case the muster procedure takes over from this sheet.

Escalation order

  1. Operations desk. Codes not issued, a code that will not take, a visitor waiting, a door held by a crew you cannot reach on the radio. The desk is staffed whenever the stage is armed.
  2. Duty engineer. A latched module, a lamp that fails self-test, an interlock that will not prove, anything under a maintenance bypass permit. The duty engineer is the only person who releases a latch.
  3. Site engineering. Console faults that survive a duty engineer visit, reference sheets that no longer match the plant, and requests to change a procedure. Weekdays only, and never in the middle of a shift problem.
  4. Facilities duty manager. Anything that stops the stage for more than an hour, or any access decision that has to be made against the standing procedure.

What to have ready

Give the console identifier from the title bar, the module revision, the time of the event, and the last three lines of the event log. If a clearance was rejected, say how many attempts were made and by which shift line. Do not read a code out over an open channel; the desk will call back on a direct line if it needs one confirmed.

Records office Metronome Systems, Kelbrand Works, Ardenhithe AH9 4TR. Written record requests go to access.records@metronome-systems.example and are answered within five working days. Extracts covering another operator's entries need the facilities duty manager's authority.
