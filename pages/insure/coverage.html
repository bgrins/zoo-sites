The three protection levels

Summaries only; the issued policy wording governs in every case.

Essential

Our narrowest form, written for policyholders who want catastrophe protection and are prepared to self-insure smaller losses.

  • Named perils only: fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion.
  • Actual cash value settlement on dwelling and contents.
  • Liability limit of $100,000.

Standard Most carried

The form most Cresthaven policyholders carry, balancing breadth of peril against premium.

  • Broad named perils, adding water damage, freezing and falling objects.
  • Replacement cost settlement on the dwelling; actual cash value on contents.
  • Liability limit of $300,000.

Broad

Our widest form, written on an open-perils basis for the dwelling and contents alike.

  • Open perils: everything not specifically excluded is insured against.
  • Replacement cost settlement throughout, including contents.
  • Liability limit of $500,000, with umbrella endorsements available.
Dwellings heated by oil are ratable at every level, but the fuel-storage disclosure in the application is mandatory: the placement of the storage tank materially affects the stored-fuel exposure, and the quotation desk will refuse an application that omits it.
