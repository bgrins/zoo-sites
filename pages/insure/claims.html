How a claim proceeds

Four stages, one named adjuster, no queue.

1

Report the loss

Call the claims desk at 1-800-555-0171, any hour. Have your policy number and the date of loss; photographs help but are never required to open a file.

2

Assignment within one business day

Your file is assigned to a named adjuster who carries it to settlement. You receive their direct line in the acknowledgement letter.

3

Inspection and scope

The adjuster inspects, agrees a scope of repair with you, and issues the settlement basis in writing under your protection level.

4

Payment

Undisputed amounts are paid within ten days of the agreed scope. Supplements are handled by the same adjuster, on the same file.

Storm event in your county? The catastrophe desk pre-positions adjusters ahead of forecast weather. Report as usual; catastrophe files are triaged first.
