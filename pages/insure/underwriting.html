How we underwrite

What the department looks at, in what order, and what it does when the answer is no.

The application is the record

Form HQ-1 asks a short set of questions because each one moves loss experience. Dwelling type sets the exposure to a neighbor's fire. The heating plant sets the ignition and freeze exposures. Where the plant burns oil, the placement of the storage tank sets the stored-fuel exposure, and the disclosure is mandatory rather than optional.

The quotation desk rates the application exactly as recorded. An answer given loosely is not corrected in the customer's favor later, so read the review page before requesting the quotation.

What the desk does not ask

Cresthaven does not rate on marketing segments, and the application does not ask for them.

  • We do not rate on credit-based insurance scores.
  • We do not rate on prior inquiries, only on prior paid losses.
  • We do not rate on the ZIP code alone, but on the exposures at the address, which the underwriter confirms at binding: distance to a responding fire company, and the fire-flow class of the hydrant serving it.

Inspection

An accepted quotation that proceeds to binding is followed by an exterior inspection within forty-five days: roof surface and age, foundation and grade, outbuildings, and the condition of any outdoor fuel tank. Most policyholders never meet the inspector, because most inspections need nothing from the occupant.

Where an inspection finds a condition the application did not disclose, the underwriter writes to the policyholder with the finding, the remedy and a date. A policy is not canceled for a condition that is being corrected on schedule.

Declination and referral

Some risks the mutual declines: a dwelling that is not owner-occupied, a dwelling under substantial renovation, a knob-and-tube electrical service that has not been replaced, or an unremediated underground tank that has failed a tightness test. A declination is issued in writing, states the reason, and names the underwriter who signed it.

A declination is not a dead end. Ask the underwriting department for a referral: the mutual maintains standing arrangements with specialty carriers for dwellings we cannot write ourselves, and the referral costs nothing.

A quotation is held for thirty days from issue. If a fact changes inside that window — a new roof, a tank removed, a furnace converted from oil to gas — telephone the underwriting department rather than starting a second application. Two open applications on one address are reconciled by hand and slow the binding.
