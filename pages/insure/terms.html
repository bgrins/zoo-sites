Terms of use

The conditions on which the mutual offers this website and its online quotation desk.

What the website is for

This website describes the coverage Cresthaven Mutual writes, explains how claims and complaints are handled, and lets a prospective policyholder prepare an application on Form HQ-1 and request a quotation. It does not bind coverage. No policy exists, and no loss is insured, until the mutual has issued a written declarations page and the first premium has been received.

Quotations

A quotation is an offer to write the dwelling described in the application, at the premium shown, for thirty days from issue. It is made on the answers you give. If an answer is inaccurate or incomplete, or if the exterior inspection that follows binding finds a condition the application did not disclose, the mutual may re-rate the policy or decline to issue it, as set out on the underwriting page.

Figures shown anywhere on this website other than on an issued quotation, including introductory figures on the home page, are illustrations and not rates.

Your use of the website

  • Answer the application for a dwelling you own or are buying, and answer it truthfully.
  • Do not submit applications on another person’s behalf without their authority.
  • Do not attempt to probe, overload or work around the quotation desk. Requests that do so are refused and logged.

Content and liability

The coverage summaries on this website are summaries. The issued policy form and its endorsements govern what is insured, and where a summary and the policy differ, the policy controls. The mutual takes care to keep this website accurate and available, but it is not liable for loss arising from an interruption of the website or from reliance on a summary in place of the policy.

Text, the Cresthaven name and the crest are the property of the mutual and may not be reproduced to suggest an endorsement.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of the State of Nebraska, where the mutual is domiciled. A dispute about a policy is resolved under that policy’s own appraisal and appeal provisions, described on the complaints and appeals page.
