Join the club

Join online and your card number is ready straight away, or ask at any till or the service desk and leave with a card in hand. Member pricing on over 400 lines starts with your very next shop, at any of our 34 stores.

Points and vouchers

You earn points on every shop and on most services at the desk. Points post to your account within 48 hours and convert into money-off vouchers four times a year. Vouchers can be spent at any till or saved for seasonal events; they have no cash value, and gift card purchases do not earn points.

Replace a card

Lost or damaged card? Bring proof of your name to any service desk and we will print a replacement on the spot. Your points and vouchers carry over automatically, and the old card stops working the moment the new one is issued.

Club rules

One membership per person, aged 16 or over. Member pricing applies only when your card or app is scanned before payment. Points have no cash value and may be corrected where a transaction is refunded. Larkfield Market Ltd may amend these rules with 30 days notice on this page and at the service desk.
