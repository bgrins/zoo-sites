From one stall to 34 stores

Larkfield Market started in 1987 as a single produce stall outside the old corn exchange on Mill Street.

Founders Edith and Ray Larkin had one idea: sell honest food at prices worth shouting about, and shout about them. The first shop opened on Mill Street in 1991, and that site is still our flagship store and registered office today.

Thirty-nine years on, Larkfield Market Ltd runs 34 stores, each with its own bakery and butcher counter, and every one of them prices the same way: one weekly ad, one promotion board, one members club. No flagship-only deals, no small print that changes by postcode.

Still family owned. The Larkin family holds the company outright, and every store manager started on the shop floor.

Careers

We hire the same way we sell: straightforwardly. Most roles are store roles, including till and service desk, bakery and butcher counters, warehouse and collection desk, and early-morning replenishment. Store management is filled from within wherever possible.

To apply, ask for a paper application at the service desk of any store. The duty manager interviews weekly, and most applicants hear back within seven days.

Supplier terms

We buy direct from growers, bakers, and makers wherever we can, and we pay invoices within 30 days without exception. Standard terms in brief: goods are inspected at our Netherford depot, prices are agreed per season rather than per week, and promotional funding is always agreed in writing before an item enters the weekly ad.

Prospective suppliers can write to the buying office at our registered office address or email supply@larkfieldmarket.example with a one-page summary and price list.
