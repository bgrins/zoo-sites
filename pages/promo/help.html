Order and collect

Phone your store before noon and your order is picked the same day. Collect from the collection desk any time before it closes, one hour before the store. Pickup is free on orders over £40 and costs £2.50 below that.

Bring your member card when you collect so points and member pricing apply. Orders not collected within two days are returned to shelf and refunded in full.

Gift cards

Gift cards are sold at every till in values from £5 to £150 and never expire. They can be spent in any of our 34 stores, topped up at the service desk, and checked for balance at any till.

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, do not earn member points, and are excluded from promotion board offers.

Returns and refunds

Not happy with something? Bring it back with your receipt within 30 days for a full refund at any store, no questions asked. Fresh items can be returned without a receipt on the day of purchase.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Where a refunded shop earned member points, the points are removed when the refund is processed.

Calls to 0808 numbers are free from landlines and mobiles. This page covers store purchases only; members club questions are answered on the members club page.
