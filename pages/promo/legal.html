Notices and policies

These notices apply to Larkfield Market Ltd and all 34 of its stores. Printed copies are available at any service desk.

Privacy notice

We collect only what we need to run your membership and your orders: your name, contact details, and purchase history linked to your member card. We never sell personal data. To see or delete what we hold, write to the help desk at our registered office.

Terms of sale

Prices at the shelf edge are the prices you pay; where a shelf price and a till price disagree, the lower applies. Member prices require a card or app scan before payment. Promotional items are limited to reasonable household quantities and sold while stocks last.

Cookie choices

This site sets only the cookies needed for sign-in and for remembering your chosen store. We use no advertising or third-party tracking cookies, so there is nothing further to opt out of.

Accessibility

We aim for every page to work with keyboards and screen readers, and every store has step-free access, hearing loops at the service desk, and assisted shopping on request. Tell the help desk if anything falls short and we will fix it.

Modern slavery statement

Larkfield Market Ltd checks every direct supplier against its labour standards before trading and re-checks annually. Our full statement, approved by the board each year, is available in print from the buying office at our registered office.
