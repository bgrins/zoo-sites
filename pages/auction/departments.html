Auctions / Departments

Departments

Six departments share the Cheverell Wharf rostrum, each holding three or four sales a year. Entries close six weeks before each sale.

Instruments, marine and scientific

Chronometers, barometers, sextants, microscopes, globes and the tools of navigation and measurement. The department's April sale is the largest of its kind outside London.

Clocks and horology

Longcase, bracket and regulator clocks, watches and horological literature. Movements are described from inspection, not from the dial.

Furniture and works of art

Town and country furniture from the seventeenth century onward, treen, metalware and sculpture.

Pictures and prints

Oil paintings, watercolours and printed images, with a standing interest in marine and coastal subjects.

Books and manuscripts

Printed books, charts, maps, log books and archives. Collections are catalogued on the premises rather than dispersed unseen.

Silver and jewellery

Domestic silver, flatware by weight, and jewellery sold with plain condition notes.

Specialists

Rosalind Pethick leads the rostrum and the instruments department. Hartley Combe takes clocks and horology; Edda Voss takes pictures and books; Simon Quenell takes furniture, silver and valuations for probate. All four see items without appointment on Tuesday mornings, and by arrangement otherwise through the bidding desk.
