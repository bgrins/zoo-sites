Auctions / Valuations

Valuations

We value single items and whole houses, for sale, for probate and for insurance. An auction estimate costs nothing and does not commit you to selling.

Free sale estimates

Bring an item to the wharf on a Tuesday morning, or send photographs with the measurements to bids@marlstonesalerooms.example. A specialist replies within five working days with an estimate and the date of the next suitable sale. For anything too large to carry, we visit; there is no charge within twenty miles of Marlstone.

Probate and insurance

Written valuations for probate are prepared to the standard executors and the revenue require, itemised room by room, and charged at 90.00 an hour of the specialist's time with a written quote before we start. Insurance valuations are prepared on the same terms at replacement value. Where a probate valuation leads to a sale with us within a year, the valuation fee is credited against vendor commission.

House clearances

After a valuation we can clear a property entirely: saleable contents come to the rooms, the remainder goes responsibly, and the house is left swept. Clearance is quoted as a fixed figure alongside the valuation.
