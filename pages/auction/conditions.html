Conditions of sale

These conditions govern every lot offered by Marlstone Salerooms Limited. By registering a paddle, in the room or online, a bidder accepts them in full.

Charges to the buyer

Buyer's premium
22% of the hammer price
Taxes
Included in the premium
Online bidding fee
None
Late storage
6.00 a day after ten days

All figures quoted on this site, in the catalogue and on the rostrum are in pounds sterling.

The premium is the only charge added to the hammer price on an invoice for a lot collected from the saleroom. Shipping, if arranged through our packing office, is quoted and invoiced separately.

Increments

The auctioneer advances the bidding in fixed steps. A bid is accepted only at the amount standing as the next bid; the saleroom does not take amounts that fall between steps, and it does not take an advance of more than one step at a time.

  • Up to 500 — steps of 25
  • 500 to 1,000 — steps of 50
  • 1,000 to 5,000 — steps of 100
  • Above 5,000 — steps of 250

Bidding online

Online bids are placed against the room in real time. The figure shown on a lot page is the bid standing with the auctioneer at that moment, and it moves whenever the room advances. A bid submitted at a figure the room has already passed is declined, and the current figure is returned with the refusal so that the bidder may come again.

When no further advance comes from the room the auctioneer gives the lot fair warning before the hammer. Online bidders may come in at any time up to the hammer; once the hammer has fallen no further bid is taken and the sale is final.

A successful online bidder is issued a paddle code with the hammer. The paddle code identifies the buyer on the invoice and must be quoted when payment is made.

Commission bids

Bids may be left with the bidding desk before a sale. The auctioneer will execute a commission bid as cheaply as the other bidding allows, and will never exceed the limit given. Commission bids are executed against the room in the order received.

Payment

Invoices are issued within one working day of the sale and are payable within five working days by bank transfer or card. Title in a lot does not pass until the invoice is paid in full.

Collection

Lots may be collected from Cheverell Wharf between 09:00 and 17:00 on weekdays. Storage is charged after ten working days at the rate above.

Condition and description

Every lot is sold as found. Condition reports are offered as an opinion and not as a statement of fact, and the absence of a reference to damage does not imply that a lot is free from it.

Vendors

Vendor commission is 15% of the hammer price, with an illustration charge of 24.00 a lot. Vendor terms do not affect the sum payable by a buyer.
