Selling with us

From first estimate to settlement, a lot passes through one specialist's hands, and the vendor deals with that specialist throughout.

Consigning

Once you accept an estimate, the item is entered for the next suitable sale, photographed, catalogued and insured under our roof from the day it arrives. Reserves are agreed in writing at consignment; we do not sell below a reserve, and we do not raise one after the catalogue is printed.

Vendor commission

Vendor commission is 15% of the hammer price, with an illustration charge of 24.00 a lot. There is no charge for insurance while a lot is with us, no lotting fee, and no charge for an unsold lot offered at our suggestion. Unsold lots may be re-offered once at a revised estimate or collected without charge within ten working days.

Settlement

Settlement is by bank transfer twenty working days after the sale, once the buyer has paid in full, with a statement listing hammer price, commission and charges lot by lot.
