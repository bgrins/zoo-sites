Privacy notice

This notice covers everyone who registers to bid, consigns property or asks for a valuation.

What we hold

Bidder registrations, paddle issue records, commission bid instructions, invoices and settlement statements, and the correspondence attached to each. For vendors we also hold the consignment record and reserve agreed for each lot.

Why we hold it

Auction records establish title and provenance, so we keep transaction records for as long as the question could reasonably arise, and in any case for the six years the revenue requires. Registration details of bidders who never buy are removed after two years of inactivity.

What we never do

We do not disclose the identity of a buyer to a vendor or of a vendor to a buyer, we do not publish paddle numbers against names, and we do not pass any detail to third parties beyond our carrier and, where the law requires it, the authorities. Requests to see what we hold go in writing to the registered office and are answered within a month.
