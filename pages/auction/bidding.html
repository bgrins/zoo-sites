How to bid

Four ways into the same room: in person, by commission, by telephone and online. Every route bids against the same rostrum at the same increments.

Your paddle

In the room, register at the bidding desk with a card and photographic identification and you carry a numbered paddle for the day. Online, a paddle code is issued with the hammer when you win a lot: it identifies you on the invoice and must be quoted when paying and collecting. Keep it; the saleroom will not read a paddle code back over the telephone.

Bidding in the room

Catch the auctioneer's eye and hold your paddle up. The auctioneer calls each advance; you are bidding the figure called, nothing else.

Commission bids

Leave a limit with the bidding desk before the sale and the auctioneer executes it as cheaply as the other bidding allows, never exceeding your limit. Commission bids are executed in the order received.

Telephone bidding

For lots estimated above 500, the desk can telephone you as the lot comes up and bid on your instruction. Lines are booked by noon the day before the sale.

Bidding online

A live lot page carries the figure standing with the auctioneer and the next bid the rostrum will take. Bids are accepted at the next bid figure only. The room does not pause for the internet: what stands on the rostrum governs, and once the hammer has fallen no further bid is taken. Remember that the buyer's premium is added to the hammer price on every route.
