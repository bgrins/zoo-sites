Viewing

Sale viewings are listed with each catalogue. Outside viewing days the rooms are open for consignments and collections only.

Working at Marlstone

The rooms train their own: porters who want to catalogue are taught to catalogue, and both current specialists in the instruments department began on the van. Openings for porters, administrators and trainee cataloguers are posted at the bidding desk and filled from the letters we hold on file. Write to the saleroom manager at the wharf stating what you want to learn.
