Shipping and packing

The packing office at Cheverell Wharf quotes on every purchased lot within two working days of the sale. Shipping is always quoted and invoiced separately from the lot itself.

What we pack ourselves

Instruments, clocks, books, silver and pictures are packed in-house. Pendulums are secured or removed, chronometers travel gimbal-locked in their own cases, and glazed pictures are taped and cornered. In-house packing starts at 18.00 a consignment.

Carriers

Within the county we deliver on our own van on Thursdays. Further afield we use insured specialist carriers for furniture and fine art; a tracked service for smaller lots. Quotes name the carrier and the insured value before you commit.

Collecting in person

Lots may be collected from the wharf between 09:00 and 17:00 on weekdays, bringing the invoice or the paddle code. Storage is free for ten working days and charged at 6.00 a day thereafter, as set out in the conditions of sale.

Export

Where a lot needs an export licence the packing office prepares the application; allow four to six weeks. A lot's catalogue notice states any known licensing matter before the sale.
