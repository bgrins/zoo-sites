Privacy

The studio is four people and a milling book. This page says plainly what we keep about the people we work with, why, and for how long.

What we keep

For every order: the name, email address and telephone number of the person who placed it and of the person holding sign-off, the delivery and collection address, and the correspondence about the job. For every proof pull: who approved or sent it back, and when. For a calibration check at the calibration desk: the swatch picked and the code issued, held against the order it was made for.

Why

To run the order, to prove later which sheet was approved and by whom, and to invoice for the work. We do not keep a mailing list, we send no newsletters, and nothing is passed to anyone outside the studio except the carrier who collects or delivers your parcel and our accountant, who sees invoices.

How long

Order records and approvals are kept for as long as the approved sheet is flat-filed, which is two years from the order closing, and invoices for the six years the law requires. Calibration records go with the order they belong to.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we are not obliged to keep. Write to studio@velloquin.example or to the studio address below; we answer within a month, and usually within the week. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the data protection regulator.

This website

The site sets one cookie, which keeps a calibration check tied to your visit while you make it. There is no analytics and no advertising on these pages.

Velloquin Studio Ltd is the controller of the personal information described on this page.
