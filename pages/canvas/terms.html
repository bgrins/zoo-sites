Terms of trade

These are the terms the studio works to. A copy goes out with every quotation, and the version supplied with your quotation is the one that governs your order.

Quotations and orders

A quotation holds for thirty days and covers the work described on it, the stock named on it, and one press and profile pairing. An order begins when the studio books it onto a press, not when the enquiry arrives, and the booking is confirmed to you in writing with the order number you should quote in all correspondence.

Proof pulls

Each order carries up to three pulls. A pull is either approved by you or sent back for reproof, and the order closes on the first pull you approve. Where three pulls have been sent back, the studio will not pull a fourth against the same order: the librarian and a pressman review the reference with you and, if the work is to continue, it is requoted. That is not a penalty. It means the reference, the stock or the profile is wrong, and pulling again would only spend your money confirming it.

Approval

Approval is given on the sheet, in the studio or through the calibration desk, by the person named on the order as holding sign-off. The studio cannot accept approval passed on by a third party. Once a pull is approved the studio's obligation is to match that sheet, and nothing else, on any later run against the same order.

What the studio cannot promise

Print is a physical process on a physical substrate. The studio guarantees a match to the approved sheet within the tolerance stated on the order, on the stock supplied for that order, on the press named on it. It does not guarantee a match on a different stock, on a different press, on a screen, or after a client's own finishing, laminating or varnishing. Where a substitution is unavoidable the studio will say so before pulling and will requote rather than proceed on the old tolerance.

Ownership and retention

Proofs, recipe cards, milling notes and library sets remain the property of the studio and are released only against the originating order. Approved sheets are flat-filed for two years and then destroyed unless you ask in writing for them to be kept longer. Stock you supply and do not use is returned with the job or held for sixty days.

Payment and cancellation

Accounts are invoiced on completion and payable within thirty days. Bench time cancelled with more than three working days' notice is not charged; inside that, the booked time is charged whether or not ink was milled, because the slot cannot be resold at that notice. Ink already milled to a client's reference is charged in full.
