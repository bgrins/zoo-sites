Colour, held to the sheet

TintForge is a small studio in Dyehouse Yard that mills ink, keeps standing swatch libraries, and proofs colour for printers, packers and designers who need this year's run to match last year's.

Two pressmen and a librarian work the floor. Everything the studio promises is settled against a physical draw-down on the client's own substrate, because a number agreed on a screen is an opinion and a sheet pulled on the press is a fact. Clients send us the stock, the press profile and the job they are trying to repeat, and we send back a proof and a written recipe card.

How a job runs

  1. You send the stock, the press and profile pairing, and whatever reference you already have — an old sheet, a library number, or a sample of the finished article.
  2. The librarian matches the reference against the standing libraries and books a work order onto the proofing press named on it.
  3. A pressman mills the ink and pulls a proof. An order is pulled up to three times; it closes on the first pull you approve.
  4. Remote sign-off runs through the calibration desk, so what you approve on your screen is what the press has actually laid down.
  5. The approved sheet is flat-filed with the order and stays available for repeat work. Earlier runs are listed in the proof archive.

Working with the studio

The desk is staffed from eight in the morning until four in the afternoon, Monday to Friday. Bench time is booked a week ahead in ordinary periods and rather longer before the autumn catalogue season, so send the stock early if a date matters. Collections and deliveries come through the yard gate; there is no counter, and visits are by appointment.
