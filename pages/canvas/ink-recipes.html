Recipe practice

Every ink the studio mills is written up the same way: pigment first, then vehicle, then drier. The card records the character of each component rather than weights or ratios — the milling book at the bench holds the working quantities, and the card is what travels with the job.

A recipe card is never a formulation. When a client asks for a repeat, the pressman pulls the card, checks it against the standing library entry, and mixes to the reference draw-down rather than to any figure on paper.
