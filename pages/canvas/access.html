Visiting the yard

Dyehouse Yard is a working print yard, not a showroom, and visits are by appointment. This page says plainly what a visit involves so that nobody arrives to find out the hard way.

Getting in

The yard entrance is a vehicle gate off the lane; there is no shop front and no bell on the street. Telephone the studio when you arrive and a pressman will come out to the gate. The yard surface is old brick sett, uneven in places and slippery in wet weather. Unit 6 is entered level from the yard, with no step at the threshold, and the proofing floor and the calibration desk are both on that level. The milling room is up four steps with a handrail on the right; a pressman will bring anything you need down to the floor rather than ask you to go up.

On the floor

Presses run while visitors are on the floor. It is loud enough that a conversation has to be held to one side, and the room smells strongly of solvent. Nothing on the floor flashes or strobes, and there is no public address system. Two chairs sit by the calibration desk and you are welcome to sit for as long as you like; a proof pull and its inspection together take about twenty minutes.

Adjustments we can make

Say what would help when you book and it is arranged. The studio can hold a quiet slot at the start or end of the day with the presses stopped, meet you in the office rather than on the floor, write out anything said at the bench, or work through a proof slowly with one person doing the talking. Assistance dogs are welcome in the office and at the calibration desk, though not in the milling room. There is a parking space inside the gate that can be kept clear for you if you ask in advance; the lane itself has no parking at all.

If you would rather not come in

You do not have to visit to get work done. Recipe cards go out as plain text or in large print on request, proofs are posted, and sign-off is given in writing after a check at the calibration desk, from wherever you are. Where a decision really wants two people looking at one sheet, a pressman will bring the sheet to you inside the county.

Telling us what does not work

If something here is a barrier, or something on this site is, write to the studio and say so. Replies go out within five working days, and anything that stops a client approving work is dealt with the same week rather than added to a list.
