People Operations / New starters

Your first weeks at Orsino Consulting

This hub covers what happens between accepting your offer and settling in. Read it alongside your welcome email from your hiring coordinator.

Before day one

Once your offer is signed, your hiring coordinator confirms your start date and sends a welcome email naming your engagement path. Complete the onboarding intake before your first day so the front desk knows to expect you. IT provisions your account and equipment against your start date, and your line manager arranges a desk and a first-week plan.

Your first day

Arrive at reception for 9:15am and ask for People Operations. The front desk signs you in, takes care of your security badge and walks you over to your team.

Bring the personal document checklist generated during your onboarding intake session. The checklist is built for you from the engagement path stored in your session, so complete the intake before you travel and bring everything it lists.

The front desk scans your items and hands them straight back, then your badge lets you through the barriers for the rest of your engagement.

Buddy scheme

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from outside their immediate team for the first eight weeks. Your buddy answers the questions you would not bother a manager with: where things are, who does what, and how the firm really works. Your coordinator introduces you by email in your first week, and buddies aim to meet at least every two weeks.

Questions before you start? Call People Operations on extension 4120, weekdays 8am to 4pm.
