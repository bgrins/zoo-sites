Raise a support ticket

For anything that needs tracking — payroll corrections, policy queries, changes to your record — raise a ticket by email rather than phoning. Send your message to peopleops@orsino-consulting.example with the subject line prefixed TICKET: followed by a short description, for example TICKET: expense claim not paid in July run.

Tickets are acknowledged within one working day and assigned a reference number. Quote the reference in any follow-up so the same adviser picks it up.
Urgent issue outside these hours? Leave a voicemail on extension 4120 and the team will return your call the next working morning.
