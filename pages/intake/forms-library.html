Forms available at the desk

Paper copies of every form below can be collected from the People Operations desk, weekdays 8am to 4pm. Hand completed forms back to the desk or to your line manager.

  • Time-off request — book paid time off or request unpaid leave; see the time off and absence policy for notice periods.
  • Expense claim — monthly claim sheet for costs under the expenses policy; attach itemized receipts.
  • Change of address — update your home address and emergency contact details held by payroll.
  • IT access request — request systems access, shared drive permissions or additional equipment, countersigned by your manager.
  • Workstation assessment — ergonomic self-assessment for new starters and anyone changing desks.
  • 401(k) election — change your deferral or opt out of the plan described on pay and benefits.
Cannot get to the desk? Email peopleops@orsino-consulting.example and the team will send a form to your office address.
Not sure which form you need? Call People Operations on extension 4120, weekdays 8am to 4pm.
