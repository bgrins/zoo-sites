People Operations / Policies

Policy summaries

Short summaries of the policies staff ask about most. The full policy texts are available on request from People Operations.

Employment eligibility

Every employee must have their identity and eligibility to work in the United States verified on their first day, and again whenever a time-limited work authorization is due to expire. Checks are carried out in person at the front desk, where evidence is scanned and returned immediately. Nobody may begin billable work until People Operations records the check as complete.

Time off and absence

Full-time staff receive 20 days of paid time off plus ten firm holidays, accruing from the start date and prorated for part-time hours. Time off is requested through your manager at least two weeks ahead for periods of a week or longer. Sick time should be reported to your manager by 10am on the first day; absences longer than three days need a note from a health care provider.

Expenses

The firm reimburses reasonable costs necessarily incurred on client or firm business, including coach-class travel, lodging within the published nightly caps, and meals. Claims are submitted monthly with itemized receipts and are approved by your engagement manager. Claims older than 90 days are paid only with director approval.

Contractor engagement

Independent contractors are engaged only under a signed scope of work or master services agreement arranged through the engagement team. Contractors invoice monthly, are responsible for their own tax affairs, and are not enrolled in staff benefit schemes. Engagements are reviewed at least every twelve months to confirm the working arrangement still matches the contract.
