People Operations / Policies

Policy summaries

Short summaries of the policies staff ask about most. The full policy texts are available on request from People Operations.

Right to work checks

Everyone working for the firm must have their right to work verified before or on their first day, and again whenever a time-limited permission is due to expire. Checks are carried out in person at the front desk, where evidence is scanned and returned immediately. Nobody may begin billable work until People Operations records the check as complete.

Leave and absence

Full-time staff receive 26 days of annual leave plus bank holidays, accruing from the start date and prorated for part-time hours. Leave is requested through your line manager at least two weeks ahead for periods of a week or longer. Sickness absence should be reported to your manager by 10am on the first day, with a self-certification for absences up to seven days.

Expenses

The firm reimburses reasonable costs necessarily incurred on client or firm business, including standard-class travel, accommodation within the published nightly caps, and subsistence. Claims are submitted monthly with itemised receipts and are approved by your engagement manager. Claims older than 90 days are paid only with director approval.

Contractor engagement

Independent contractors are engaged only under a signed scope of work or master services agreement arranged through the engagement team. Contractors invoice monthly, are responsible for their own tax affairs, and are not enrolled in staff benefit schemes. Engagements are reviewed at least every twelve months to confirm the working arrangement still matches the contract.
