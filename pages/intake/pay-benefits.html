How you are paid and what comes with the job

A summary of payroll and the core benefits available to staff. Your offer letter states which apply to your engagement.

Payroll dates

Salaried staff are paid monthly on the 25th of each month. When the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday, payment lands on the last working day before it. Payslips are placed in your staff mailbox two working days before payday. Changes to bank details must reach People Operations by the 10th of the month to take effect in that month's run.

Starting mid-month? Your first payment covers the days worked from your start date and is included in the next scheduled run.

Pension

Staff are enrolled in the firm's group pension scheme from their first full month. The firm contributes 6 percent of base salary and matches personal contributions up to a further 2 percent. You may adjust your contribution level, or opt out, using the pension election form from the forms library.

Healthcare cash plan

The healthcare cash plan reimburses everyday health costs such as dental check-ups, eye tests, prescriptions and physiotherapy, up to an annual limit per category. Cover starts on the first day of the month after you join, and you can add family members at a payroll-deducted rate.

Season ticket loans

Staff who have passed probation can apply for an interest-free loan to cover an annual public transport season ticket. The loan is repaid in equal monthly instalments through payroll over the life of the ticket. Apply through People Operations with a quote from the transport operator.
