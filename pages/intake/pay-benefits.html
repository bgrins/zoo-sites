How you are paid and what comes with the job

A summary of payroll and the core benefits available to staff. Your offer letter states which apply to your engagement.

Pay dates

Staff are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and on the last business day of each month. When the 15th falls on a weekend or a firm holiday, payment lands on the business day before it. Pay statements are posted to your staff mailbox two business days before payday. Changes to direct deposit details must reach People Operations by the 5th or the 20th to take effect in the next run.

Starting mid-month? Your first payment covers the days worked from your start date and is included in the next scheduled run.

401(k) plan

Staff are enrolled in the firm's 401(k) plan from their first full month, at a default deferral of 4 percent of base salary. The firm matches contributions dollar for dollar up to 5 percent of base salary. You may change your deferral, or opt out, using the 401(k) election form from the forms library.

Health coverage

The firm's group plan covers medical, dental and vision care, and the firm pays 80 percent of the employee premium. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after you join, and you can add a spouse or dependents at a payroll-deducted rate during your first 30 days or at open enrollment.

Commuter benefits

Staff can set aside pre-tax pay for transit passes and for parking at the office garage, up to the monthly federal limit. Elections are made through People Operations and take effect from the next pay date; you can change or stop them at any time.

Payroll query? Call People Operations on extension 4120, weekdays 8am to 4pm.
