People Operations / Legal information

Notices for users of this intranet

These notices apply to all pages of the People Operations intranet, including the onboarding intake.

Privacy notice

Personal information entered on this intranet is processed by Orsino Consulting LLP to administer employment and contractor engagements. Candidate and onboarding data is held under the firm's candidate privacy notice and retained only as long as the engagement and applicable law require. Questions about your data can be raised with People Operations.

Terms of use

This intranet is provided for staff, contractors and incoming joiners of Orsino Consulting LLP. Content may not be copied outside the firm, and access credentials must not be shared. The firm may amend or withdraw any page without notice.

Accessibility

These pages are built to work with keyboards and screen readers, use consistent landmarks and headings, and maintain readable text contrast. If any page is difficult to use, contact People Operations on extension 4120 and an accessible alternative will be arranged.

Cookies

The intranet uses only the session identifiers needed to keep you signed in and to hold your intake session open. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are set, and session identifiers expire when the session ends.

Questions about any of these notices? Call People Operations on extension 4120, weekdays 8am to 4pm.
