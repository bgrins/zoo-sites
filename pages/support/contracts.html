Contracts and notice

Plans run on a minimum term of 12 or 24 months and then roll monthly at the same price. We do not raise prices mid-term.

Leaving

After the minimum term, thirty days' notice ends the service with nothing more to pay. Inside the minimum term, an early-exit charge applies: the remaining months at half the plan rate, capped at six months. The exact figure for your account is shown to an adviser before you commit to anything.

Switching to another provider

If the new provider uses the One Touch Switch process, they handle the notice and the timing; you do not need to call us. Your final bill is pro-rated to the switch date.

Your equipment

The gateway and any discs are returned in the prepaid bag we send with the final bill. Unreturned equipment is charged at 40.00 per item after sixty days.

Fourteen-day cooling off

New orders can be cancelled without charge within fourteen days of the order being placed, even if the installation has already happened. The terms of service set this out in full.
