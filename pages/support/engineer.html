Engineer visits

Visits are booked by advisers once remote checks point at something physical. You choose the slot in the chat.

Appointment slots

Morning slots run 08:00 to 13:00 and afternoon slots 13:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday. The engineer sends a text when they are on their way, usually about forty minutes out.

Being there

Someone over eighteen needs to be in for the whole slot. The engineer will want to reach the gateway and the point where our fibre enters the property; a cleared metre around each is plenty.

If we miss it

If the engineer does not arrive in the slot and did not rearrange with you beforehand, a missed-appointment credit of 30.00 is applied to your next bill automatically. It appears on the bill without you asking.

Charges

Visits for faults in our network, our equipment or our installation are free. Where the fault is found in your own wiring or equipment, the visit is charged at 65.00 and the engineer tells you before doing any chargeable work.
