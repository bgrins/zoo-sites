Moving home

Give us your moving date and the new address as soon as you have both, and we will line the two up so you are not paying twice or offline for weeks.

If the new address is on our network

Check the address with the coverage checker. Where we already serve it, the move is an engineer visit at the new address and nothing at the old one: take the gateway with you, and your plan, price and account number carry over unchanged.

If it is not

Where we cannot serve the new address, the contract ends on your moving date without an early-exit charge on plans taken after January 2025; earlier plans pay the remaining months at half rate, as set out in contracts and notice.

Timing

Two weeks' notice is enough in most of Ashcombe; new-build streets can need an external crew and a month is safer. The old address stays live until midnight on your moving date so the changeover does not strand you.

Start the move

Chat with an adviser with your completion or tenancy date to hand. Moves are handled by the same team seven days a week.
