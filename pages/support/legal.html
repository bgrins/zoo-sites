Privacy notice

We hold your name, address, contact details, equipment record and twelve months of connection quality data, because we need them to run and fix your service. We do not sell personal data, and traffic content is never inspected or retained. Marketing uses only the channels you have ticked under marketing choices. To see, correct or export what we hold, write to help@kelverne-fibre.example; we answer within thirty days.

Terms of service

We supply the service described in your order, maintain the equipment we provide, and fix network faults without charge. You pay bills when due, house our equipment reasonably, and do not resell the connection. Minimum terms, notice and early-exit charges are as described under contracts and notice. Where we miss the automatic compensation standards for a total loss of service, credits are applied to your next bill without a claim.

Complaints code

Raise a complaint with any adviser and it gets a case reference and an owner the same day. If we have not resolved it within eight weeks, or we send you a deadlock letter sooner, you may take it to the independent adjudication scheme named in the letter, free of charge. The full code is available in print on request.
