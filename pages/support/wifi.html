Wi-Fi and equipment

Your gateway serves the whole home over Wi-Fi and four wired ports. Most coverage problems are about where it sits, not what it is.

Where to put the gateway

Keep the gateway upright, out in the open and roughly central to the rooms you use most. Cupboards, television cabinets and fish tanks all soak up signal, and a gateway on the floor loses reach compared with one at desk height.

Cordless phone bases and baby monitors share the 2.4 GHz band; give the gateway a metre of clearance from either.

Extending coverage

If a far room drops to a crawl while the room with the gateway is fine, a mesh disc is usually the answer. Discs pair to the gateway automatically and carry the same network name; up to three can be added to a plan from the account page or through an adviser.

The equipment we supply

Kelverne supplies and maintains the gateway and any discs for as long as you hold the service; they remain our equipment and are replaced without charge if they fail. The model number is printed on the base plate and listed in the Equipment panel of your account, and an adviser will ask for it before booking equipment work.

Using your own router is supported in modem mode, but our advisers can only trace a fault as far as our own equipment.

Still stuck?

If coverage was fine and has worsened without the furniture moving, run through connection problems first, then raise it with an adviser.
