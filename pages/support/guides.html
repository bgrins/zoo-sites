Gateway guides

Three short checks that clear most connection problems, in the order an adviser would ask you to do them.

Read the status lights

The single light on the gateway front tells you where the problem is:

Steady whiteConnected and healthy. If things are still slow, it is a Wi-Fi or device matter, not the line.
AmberThe gateway is up but cannot reach the network. Note how often it happens; an adviser will ask.
Blinking whiteStarting up or updating. Leave it for ten minutes.
RedHardware fault. Do not factory-reset a red gateway; raise it with an adviser.

Restart your gateway

Order matters, or devices come back before the gateway is ready:

1. Unplug the gateway at the wall and wait thirty seconds.
2. Plug it back in and wait for a steady white light — up to five minutes.
3. Only then restart the devices that were struggling.

Do not use the recessed reset pin unless an adviser asks; it erases your Wi-Fi name and password.

Test a wired device

Two minutes with a cable rules Wi-Fi in or out:

1. Plug a laptop into any yellow port on the gateway.
2. Turn the laptop's Wi-Fi off so the cable is the only path.
3. Run a speed test in the laptop’s browser and note the download figure.

A good wired figure with poor Wi-Fi means coverage: see Wi-Fi and equipment. A poor wired figure is a line matter: raise it with an adviser.
