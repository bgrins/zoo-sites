Speed and performance

Full fibre does not slow at peak times on our network, so a slow evening usually points at Wi-Fi or at one busy device.

What speed to expect

Your plan speed is measured at the gateway's wired ports. Over Wi-Fi, the fastest single device typically sees between half and two thirds of the plan speed, less through walls and floors. That gap is normal for every provider; it is the radio, not the line.

Test it properly

Run the speed test from the account portal on a device plugged into the gateway with a cable, with other heavy use paused. A wired test inside ten percent of your plan speed means the line is healthy. The two-minute wired test guide walks through it.

One room is slow

If the wired figure is right but a particular room is not, treat it as a coverage problem: see Wi-Fi and equipment.

Everything is slow, even wired

A wired test well below plan speed is worth a case. Note the figure the test gave you and raise it with an adviser; they can read the line rate from our side while you are in the chat.
