About Kelverne

Kelverne Fibre builds and runs its own full-fibre network from Bolt House on Redgate Wharf, and answers its own phones.

Who we are

We started in 2019 by lighting three streets around the wharf, and grew by finishing each area properly before starting the next. The network, the vans and the advisers are all ours; nothing about your service is subcontracted to a call centre elsewhere.

Network rollout

Build order is decided by registered interest and by how far a street sits from fibre we already own. Ashcombe and Redgate are complete; Bolt Vale is in its final phase, and survey work has begun east of the river. The coverage checker gives the current answer for any postcode, and registering interest with an adviser genuinely moves a street up the list.

Work with us

We hire fibre engineers, network planners and advisers as the build grows. Adviser roles are based at Bolt House — we do not run remote scripted support — and engineering crews work the towns they live in. Send a note of what you do to help@kelverne-fibre.example with "Working at Kelverne" in the subject line.
