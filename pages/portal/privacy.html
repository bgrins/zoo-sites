Carrier Access / Privacy

Privacy notice

How Overlane Logistics Group handles carrier account data. Last revised 3 March 2026.

What we hold

For each Carrier Access account we hold the name, work email address, role and carrier assignment issued by your administrator, together with sign-in timestamps and the console actions needed to operate the service. Shipment and invoice records belong to the contract between Overlane and your carrier and are retained for seven years as required by our carriage terms.

What we do not do

We do not sell account data, and we do not use it for advertising. Access to account records inside Overlane is limited to the service desk and to staff with a contractual need, and every such access is logged.

Your choices

Your carrier administrator can correct or close an account at any time. Closed accounts are purged from the active directory within 30 days; audit records of past actions are kept for the retention period above. Questions go to privacy@overlane.example or to the registered address below.
