Terms of use

Conditions for using Carrier Access. Effective 1 January 2026.

1. Accounts

Accounts are issued to named individuals by a carrier administrator under an active carriage agreement with Overlane Logistics Group. Accounts are personal: sharing credentials, or signing in for another person, is a breach of these terms and may lead to suspension of the carrier's access.

2. Acceptable use

The console, the reports area and the driver app may be used only to manage shipments, invoices and reporting under your carrier's agreement. Probing the service for weaknesses, harvesting data beyond your carrier's records, or interfering with another carrier's use is prohibited.

3. Figures and reports

Report figures are drawn from district pollers at the close of each reporting window and are provided for operational use. Invoiced amounts on the contract itself prevail over any figure shown in the console. Corrections are handled per the process in the Support centre.

4. Availability and changes

We aim for continuous service but may take the platform down for maintenance with notice per the Network status page. We may revise these terms with 30 days notice to carrier administrators; continued use after the effective date is acceptance.
