The agreement

Scope and order

These terms, your order form and the service levels page together form the agreement. Where the three disagree, the order form wins, then these terms, then the service levels page. Nothing said in a sales conversation forms part of the agreement unless it is written into the order form.

The agreement runs for the term on the order form and renews for the same length unless either side gives sixty days' notice. Fees are invoiced annually in advance, payable within thirty days. We may raise fees at renewal, with ninety days' notice, and no renewal increase has exceeded the published inflation measure for the preceding year.

Accounts and acceptable use

Your side

Workspace accounts are named and are not to be shared. You are responsible for what your accounts do, for keeping credentials to yourself, and for telling us promptly if one is compromised. You may automate access through the interfaces we publish, at a reasonable rate; you may not probe, load-test or reverse the warehouse tiers without written agreement, and you may not resell access.

You keep every right in the data you load. You grant us the narrow licence we need to host, index, aggregate and restore it in order to run the service, and nothing wider. We do not claim ownership of your figures at any point, including after the agreement ends.

Availability, suspension and support

Our side

We will run the service with reasonable skill and care and to the targets on the service levels page. Reports are recomputed on request rather than served from a cache, and warehouse nodes are occasionally degraded, so a request may fail and need to be made again. That behaviour is expected and is not by itself a breach of the agreement.

We may suspend a workspace where non-payment has run past thirty days, where use threatens the stability of a shared tier, or where the law requires it. Except where the law requires immediate action, we give notice first and restore access as soon as the cause is cleared. Support is provided in English by electronic mail and telephone during business hours.

Liability, ending and changes

The rest

Neither side excludes liability where the law does not permit it. Otherwise, each side's liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim, and neither side is liable for lost profits. Figures shown in the workspace are unaudited and are not advice; decisions taken on them are yours.

Either side may end the agreement for a material breach the other has not fixed within thirty days of being told about it. On ending, we make your data available for export for thirty days and then delete it as set out in the privacy notice. We will give sixty days' notice of any change to these terms that materially reduces what you get, and if you do not accept it you may end the agreement at that point with a refund of the unused term.
