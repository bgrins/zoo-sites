Privacy notice

How Zellick Analytics handles workspace data, customer data and the records the warehouse keeps about both.

Two kinds of data

Scope

Customer data is what your organisation loads into the reporting warehouse: billing ledgers, subscription events, support exports and payroll journals. We process it on your instructions and for no other purpose. We do not mine it, we do not use it to train models, and we do not aggregate it with another customer's data to build benchmarks.

Workspace data is what we generate as you use the product: account records, sign-in times, the reports and extracts you run, and the retrieval jobs you submit. We use it to run the service, to bill for it, and to work out why something failed. The access log page in this workspace shows you the same records we hold.

How long we keep it

Retention

Access and retrieval records are retained for eighteen months and then deleted. Quarterly close packs are sealed to tier 3 cold storage after ninety days and retained for seven years, because closing records normally have to be kept that long. Cohort extracts are sealed after thirty days and retained three years. Draft workbooks are purged at thirty days and are not recoverable afterwards, sealed or otherwise.

When a workspace closes, customer data is deleted within thirty days and purged from sealed media at the next volume rotation, which runs quarterly. We will confirm both in writing if you ask.

Who else sees it

Processors and access

Warehouse nodes and cold-storage volumes are operated by us. Two sub-processors are involved in the wider service: a hosting provider for the compute tier and a mail relay for scheduled extract delivery. Both are contracted as processors, both are covered by the same confidentiality terms, and the current list is available on request.

Inside Zellick, access to customer data is limited to the support and warehouse engineers who need it, is logged against a named account, and is reviewed quarterly. We do not read your data to answer a support ticket unless you ask us to, and we say so in the ticket when we have.

Your rights and how to use them

Requests

You can ask for a copy of the workspace data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Where the data belongs to your employer's workspace we will pass the request to the workspace administrator rather than act on it alone. Requests are acknowledged within five working days and answered within thirty.

Write to privacy@zellickanalytics.example, or to the Data Protection Lead, Zellick Analytics Ltd, Callowmere House, 40 Orrinford Road, Callowmere CX4 9RT. Telephone 0808 157 0231. If we get it wrong, you can complain to your national data protection authority, and we would rather you told us first so we can put it right.
