Reading the table

Definitions

A cohort is every account whose first invoice fell in the month shown. An account counts as retained while it holds at least one paid subscription. Cohorts younger than the column show a dash rather than a projected figure.

The full cohort table is delivered as CSV each Monday through Scheduled extracts.

Cohorts settle at month close; figures for the current month are provisional.
