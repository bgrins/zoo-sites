Service levels

What we commit to for the workspace, the reporting warehouse and cold storage, and what we do when we miss it.

Availability target

Monthly, per workspace

We target 99.5 per cent monthly availability for the workspace and the reporting warehouse, measured over the calendar month and excluding announced maintenance. Availability is measured against the workspace loading and a report request completing, not against a network path we do not own.

Cold storage carries no availability target because the media are offline by design. It carries a retrieval target instead: a sealed archive is mounted and returned within thirty seconds of a restore being submitted. In practice the median mount takes about eight seconds, and a job re-queued behind another takes longer, never less.

Degraded is not unavailable

Definitions

A node is degraded when it answers, but slowly or not every time. A node is unavailable when it does not answer at all. The distinction matters because our reports are recomputed per request rather than served from a cache: while an aggregate node is degraded, a report request can fail and the same request made again can succeed. Only the second case counts against the availability target.

Clients should therefore treat a failed report request as something to repeat, not as an outage. Where a request fails repeatedly across several minutes, open a ticket and quote the time in your workspace time zone; the bulletin in the sidebar carries anything we already know about.

Claiming a credit

Process

Credits are not applied automatically. Write to help@zellickanalytics.example within thirty days of the end of the month concerned, with the workspace name and the periods you were affected. We check the claim against our own measurements and answer within ten business days. Where we agree, the credit is applied to the next invoice; where we disagree, we send you the measurements we relied on.

Credits are the remedy for a missed target under this page. Support hours are 08:00 to 18:00 on business days, with severity 1 cover outside them for workspaces on the extended plan. Telephone 0808 157 0231.
