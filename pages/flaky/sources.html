When a feed fails

Retries

A failed feed is retried automatically at the next scheduled slot. Reports built from it carry the last good load until then, so a failed feed does not blank a figure. Two consecutive failures raise a ticket with the warehouse team.

Feed schedules are set by your workspace administrator.
