Privacy notice

Last revised 12 February 2026.

We collect the information needed to run your accounts: identity and contact details, transaction records, and the device details created when you use Online Banking. We use it to provide banking services, prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations. Records are retained for seven years after an account closes, as banking regulation requires.

We share data with payment networks to process your transactions, with credit reference agencies where you apply for lending, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell customer data.

You can request a copy of the information we hold, or ask us to correct it, by writing to the Data Office at the registered address or asking in any branch.
