Security centre

How CaldmoorBank protects you, and what to watch for.

CaldmoorBank will never ask for your password, full card number or a one-time code by email, text message or phone call, and will never send you a link that asks you to sign in urgently. If a message pressures you to act immediately or threatens to suspend your account, do not follow its link: type the address of your branch site yourself and check the account activity page.

Spotting a lookalike sign-in page

Fraudulent copies of bank sign-in pages are usually near-perfect. Check the small print: the legal name and firm reference in the footer, the exact spelling of the bank's name, and whether the page adds trust badges of its own. A security indicator that is drawn inside the page body, rather than shown by your browser, proves nothing. When in doubt, close the page and use a bookmark you created yourself.
