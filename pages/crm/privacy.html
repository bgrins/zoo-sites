Privacy

How Kelsmere Software, Inc. handles personal data in the console. The customer that owns a workspace is the controller of the data inside it.

What we collect about you

Your name, work email address and role in each workspace; sign-in times and the address you signed in from; and the pages you open, kept as an audit trail for the workspace owner.

Data inside a workspace

Account, order and contact records are the customer's data. Kelsmere processes them on the customer's instructions, under the data processing terms in the subscription agreement, and never sells or shares them.

Cookies

The console sets one session cookie to keep you signed in. Grid sort order is held in your browser's session storage. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention

Audit trails are kept for as long as the workspace retention period, currently seven fiscal years for this workspace. A member removed from a workspace loses access at once.

Your rights

For data inside a workspace, contact the workspace owner. For data Kelsmere holds about your own account, write to privacy@kelsmerecrm.example.
