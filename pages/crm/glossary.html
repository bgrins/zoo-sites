Glossary

Terms as this workspace uses them. Where an operator and a report seem to disagree, the difference is usually one of these definitions.

Records

TermMeaning
AccountA trading customer of the workspace, carrying one account id for its whole life.
Account idThe permanent key on an account record. It is never reissued, even after an account closes.
OrderA confirmed sale against one account, carried at its recorded order value.
OpportunityAn open deal that has not become an order. Opportunities never carry an order value.

Territory and region

TermMeaning
TerritoryThe sales patch an account is assigned to, set when the account is opened.
RegionThe name the territory rolls up to. It follows the territory, not the billing address.
OwnerThe named analyst accountable for an account and its open opportunities.
ReassignmentA change of territory or owner. Currently frozen workspace-wide.

Warehouse

TermMeaning
SnapshotThe nightly copy of the records that every grid reads from.
RebuildThe 02:00 job that produces the snapshot.
Retired reportA catalogue entry no longer built. Its last run date stands; nothing refreshes it.
SourceThe grids a report is compiled from, named on its catalogue row.

Console

TermMeaning
WorkspaceOne customer's data, settings and members, identified by workspace id.
Grid engineThe component that renders and sorts every table. This workspace runs v3.
Saved viewA stored filter and column arrangement. Read-only on a shared link.
Shared workspace linkA read-only session on the workspace, with export and delivery disabled.
RoleOwner or analyst. Owners administer the workspace; analysts read it.

Period

TermMeaning
Fiscal yearJanuary to December for this workspace.
CloseThe 9th of the month following a quarter end.
RolledRecorded after close, and therefore counted in the next period.
RetentionSeven fiscal years, after which records leave the workspace.

Usage notes

Region and territory are not interchangeable in writing: a territory is the patch, a region is what it rolls up to. An order value is a property of an order and of nothing else, so an account, an opportunity or a task never carries one.
