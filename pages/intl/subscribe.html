Subscribe to notices

The Authority circulates a bulletin by email whenever a supplementary notice is issued or withdrawn.

The notices bulletin

The bulletin carries the full text of each new supplementary notice, names the destination and level it concerns, and records notices withdrawn since the last bulletin. It is sent in the language edition of your choice and carries nothing except notices.

How to subscribe

Send an email to notices@qta.gov.example from the address at which you want to receive the bulletin, with the word Subscribe in the subject line and the edition you want (English, Arabic, or Japanese) in the body. Subscriptions are confirmed by return within two working days.

To stop the bulletin, write to the same address with Unsubscribe in the subject line. The address is removed within two working days and no record of it is kept.

If you cannot use email

Current notices can be read on any advisory page of this site, heard from the duty officer on +44 20 7946 0118, or consulted in print at the Harbour Administration Building.
