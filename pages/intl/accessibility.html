Accessibility

The Authority intends this site to be usable by every traveller, including those using assistive technology.

Design of the site

Pages are plain documents readable without images, and every page other than the destination advisories reads in full without scripts. Headings follow a consistent order, links describe their destination, and every page can be operated from the keyboard alone. Text can be enlarged in the browser without loss of content.

The site is published in English, Arabic, and Japanese, and each page declares its language and text direction so that screen readers pronounce it correctly.

Known limitations

The destination advisory pages, supplementary notices included, are assembled by a script. Where scripts are unavailable the page says so and gives the duty officer's number; the duty officer can read the current advisory and notices over the telephone.

Reporting problems

If any part of the site is difficult to use, write to access@qta.gov.example describing the page and the difficulty. Reports are acknowledged within five working days and inform the next revision of the site.
