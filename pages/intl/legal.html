Privacy

The site sets one session cookie, which the destination pages need to load their supplementary notices. It is deleted when you close the browser and identifies no one, and the Authority keeps no account of individual visitors. The server records standard technical logs, kept for thirty days for the security of the service and then destroyed.

Email addresses sent to the notices list or to the Authority's offices are used only for the correspondence or subscription concerned and are not passed to any other body.
