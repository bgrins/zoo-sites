Register a trip

Registration tells the duty officer where travellers expect to be, so that they can be reached if a supplementary notice affects their route.

What registration does

A registered itinerary is held at the duty office for the duration of the trip and for thirty days afterwards, then destroyed. If a notice is issued for a destination on the itinerary, the duty officer attempts to reach the traveller at the contact given. Registration is voluntary and free of charge.

How to register

Email registrations@qta.gov.example before setting out, giving: full name as in the travel document; travel document number and nationality; the itinerary with dates; and a telephone number or email address usable during the trip. Registrations are confirmed by return within two working days.

Travellers already in the region may register in person at the Harbour Administration Building, or through their own government's mission, which can pass the details to the duty office. Trekking parties for the Neruva Highlands should also register with the district office, as the standing guidance directs.

Changes and cancellation

Write to the same address to change or cancel a registration at any time. If a trip ends early, cancelling promptly spares the duty officer needless contact attempts.
