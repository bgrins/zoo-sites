Harbour operations

The Authority publishes operating information for Qandara Harbour, at Marsat Qandara, on behalf of the harbour master's office in the Harbour Administration Building.

The harbour office

The harbour operations desk is staffed at all hours. It confirms the berthing schedule below each week, posts tide tables at the office entrance, and relays weather warnings for the coastal approaches during the summer squall season.

Harbour berthing schedule

The schedule below is the standing weekly pattern for the current season. The harbour master confirms each week's berthing on the preceding Thursday; arrival and departure times shift with the tide.

Passengers joining the Qandara Pearl should be at the South 2 gate an hour before the posted departure, with travel documents to hand.

Visitors to the harbour

The working areas of the harbour are closed to the public. Visitors and meeters should wait at the terminal building. Photography of harbour installations requires written permission from the port office.
