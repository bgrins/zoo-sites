Operating terms

Conditions of use for Ostara Surface Systems fleet operations consoles. Opening a console session constitutes acceptance of these terms.

Authorised use

Consoles are provided to certified drive team members for the conduct of assigned sol plans. Certification is per vehicle class and lapses after ninety days without a logged drive. Sharing a live session, or issuing commands under another operator's identity, is grounds for immediate suspension.

Command authority

The onboard hazard interlock holds final authority over drive execution. Operators must not attempt to defeat, replay around, or otherwise circumvent an interlock refusal. Disputed refusals are dispositioned through the hazard review, never at the console.

Liability and records

Ostara Surface Systems Ltd accepts liability for vehicle loss only where fleet procedure in the drive handbook was followed. All session activity is recorded as described in the data policy. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
