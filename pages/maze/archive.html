Sol archive

Narrative summaries of Kestrel 4's campaign to date. Full telemetry for each sol is held in the operations record; these entries are the drive team's plain-prose account of each site.

Halden Flats, sols 1-86

Commissioning and shakedown. Open terrain with long sight lines, used to trim the odometer, exercise the interlock against staged targets, and settle the one-cell-per-drive cadence the fleet now runs everywhere. Two extraction exercises completed, both codes read back clean on the voice loop.

Merrow Slope, sols 87-158

First blind-drive campaign. Broken ground meant frequent interlock refusals and slow sols; the team learned to let the survey record fill in before committing long sequences. A stuck mast louvre on sol 131 cost four sols of drives and led to the current thermal floor rule on the power sheet.

Corran Terrace, sols 159-206

Short site between relay reconfigurations. Mostly instrument work and calibration, with a compact traverse closed out in nine drive sols. The sol 199 odometer scale check listed on the instruments sheet dates from this stay.

Vantor Basin, sols 207 to date

Current site. Approach, parking and entry checks occupied sols 207-213; the sol 214 traverse to the extraction pad is running now on the traverse console. Site survey detail accumulates on the survey record as the rover maps it, and this archive entry will be written up after extraction.
