The navigation camera is the sensing element behind the hazard interlock: it resolves obstructions only for the cell the rover currently occupies, one cell in each heading. There is no orbital or long-range supplement at this site.

Calibration

  • SOL 199 odometer scale check

Calibration products are archived with the sol they were taken on and are downlinked in the maintenance queue, behind traverse telemetry.

Notes

The spectrometer stays in standby during blind-drive sols to hold the power margin described on the power and thermal sheet. Requests for additional imaging go through the drive desk, not directly to the instrument lead.
