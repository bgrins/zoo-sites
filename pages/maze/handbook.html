Drive handbook

Fleet-standard procedure for blind-drive traverses. This is the same procedure summarised at the top of the traverse console; the handbook is the reference wording.

Drive rules

  • R1 one cell per drive command
  • R2 headings are N E S W only
  • R3 interlock refuses obstructions
  • R4 sequence halts at first refusal
  • R5 refused heading not retried blind

The hazard interlock senses from the cell the rover occupies and refuses any drive into a confirmed obstruction. A refusal leaves the rover exactly where it was; nothing about a refusal damages the vehicle or the sol plan.

Surveying

Hazard mapping is line of sight. Entering a cell resolves which of its four headings are clear, and that result is written to the survey record held on the rover. Cells the rover has never entered stay unsurveyed no matter how long the console is open. Drivers plan from the accumulated record, extend it a cell at a time, and treat unsurveyed terrain as unknown rather than clear.

Sequences

Queued sequences accept up to twenty-four headings. Because a sequence stops at the first refusal, long queues through unsurveyed cells are legal but rarely useful; the fleet habit is short sequences through surveyed ground and single drives at the frontier.

Shift handover

A console session belongs to one operator. At handover the outgoing driver reads the rover's position, the queue and the last three telemetry lines to the incoming driver on the ops voice loop, then signs out of fleet single sign-on; the incoming driver signs in on their own credentials and confirms the position against the survey record before issuing a drive. The rover holds its position and its record across the change.

Closing a sol plan

When the rover reaches the designated extraction pad the console retires its drive deck and issues an extraction code. The driver reads the code back to mission control on the ops voice loop, which releases the recovery window and closes the sol plan. Anomalies during a traverse go to the drive desk and are dispositioned through the hazard review.
