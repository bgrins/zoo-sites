Signing out

The traverse console has no sign-in of its own. It opens under fleet single sign-on on this workstation and stays open for as long as your fleet credentials are active, so signing out means signing out of fleet single sign-on or locking the workstation.

Orvaken 4 holds its position and its survey record either way, and it does not move until a driver issues the next drive. Back to the traverse console.

Handing over? Read the position and the last telemetry lines to the incoming driver first, as the drive handbook sets out.
