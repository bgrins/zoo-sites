Data policy

How Ostara Surface Systems handles the data generated by fleet operations consoles and the people who use them.

Operational telemetry

Rover telemetry, survey records, interlock events and drive commands are retained for the life of the vehicle plus seven years. Telemetry is attributed to the console session that issued it and is auditable by the hazard review board and the fault board.

Operator data

Console sessions record the operator identity shown in the masthead, command timestamps and console configuration. This data is used for shift handover, incident reconstruction and training review. It is not shared outside the flight operations organisation except where an insurer or regulator requires it.

Retention and access

Session records older than twenty-four months are moved to cold storage and accessible only by written request to the operations records office. Operators may request a copy of their own session records through the drive desk; requests are answered within ten working days.
