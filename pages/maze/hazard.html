Hazard review

The hazard review is the standing process by which interlock activity is audited after every sol. It exists to confirm that the interlock is behaving correctly, not to second-guess it: a refusal is treated as correct until the review proves otherwise.

Review cycle

  • T+0 sol ends, telemetry freezes
  • T+1 interlock events pulled
  • T+1 navcam frames matched
  • T+2 review board disposition
  • T+2 findings filed to archive

Every interlock refusal is matched against the navigation camera frame taken at the moment of refusal. A refusal with no supporting frame is escalated to the fault board the same sol.

Dispositions

  • CONFIRMED obstruction real
  • SENSOR frame quality low
  • SPURIOUS no obstruction seen
  • OPEN awaiting more frames

A spurious finding does not clear a heading for drivers. Only the interlock itself, sensing from inside a cell, clears or refuses a heading at drive time.

Standing rules

Review findings are filed per sol in the operations record and never fed back into the live traverse display. Drivers plan from the survey record their own rover has built, and from nothing else. Questions about a specific disposition go to the drive desk.
