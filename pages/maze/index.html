Traverse console

Kestrel 4 is parked at the basin entry cell A1. Sol plan 214 calls for a transit to the extraction pad at F6, the far corner of the survey grid. Hazard mapping is line of sight: the navigation camera resolves obstructions only for cells the rover has entered, so the rest of the grid stays unsurveyed. Drives are issued one cell at a time, a drive into an obstruction is refused by the hazard interlock, and a queued sequence stops at the first refusal.

Extraction code

Kestrel 4 is on the extraction pad and sol plan 214 is closed. Read the code back to mission control to release the recovery window.
