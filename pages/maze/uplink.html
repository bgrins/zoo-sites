Uplink windows

Relay passes available to Orvaken 4 for sol 214 and the following two sols, local solar time. Drive commands ride the continuous low-rate link; the windows below carry bulk telemetry, imaging and software loads.

Pass schedule

  • SOL 214 08:12 R1 11 MIN
  • SOL 214 13:47 R2 8 MIN
  • SOL 214 19:03 R1 14 MIN
  • SOL 215 08:41 R1 10 MIN
  • SOL 215 14:19 R2 7 MIN
  • SOL 215 19:36 R1 13 MIN
  • SOL 216 09:10 R1 9 MIN
  • SOL 216 14:52 R2 7 MIN
  • SOL 216 20:08 R1 12 MIN

R1 and R2 are the two relay orbiters supporting this site. Pass times shift roughly half an hour later each sol as the orbits precess.

Link rules

  • DRIVE cmds low-rate link only
  • BULK data queued to next pass
  • EXTRACTION readback voice loop
  • SW loads evening passes only

If the low-rate link drops, the console inhibits its deck and asks for a reload; the rover holds position and keeps its survey record. Extraction code readback to mission control happens on the ops voice loop, not on a relay pass.
