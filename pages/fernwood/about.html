About the Commons

Fernwood Commons is the noticeboard for the five blocks between Alder Creek and the old mill race: Maple Row, Old Mill, Birchside, The Green and the creekside itself. It is run by volunteers from the Fernwood Neighbourhood Association and paid for out of the association's dues.

Anything a neighbour would tape to the bakery window belongs here: found cats, surplus zucchini, porch concerts, cleanup tallies. Posts appear newest first, and older ones load in as you read down the feed.

House rules

  • Sign what you post. First and last name, like over the fence.
  • No commerce beyond a yard-sale notice. No flyers for chains.
  • Disagree on the porch, not on the feed.
  • Each post carries a reference code; quote it when you write in.

Contact

Write to the stewards at post@fernwoodcommons.example.org, or leave a note in the green box outside the tool library on Maple Row. Posts sent that way go up once a steward has typed them in, usually the same evening. Corrections to a post go to its author first.
