Terms of use

By posting to or reading the Commons you agree to these terms, which sit alongside the house rules.

Posts

  • Posts belong to the neighbours who wrote them. By sending a post you let the association show it on the feed and in the printed newsletter.
  • The stewards may shorten, hold back or remove any post that breaks the house rules, and say why when asked.
  • Notices on the feed are neighbours' own words. The association does not check them and is not responsible for them.

Using the feed

The feed is free to read and is paid for out of the association's dues. Please do not copy it wholesale onto other sites or use it to send neighbours anything they did not ask for.

Changes

These terms were agreed at the association meeting of March 2026. Changes are announced on the feed first.

Fernwood Commons is run by volunteers from the Fernwood Neighbourhood Association. Posts belong to their authors. Back to the feed.
