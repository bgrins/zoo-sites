Privacy on the Commons

The Fernwood Neighbourhood Association runs this feed. Here is what it keeps and how to have something taken down.

What the feed shows

Every post carries the name its author signed it with, the block it was posted from and the day it went up. That is all the feed knows about anyone. It has no accounts, no tracking and no advertising, and it sets a single cookie that keeps the feed loading in order while you read.

How posts reach the feed

Neighbours send posts to the stewards at post@fernwoodcommons.example.org or leave them in the green box outside the tool library. The stewards type them up and keep the original note or email for three months, then delete it.

Taking a post down

  • An author can have their own post removed at any time. Quote its reference code.
  • Anyone named or pictured in a post can ask for it to come down. The stewards remove it first and ask questions afterwards.
  • Found-pet and lost-property posts come down once the owner is found, or after two months.
