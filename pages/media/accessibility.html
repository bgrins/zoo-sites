Accessibility

How the bulletins are made available to people who cannot use the audio or the page as it stands.

Transcripts as the alternative to audio

Every recording is published with a written transcript that is written out as the recording runs, with a timecode on each line. The chapter marks let you jump straight to the synopsis, the sea areas, the station reports or the inshore statement without listening through the rest.

The player has its own Play and Pause buttons as well as the browser's controls, and the playhead position is announced as it changes.

Using the site

Pages are plain text in a single typeface and work with the browser's own zoom and with a screen reader. The layout reflows to a single column on a narrow screen, with the side panel above the page content.

Known problems

The transcript is written out only for the part of the recording that has been played or jumped to, so a screen reader user who wants the whole bulletin should play it through or take each chapter in turn. Purged recordings have no transcript; the duty desk reads those from the log.

Telling us

If something on the site does not work for you, write to the station office or tell the duty desk, and say which page and what you were trying to do.
