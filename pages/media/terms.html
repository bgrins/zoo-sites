Terms of use

The conditions on which the recordings, transcripts and notices on this site are published.

Not a substitute for the broadcast

The recordings and transcripts here are a convenience copy of what went out on air. The broadcast itself, and any correction read at a later transmission, is the authoritative forecast.

A recording can be delayed in publishing, and a transcript can lag the audio. Before putting to sea, listen to the most recent transmission or call the duty desk rather than relying on a page that may have been open in your browser for hours.

Using the material

Forecast material is reproduced under marine safety licence MS-114. You may play, save and share the recordings and transcripts for navigation, training and club use. Do not edit a recording or republish a transcript in a way that could be mistaken for a current forecast, and do not use either in advertising.

Availability

We aim to publish every scheduled transmission within a few minutes of it ending, but the website is not covered by the availability standard the licence sets for the transmitter. An outage of the site alone is not a reportable event and will not be announced on air.

Liability

The station takes every care to read the forecast accurately and to publish it faithfully. Nothing in these terms limits a liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Questions about these terms go to the station office at the Harbour Station address.
