Coverage

Skerrow works the northern approaches from Cape Ardnoy to the Fetlan bank, including the offshore sea areas listed opposite.

Reporting stations

  • Skerrow Head
  • Braithe Light
  • Munroe Bank buoy
  • Orrin Sound mast

Sea areas

Areas named in the coastal forecast, working from the west.

Offshore areas

TalvigOpen water west of Cape Ardnoy, out to the shelf edge. The first area to feel a westerly low, and usually the roughest in the bulletin.

BraitheFrom Cape Ardnoy north to Braithe Light, twelve to forty miles offshore. Reported from the light and from passing traffic.

Munroe BankThe shoal ground around the Munroe Bank buoy. Seas steepen quickly here on a spring ebb against the wind.

Calder DeepThe deep channel between Munroe Bank and the Orrin shore, used by through traffic. Wind here often backs ahead of the rest.

Orrin SoundThe sound inside the Orrin islands, reported from the Orrin Sound mast. Sheltered from the west, exposed to the north.

FetlanNorth and east of Fetlan Point to the Fetlan bank. The last area read, and the one the inshore statement ends on.

Inshore waters

Cape Ardnoy to Fetlan Point, out to twelve nautical miles, is issued as a single inshore statement at the end of every coastal forecast.
