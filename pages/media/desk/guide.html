Desk guide

How the news desk builds and hands over a bulletin.

The running order

Each news bulletin has one running order: the stories in the order the presenter reads them, with each story's slug, a one-line summary and its read time. The on-air time beside each story is worked out from the read times above it, so it changes as stories move.

The duty editor sets the order for each bulletin and leaves it as a note beside the running order. Stories arrive on the desk in the order they were filed, not the order they will be read.

Moving a story

With a mouse or touchpad

Press on a story and drag it up or down, then let go where it should sit. A story dropped on another takes that story's place, and the stories between close up.

From the keyboard

Tab to the story's grip, the :: at the left of the row. Press Space or Enter to pick the story up, the up and down arrow keys to move it, and Space or Enter again to drop it. Escape puts it back where it was.

From the story menu

The ... button at the right of each row opens that story's menu, which moves it up or down one place, or straight to the top or the bottom of the bulletin.

Every move is saved to the desk as it is made. The line under the list says when all of them have been saved.

Locking

When the order is right, lock it. Locking sends the running order to the studio and issues a lock reference, which the transmission log records against the bulletin. A locked running order cannot be changed from the desk.

If a locked bulletin has to change, for a late story or a correction, ring the duty desk on 0117 496 0261 and give the lock reference. The operator changes the order in the studio and notes the change on the log.
