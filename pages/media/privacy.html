Privacy notice

What the station records, why, and for how long. Last revised 1 March 2026.

What we record

Every transmission from Harbour Station is recorded, and so is every call the duty desk takes, on the announce channel, the working channels and the telephone. Recording is a condition of the marine safety licence: the log has to show what was said, when and to whom, so that an incident can be reconstructed afterwards.

A call on the working channel may therefore capture a vessel name, a position, a skipper's name and the nature of a problem. Telephone calls to the duty desk also record the calling number.

How long we keep it

Bulletin audioSix hours from transmission, then deleted.

Call recordingsNinety days, or longer where an incident is under review.

Written logSeven years, as the licence requires.

CorrespondenceTwo years from the last letter.

Who sees it

Operators on watch, the station office, and the rescue coordination centre when a call concerns safety of life. The log is disclosed to the licensing authority or to an inquiry when it is formally requested, and to nobody else.

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It sets a single session cookie, which the bulletin player needs to load a recording, and that cookie expires when you close the browser.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of any recording or log entry that concerns you, ask for a correction where the log is wrong, or object to how it is used. Put the request in writing to the station office with the date and time of the call. Entries the licence obliges us to keep cannot be deleted early, but a correction is annotated beside the original, as it is on air.
