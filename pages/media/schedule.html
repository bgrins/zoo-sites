Duty desk

Staffed continuously. Call on the announce channel and stand by for a working channel.

Transmission times

All times UTC. The coastal forecast runs to roughly fifty seconds.

Coastal forecast

Broadcast at 0535, 1135, 1735 and 2335 on the working channels, preceded by an announce call. Each transmission carries the general synopsis, the sea area forecast, station reports and the inshore statement, in that order.

Gale warnings

Issued on receipt and repeated at the next two scheduled transmissions. A warning in force is read before the general synopsis.

Recordings

Every transmission is recorded and published to the bulletin list within a few minutes. Recordings are purged six hours after transmission; the written forecast remains available from the duty desk on request.
