Privacy notice

Effective March 1, 2026.

What we collect on this site

We collect the configuration choices you make on this site to prepare your draft order and to size our network investment. A draft is tied to your browser visit, not to your name, until you reserve it or complete an order. We do not sell browsing or location data.

Customer records

Once you are a customer we hold your account details, the numbers and devices on it, your billing history and your call and data records. Details of the services you use and how you use them are customer proprietary network information, and federal rules limit how we may use and share them. We use them to run and bill your service and do not use them for marketing unless you opt in.

How long we keep it

Account and usage records are retained as required by telecommunications regulation. Draft orders that are never reserved are deleted after thirty days.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of the records we hold about you, or for a correction.
  • Opt in to, or out of, marketing that uses your service records.
  • Close your account; we then keep only what the law requires.
