Terms of service

Effective March 1, 2026. These terms apply to every Lumeva Mobile line.

Your plan

Lumeva Mobile service is month to month with no annual contract. Quoted monthly totals include applicable taxes, regulatory fees and multi-line credits at the time of quotation; credits require all lines to remain on one account. Unlimited talk and text apply to domestic use. High-speed data allowances reset on the first day of each billing cycle, after which data continues at reduced speed.

Draft orders and reservations

Draft orders prepared in the plan builder are estimates held for the duration of your visit and do not reserve service, numbers or equipment. A reservation made at checkout holds the quoted monthly total for seven days. Service begins when an order is completed with our care team or in a Lumeva store, after an identity check and, where you keep a number, a completed port-in.

Billing and AutoPay

Service is billed in advance on the same day each month. AutoPay is optional; if you turn it on, we charge the card or bank account on file two days before the due date and email a receipt. A payment that fails is retried once, and service is not suspended for a missed payment until thirty days after the due date.

Acceptable use

Tethering beyond the plan's hotspot allowance, machine-to-machine reselling and continuous unattended streaming are outside residential use and may be moved to a business tariff after notice.

911 and emergency calls

911 calls are free on every line and connect even without an active plan. Location accuracy depends on the handset and the network in use; Wi-Fi calling requires a registered emergency address, which you can set in your device settings. Read the 911 and E911 notice before relying on Wi-Fi calling.

Ending service and changes to these terms

You can cancel a line at any time; service runs to the end of the paid month and is not prorated. We give at least thirty days' notice of a change that raises your price or reduces what your plan includes, and you may cancel without charge before it applies.

Questions about these terms go to care@lumeva.example. See also the privacy notice.
