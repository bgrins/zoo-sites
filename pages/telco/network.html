Mid-band 5G in every metro we serve, with 4G fallback across the rest of the map.

Built out where you actually are

Lumeva runs its own mid-band 5G core in metropolitan service areas and partners on rural 4G so a road trip never means a dead phone. Speeds step down, never off: when 5G thins out, your line hands over to 4G LTE without dropping a call or a stream. Video quality follows your plan tier wherever you are on the map.
