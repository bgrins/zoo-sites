Network management and typical speeds

Typical speeds are measured across our 5G metro network at busy hours. Your speeds vary with location, device and load. After a plan's high-speed allowance is used, data continues at up to 1 Mbps down for the rest of the cycle.

Video resolution follows the plan tier: SD on Signal, HD on Signal Plus, 4K on Signal Plus Ultra. We do not block lawful content, and we do not charge for faster delivery of any service.

911 and E911 notice

911 calls connect on every Lumeva line, and text-to-911 works where the local emergency center accepts it. When you call over Wi-Fi, the call center receives the emergency address registered on the line rather than your live location, so keep that address current in your device settings. Wi-Fi calling to 911 fails if the connection or power fails.

Accessibility

Real-time text works on supported phones at no charge, and bills are available in large print or braille on request. Call 1-512-555-0142, or use 711 relay services to reach the care team.
