Terms and privacy

Service terms

Lumeva Mobile service is month to month with no annual contract. Quoted monthly totals include applicable taxes, regulatory fees and multi-line credits at the time of quotation; credits require all lines to remain on one account. Unlimited talk and text apply to domestic use. High-speed data allowances reset on the first day of each billing cycle, after which data continues at reduced speed.

Draft orders prepared in the plan builder are estimates held for the duration of your visit and do not reserve service, numbers or equipment. Service begins when an order is completed with our care team or in a Lumeva store.

Privacy notice

We collect the configuration choices you make on this site to prepare your draft order and to size our network investment. We do not sell browsing or location data. Account and usage records are retained as required by telecommunications regulation and are available to you on request at care@lumeva.example.

Acceptable use

Tethering beyond the plan's hotspot allowance, machine-to-machine reselling and continuous unattended streaming are outside residential use and may be moved to a business tariff after notice.
