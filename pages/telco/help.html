Help center

Answers to the questions our care team hears most.

How do plan prices work?

Monthly totals depend on the plan tier and how many lines share the account, so the plan builder quotes your exact total live as you configure it. Multi-line credits are applied automatically to every line after the first, and the quoted figure already includes taxes and fees.

What happens when I change my draft order?

Every change you make in the builder updates the draft order saved to your visit, so the summary always reflects the last configuration you touched — even after you leave the page and come back. Nothing is billed until you bring the draft to a Lumeva store or complete a port-in with our care team. When the summary looks right, continue to checkout to reserve it.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Port-ins from any carrier complete within a few hours once your old account PIN checks out. Keep your old SIM active until the transfer text arrives.

What if I use up my high-speed data?

Your line keeps working at reduced speed for the rest of the cycle. There are no overage charges on any Lumeva plan.
