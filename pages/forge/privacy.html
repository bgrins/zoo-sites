Privacy notice

What Bellhollow Systems Ltd holds about the people who use Kettleforge, and why. Revised 2 March 2026.
Privacy Terms
What we hold

For every account: a username, a verified email address, a hashed password, two-factor settings, and the sessions and access tokens you have created. For organisations: billing contacts and invoices. For everyone who uses the site: request logs, kept for thirty days to investigate faults and abuse and then deleted.

Repository contents, issues, reviews and pipeline output belong to the organisation that owns the repository. We process them to provide the service and for no other purpose, and we do not use them to train anything.

Who sees it

Your username and the reviews, comments and commits you write are visible to anyone who can read the repository they belong to. Your email address is shown only to organisation owners. Inside Bellhollow, access to customer data follows the per-incident rule described on the security page.

We use two processors, both under written contracts: a card payment provider for invoices, and a mail provider for notifications. Neither receives repository data.

Your rights

You can download everything held against your account from your profile, correct it, or ask us to erase it. Erasing an account keeps the commits and reviews you wrote, attributed to a placeholder name, because they are part of other people's history.

Write to privacy@kettleforge.example or to the Data Protection Officer, Bellhollow Systems Ltd, 14 Cranmoor Wharf, Vellinmouth VM4 3TR. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the data protection regulator.
