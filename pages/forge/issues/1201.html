Reconciliation disagrees with pricing on window edges #1201

Open p.okonkwo opened this issue on 03 July · 2 comments
All issues4 #1201
p.okonkwo opened this issue on 03 July

Nightly reconciliation flagged 214 quotes from 1 July as priced in the wrong rate window. Every one of them was priced in the last second before a boundary. Pricing puts them in the window that is closing; the reconciliation job puts them in the next one.

The job carries its own copy of the window calculation, and that copy rounds the epoch value where the gateway floors it. The two only disagree in the final half second of a window, which is why the count is small and why it never shows up in the unit suite.

m.calderwood commented on 04 July

Confirmed against the replay set: 0.7% of quotes land within half a second of a boundary on a busy day. The job should import the gateway's helpers rather than carry its own maths.

t.ashgrove commented on 14 July

#482 moves the window helpers into their own module. The reconciliation job switches to them in a follow-up once that lands.
