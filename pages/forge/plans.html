What a seat is

A seat is an account that can write to a repository the organisation owns, push a branch, open a pull request or submit a review. An account with read access uses no seat, and neither does a bot account that only reports pipeline results. Seats are counted once a day and charged for the days they existed, so a contractor added for a fortnight costs a fortnight rather than a month.

Changing plan takes effect immediately. Moving up is charged pro rata from the day of the change; moving down applies at the end of the billing period, because a repository can be relying on a feature the smaller plan does not carry and we would rather not disable a required job in the middle of someone's release.

Anvil minutes and storage

An Anvil minute is a minute of wall-clock time on a hosted runner of the default size. A larger runner consumes minutes faster, at the multiplier shown when the runner class is selected. Self-hosted runners consume no minutes at all, which is the usual reason a team moves to Foundry. Minutes do not carry over between months.

Every plan includes 25 GB of registry and artefact storage per organisation, with further storage charged monthly by the gigabyte. Retention defaults to ninety days for build artefacts and indefinitely for published registry versions; Foundry can set both. When an organisation goes over its storage allowance we write to the billing contacts and keep serving artefacts for thirty days before anything is pruned, oldest first.

Billing and cancellation

Invoices are issued on the first working day of the month for the month that has just ended and are payable within fourteen days. Annual invoicing by bank transfer is available on Foundry. Cancelling stops the next invoice and leaves the organisation on Bench rather than deleting anything; repositories, pipelines and registry versions stay readable, and a repository that needs a paid feature simply stops enforcing it.

An education or registered-charity discount of half the seat price is available on Workshop and Foundry. Write to billing@kettleforge.example with evidence of status, or call 0808 157 0198.
