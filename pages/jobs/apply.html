How to apply

Applications come to the hiring office by letter or by electronic mail, and a person reads every one. There is no portal, no account to create and nothing to upload. Quote the reference from the listing you are answering, tell us what you have done, and give us a way to reach you during the day.

What to send

The reference

Each listing carries its own reference. Put it at the top of your letter so the office routes you to the right crew leader rather than to the general pile.

Whatever record you have

Codings, tickets, apprenticeship papers, a page of drawings you produced, or the name of a foreman who will speak for you. A polished document is not the point; evidence is.

When you can walk the floor

Shortlisted candidates spend half a day on the floor. Tell us the days you could come in, and whether you need the visit outside your current shift.

Draft your letter here

This form opens a message in your own mail program addressed to the hiring office. Nothing is stored on this site. If you would rather write your own letter, or send it on paper to Foundry Road, that is just as welcome.

What happens next

The hiring office acknowledges every application within two working days. If your letter answers a live listing and the crew wants to see you, the floor visit is arranged inside a fortnight. Offers follow within a week of the visit, at the salary the listing advertised.

If nothing open suits you, say what you do and we will hold your letter for six months against the next listing in that trade. Speculative letters are read in the same batch as the rest; the pattern shop and the drawing office in particular have taken people on that way.

Adjustments for the floor visit, whether that is access, hearing or reading support, are arranged by the hiring office without a note from anyone. Ask when you write, or telephone the office on 01632 960184.
