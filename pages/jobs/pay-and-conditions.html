Pay and conditions

Every listing advertises the figure an offer is made at, and that is the figure on the contract. What follows applies on top of it, on the same terms for the drawing office, the plant floor and the yard.

Pay

Pay is reviewed once a year, in April, against the trade rate in the valley rather than against a performance curve. A review has never returned a reduction. Shift crews carry a premium of a fifth of base on nights and a tenth on the late turn, paid on the roster rather than on attendance, so a shift you were rostered for and stood down from is still paid at the premium.

Coded trades are paid for the sit, the practice plates and the re-sit, in work time. Overtime is voluntary, offered to the crew before it is offered outside, and paid at time and a half; Sunday and statutory days are double. Nobody is asked to work more than one weekend in four.

Time off

Holiday

Twenty-eight days plus statutory days, rising by a day for every four years of service to a maximum of thirty-five. The shed shuts for a fortnight in August and a week at the turn of the year.

Sickness

Full pay for the first twelve weeks of any absence and half pay for a further twelve. Injury sustained on the floor is not counted against either.

Family leave

Eighteen weeks at full pay for a birth or an adoption, taken by whichever parent, and five days a year of paid leave to care for a dependent.

Pension, tools and the canteen

The Works matches pension contributions to a tenth of base pay and keeps matching whatever you add above that up to a further twentieth. The scheme is run by trustees, two of whom are elected by the crews.

A tooling allowance is paid every April, and the tool crib carries consumables against your name at no charge. Boots, overalls and personal protective kit are issued and replaced by the stores rather than bought back from you. Prescription safety glasses are made up by the works optician. The canteen serves a cooked lunch on both shifts at a subsidised price, and the shift crews get a hot meal on nights.

Learning and indenture

Apprentices are indentured to a named journeyman rather than to a department, and the indenture is not transferred without the apprentice's agreement. Day release for a trade qualification is paid, including the travel, and the pattern-shop library is open to anyone on the payroll.

Anyone can ask to spend a week with another crew. Most of the plant services fitters started somewhere else on this site. If you want to move trade, the hiring office will tell you plainly what the route looks like and how long it takes.
