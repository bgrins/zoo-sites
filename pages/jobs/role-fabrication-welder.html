Fabrication Welder

Fabrication Harrowgate - North Shed Shift

$61,800 a year

Coded plate work on penstock and bridge sections. You would join the plate crew in the North Shed, the long bay where the heavy sections leave the site by barge and rail. The crew works two shifts and has taken people on in each of the past three years.

Apply for this role
Ref HW-2384

The work

Plate from 8 mm up to 60 mm, in flux-cored and manual metal arc, on rolled penstock cans and welded bridge girders. The rolls and the press sit two bays over, so sections reach you already formed and tacked, with the jig drawn by the drawing office and cut in the pattern shop. Most weeks you work to a class procedure, on a joint that will be shot by the Quality crew with ultrasonic and dye penetrant before it leaves the shed. Rework happens. Nobody on the plate crew is judged on a first-pass figure, and the inspector's note goes to the crew, not to a file with your name on it.

Heavy sections move under a lift director, on tandem lifts up to 180 tonnes. You will be trained as a slinger in your first six months whether you ask for it or not, because on this floor the person welding the section is the person who best knows where it wants to be picked.

What you need

Coded on plate

A current coding on plate in at least one process, or a lapsed one you are willing to re-sit in our own test bay in your first month.

Drawings, not instructions

You should be able to take a shop drawing, find the weld symbol and the procedure it calls for, and query it when the two disagree.

Shed discipline

Hot work permits, screens, extraction and a habit of stopping the bay when something looks wrong. This is the part we will not train around.

Shifts, tools and the rest

Two shifts rotate monthly and the roster is posted six weeks out. A tooling allowance is paid every April and the tool crib at Ferris Quay carries consumables and welding sets against your name. The canteen serves a cooked lunch on both shifts. Pay and conditions are set out in full on the pay page, and the figure above is the figure an offer is made at.

Write to the hiring office quoting the reference on this page. The office acknowledges every application within two working days, and there is no portal and no account to create.
