Candidate privacy notice

This notice covers what the hiring office at Harrowgate Works Ltd keeps about people who apply to work here, and for how long. Harrowgate Works Ltd, Foundry Road, Harrowgate HW3 1FR, company number 00000000, is responsible for it.

What we keep

Your letter and anything you send with it: the name and contact details you give, the listing reference, your record of work, codings and tickets, and the days you offered for a floor visit. If you walk the floor, the crew leader's note of the visit is added. This site does not store the draft letter form; it only opens a message in your own mail program.

How long

Unsuccessful letters

Held for six months against the next listing in the same trade, then destroyed. Ask us to destroy yours sooner and we will.

Offers accepted

Your letter moves to your personnel file and is kept for as long as you work here and six years after.

Right-to-work checks

Copies are taken on the first day only, and kept as the law requires.

Who sees it

The hiring office and the crew leader for the role. Nobody outside the Works sees an application, and we do not use recruitment agencies or screening software. Write to careers@harrowgateworks.example or ring 01632 960184 to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.
