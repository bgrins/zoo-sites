How we hire

1. Send your particulars

Every opening carries a reference. Write to the hiring office quoting it, with whatever record of your work you have - tickets, drawings, a foreman's word.

2. Walk the floor

Shortlisted candidates spend half a day on the floor with the crew they would join. You see the work; the crew sees you.

3. A straight offer

Offers go out within a week of the floor visit, at the salary the listing advertised. We do not negotiate people downward for saying yes quickly.

Life at the Works

Tooling allowances, a pattern-shop library going back a century, and a canteen that takes lunch seriously. Shift crews rotate monthly and rosters are posted six weeks out. Apprentices are indentured to a named journeyman, not to a department.
