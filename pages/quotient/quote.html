Lane notes

Rate card v14 applies from 1 July 2026 and covers consignments from 1 to 200 kg. Heavier consignments are priced by the carrier desk under the groupage schedule. The market rate multiplier moves with the carrier index and is fixed per quote at the moment it is issued.
