Terms & privacy

The short version of the agreements that govern your use of Quotient. The full subscription agreement is included with your onboarding pack and prevails where the two differ.

Service terms

Quotient is provided on a per-workspace subscription billed annually. Ledger data belongs to the subscribing entity at all times; we hold it only to provide the service. Reconciliation batches, journals and extracts are retained for the life of the subscription plus ninety days.

Privacy

We store the workspace records you enter, sign-in metadata, and the minimum operational logs needed to run the service. We do not sell or share workspace data with third parties, and we do not use ledger contents to train or improve any product feature. Data is processed at our Pellingstow facility and encrypted in transit and at rest.
