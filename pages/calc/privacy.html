Privacy notice

Effective 1 March 2026. Applies to Abaca Sheets and the Abaca workbook service.

What we hold

Workbook contents belong to the licensing organisation and are stored in that organisation's own workbook service tenancy. Abaca Software Ltd holds account records (name, work email, licence assignment) and service logs (sign-in events, workbook open and save events) for the operation of the service.

What we do not do

We do not read workbook contents except where the licensing organisation opens a support case that requires it, and then only with that organisation's written consent recorded on the case. We do not sell or share account records with third parties.

Shared links

A workbook opened over a shared link is governed by the sharing organisation's retention and access policy. Edits made over a shared link are attributed to the link, not to an Abaca account, unless the organisation requires sign-in.

Retention

Service logs are kept for 12 months. Account records are kept for the life of the licence and for 24 months afterwards to meet audit obligations.
