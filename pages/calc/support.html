Before you write in

  • Recalculate the sheet first. Most stale-total reports resolve on a recalculation.
  • A formula-audit flag is advisory: it marks a formula inconsistent with its neighbours, not necessarily wrong.
  • Workbooks opened over a shared link are limited by the sharing organisation's policy; Abaca support cannot raise those permissions.

Known limitations in 4.2

  • Column widths do not persist on shared-link workbooks.
  • Printing renders formulas as values regardless of the Show formulas setting.
