Employee privacy notice

How People Hub uses the personal information of everyone on the Quennell Group roster. Reviewed each January by HR Operations with the data protection lead in Legal; last reviewed January 2026.

People Hub shows four fields about each person and nothing else. The HR record behind it holds more, under its own access rules; see data standards.

What People Hub holds

Your name, badge number, department and floor, copied nightly from the HR record, plus the onboarding checklist your manager keeps for you in your first fortnight and any requests you raise in the request queue.

Why

So colleagues can find one another, so the badge office and Facilities can run the buildings, and so People Operations can keep the record accurate. The lawful basis is the group's legitimate interest in running its workplace, and, for onboarding, the employment contract.

Who can see it

Every employee can read the directory. Who can see anything more is set out under access and roles, and every view of someone else's badge record is logged against the account that made it.

How long it is kept

Directory entries are removed on your last working day. Badge records are kept for six years after you leave, then destroyed. Requests are kept for two years after they close.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what People Hub holds about you, ask for a correction, or object to a use. Raise it in the request queue under "People / Directory correction", or write to the data protection lead, Legal, Quennell Group headquarters. If you are unhappy with the answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
