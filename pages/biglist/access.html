Access and roles

Everyone at the group can read the directory. Everything beyond reading it depends on the role you hold in People Hub, and roles are granted for a job of work rather than by seniority.

A role never widens what a directory entry shows. The four fields described under data standards are the whole of what any role can see there.

Colleague

Every employee, from day one. Reads the directory, the teams page and the published policies, and raises corrections to their own entry.

Automatic on joining

Manager

Adds the onboarding checklist for their own reports, and may ask for a copy of a report's badge record where there is a documented reason.

Set by the reporting line

Badge office

Issues, reissues and deactivates badges, and amends the badge field on a record. Holds no view of employment terms.

Facilities

People Operations

Amends name and department on the record, administers onboarding across the group, and runs the quarterly quality sample.

HR Operations

Hub administrator

Configures People Hub itself: the modules on the overview, the request queue categories, and the schedule of the overnight refresh.

Technology, on request from HR

Asking for a role

Raise a request in the workspace queue under "People / Hub access", naming the role and the work it is for. Your manager confirms the need and the granting team decides. Manager access follows the reporting line automatically and does not need requesting; if it is missing, the reporting line on the record is wrong and that is the thing to fix.

Looking at someone else's record

  • A manager may see the badge record of a direct report, with a reason recorded.
  • Facilities may see any badge record where physical security requires it.
  • Nobody may see another person's record out of curiosity, and every such view is logged against the account that made it.

Review

Granted roles are reviewed twice a year alongside the departmental structure review. A role that has not been used in six months is withdrawn and can be requested again. Leavers lose every role on their final working day through the joiners and leavers process, which runs ahead of the overnight refresh so that a leaver never appears in the directory the morning after.

Where to raise a problem

Access questions go to HR Operations through the request queue. Where the queue itself is unavailable, the people team can be reached on 01632 960118, and the badge office on 01632 960142.
