Terms of use

The rules for using People Hub, which apply to every employee, contractor and agency worker with a Quennell Group workspace account.

Use it for work

Look people up to do your job: to contact a colleague, to route a request, to find a desk. Do not browse other people's entries out of curiosity, and never look up a record for someone outside the group.

Keep the roster inside the group

Directory details stay inside Quennell Group. Do not paste entries into a system outside the group or pass them to anyone outside it. A manager who needs a list of their own reports should ask for one through the request queue.

Keep your entry right

Check your own entry when you join and after any move, and report anything wrong. The HR record is authoritative, so corrections go to the field's owner as described in data standards.

Your account

Your workspace sign-in is personal. Do not share it, and lock your screen when you leave your desk. Suspected misuse is investigated under the group's disciplinary procedure.
