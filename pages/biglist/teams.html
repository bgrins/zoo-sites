Teams

The Quennell Group is organised into the ten departments below, the same departments the employment record and the directory use. To find a specific person within a department, use the directory search.

Research

Applied research and product investigation. Reports to the group technical director and runs its own quarterly review cycle.

Headquarters and northern campus

Technology

Internal platforms, workplace systems, and the service desk. Owns People Hub itself alongside the wider workspace tooling.

All sites

Finance

Group accounting, payroll, and procurement. Payroll queries should go through the finance service queue rather than to individuals.

Headquarters

People Operations

Recruitment, onboarding, employee relations, and the upkeep of the directory and badge records described elsewhere in People Hub.

Headquarters

Facilities

Buildings, front-of-house, post room, and physical security, including the badge office on the ground floor of each staffed site.

All staffed sites

Legal

Contracts, company secretarial work, data protection and litigation. The data protection lead for People Hub sits here.

Headquarters

Marketing

Brand, campaigns, events and the external website. Works closely with Sales on account-based programmes.

Headquarters and northern campus

Sales

New business and account management, organised in regional teams with a central bid office.

Headquarters and regional offices

Customer Support

The customer help desk and second-line product support, staffed in two shifts on weekdays and a skeleton rota at weekends.

Northern campus

Field Services

Client-site engineers and regional support staff. Most of the department works away from group premises during the week.

Regional, by client assignment
