Onboarding

Every new starter follows the same first-fortnight plan, whatever their department. Managers own the checklist; People Operations tracks completion.

Before day one

Manager and People Operations
  • Contract signed and right-to-work checks completed.
  • Workspace account provisioned and hardware ordered.
  • Badge photograph requested through the badge office.
  • Desk or locker allocated by Facilities at the home site.

Day one

New starter with their manager
  • Collect the identity badge from the ground-floor badge office.
  • Building induction and fire-safety walk-through.
  • Confirm personal details in People Hub and check your directory entry.
  • Meet the team and agree the first-week schedule.

First week

New starter
  • Complete the mandatory policy training set in the workspace.
  • Read the badge policy on the badge records page.
  • Book introductory sessions with neighbouring teams.

End of the first fortnight

Manager sign-off
  • Checklist confirmed complete in People Hub.
  • Probation objectives recorded and shared with the new starter.
  • Any directory corrections reported to the people team.

Managers who cannot complete an item on time should note the reason in the workspace request queue so People Operations can follow up.
