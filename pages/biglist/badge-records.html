Badge records

How identity badges are issued at the Quennell Group, what the badge record holds, and how to request a copy of your own record.

Badge numbers identify a person in the HR record; they are not access credentials and do not open doors by themselves. Individual badge numbers are visible only in the directory, never on this page.

What a badge record contains

Each record holds the badge number, the holder's name and department, the issue and expiry dates, the photograph on file, and a log of reissues. Door-access events are held separately by Facilities and are not part of the badge record.

Issue and renewal

Badges are issued by the ground-floor badge office during onboarding and renew automatically while employment continues. A worn or damaged badge is replaced on the spot; a lost badge is deactivated first and replaced within one working day.

Requesting a copy of your record

  1. Open a request in the workspace request queue under "People / Badge record copy".
  2. State whether you need the current record only or the full reissue history.
  3. People Operations verifies the request against the HR record and responds within five working days.

Requests for another person's record are granted only to their manager or to Facilities, and only with a documented reason.

Leaving the group

Badges are surrendered on the final working day. The record itself is retained for six years in line with the group retention schedule, then destroyed.

This page describes policy only. For a specific person's current badge number or department, use the directory; for door-access queries, contact Facilities. Reference: data standards, access and roles, release notes.
