Release notes

What has changed in People Hub, newest first. Technology builds the workspace; HR Operations decides what goes in it. Anything affecting a policy is agreed with the group people committee before it ships.

Known limitation, unchanged in 4.2: directory search covers only the batches already streamed to your browser. A roster-wide search is the most requested item on the backlog and is not yet available.

People Hub 4.2

Current release
  • The directory streams the roster in batches of 250 as you scroll, replacing the single request that previously assembled all five thousand rows before the page drew anything.
  • The list now reports how many rows have been streamed so far, so the scope of a search is visible rather than implied.
  • Reference pages for data standards, access and these notes were split out of the old single help page.

People Hub 4.1

Previous release
  • Onboarding became a four-stage checklist with manager sign-off at the end of the first fortnight, in place of the old day-one list.
  • Badge policy moved onto its own page, and individual badge numbers were removed from every page except the directory.
  • Department names on the teams page were aligned with the employment record after the structure review.

People Hub 4.0

Workspace rebuild
  • People Hub became one workspace with a single left rail, replacing four separate HR tools that each held their own copy of the roster.
  • The overnight HR sync became the only writer into the hub, which is why a correction now appears the following morning rather than within the hour.
  • Requests moved into the workspace queue, with categories per owning team.

Earlier

Directory 3.x, retired

The 3.x directory paged the roster a hundred rows at a time behind next and previous links, and held its own department list that drifted from the employment record between structure reviews. It was retired with the 4.0 rebuild and its saved links no longer resolve.

On the backlog

Not scheduled

Roster-wide search, a department filter on the directory, and an export of a manager's own reports are all raised and unscheduled. Progress is reported to the people committee each quarter; asking again in the request queue does not move an item up, though it does record the demand.
