Data standards

What each field on a directory entry means, who owns it, and how a correction gets made. These standards are what the nightly HR sync writes against, so a value that does not meet them is what an operator sees the next morning.

One rule governs the rest: the HR record is authoritative and People Hub is a view of it. Correcting the view without correcting the record lasts until the next overnight refresh and no longer.

Name

The name the person is known by at work, written as given name then family name, in the case they use themselves. No titles, no qualifications, no nicknames in brackets. A change of name needs a record change, not a directory edit.

People Operations

Badge

A two-letter prefix set by the issuing badge office, a hyphen, then the serial. One badge number belongs to one person for the whole of their employment; a reissue keeps the number and adds a line to the reissue history.

Facilities, badge office

Department

The department on the employment record, which is not always the team a person sits with day to day. Secondments keep the home department until the secondment is made permanent.

People Operations

Floor

The floor of the person's home site, allocated with their desk or locker. Field staff and permanently remote colleagues carry the floor of the site that holds their post and their equipment.

Order of the directory

The directory is ordered by hire record and by nothing else. That order is stable, which is what lets the service stream the roster in batches rather than assembling all of it before showing anything. It is not alphabetical and it is not grouped by department, so position in the list carries no meaning beyond seniority of joining.

Making a correction

  1. Check the field's owner above. A correction goes to the owner, not to HR Operations by default.
  2. Raise it in the workspace request queue under "People / Directory correction", quoting the badge number of the entry.
  3. The owner amends the record. The change appears in People Hub after the next overnight refresh, not immediately.
  4. Where the correction affects a badge, the badge office reissues before the record is amended, so that the two never disagree.

What the directory does not hold

No salary, no contract terms, no home address, no emergency contact, no door-access history. Those live in systems with their own access rules, and a request for any of them through the directory queue is closed with a pointer rather than answered.

Quality reporting

People Operations samples the roster each quarter and reports the count of entries failing any standard above, by field and by owning department, to the group people committee. The report names departments, never individuals.
