Vault desk

The vault desk is the first line for everything Stavelock. Bring it access questions, refused rotations, sealing requests and anything the console will not do; it escalates to Security Engineering where a countersignature is needed.

What to bring the desk

The desk resolves role grants and lapses, sealing a new secret, retiring one, correcting an owner or a cadence, and any rotation the vault refused for a reason you cannot place. It also takes reports of a value that has been written somewhere it should not have been.

Severities

One — exposure
A live value has left the vault's handling rules. Phone the desk, do not wait for a ticket, and rotate as soon as the desk confirms it is safe.
Response 15 minutes
Two — blocked release
A rotation or a copy is failing and a release is held behind it.
Response 1 hour
Three — access
A grant is missing, wrong, or about to lapse.
Response 1 working day
Four — housekeeping
Metadata corrections, cadence changes, questions about policy.
Response 3 working days

Before you call

Have the namespace, the secret's name, your account, and the wording of any message the console gave you. Never read a value to the desk and never paste one into a ticket: the desk has no need for it and asking is itself a severity one. If the console refused a rotation, say which secret and what it said, not what you entered.

Break-glass out of hours

A reveal still needs a countersignature at three in the morning. Phone the out-of-hours line; the on-call custodian raises the request and pages an approver. Expect the fifteen-minute window and the forced rotation afterwards exactly as during the day, and expect the desk to ask what the reveal is for before it pages anyone.
