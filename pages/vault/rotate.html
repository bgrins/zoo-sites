Rotation replaces the stored value and issues a rotation receipt. To prove you hold the credential you are replacing, enter its current value in full. The masked form shown in the console will not be accepted.

Whitespace is trimmed. The value is checked against the sealed record and is not stored in this form.

What rotation does

The previous value stops authenticating the moment the receipt is issued. Build agents holding a lease on the old value fail their next renewal and pull the replacement automatically; anything pinned to the old value by hand has to be updated from the vault.
