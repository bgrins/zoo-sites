Secrets / Roles

Roles and entitlements

Four roles exist in the vault. A member holds one role per namespace, never two, and the role decides which controls the console renders for that member. Roles are granted for a named piece of work and expire with it.

Reader
Sees the secret list, the masked form of each value, and the audit log. No copy control is rendered.
Namespace owner
Deploy
Everything a reader sees, plus the clipboard copy on a secret detail view and the rotation form.
Namespace owner
Approver
Countersigns break-glass requests raised against the namespace. Holds no copy or rotation entitlement of its own.
Security Engineering
Custodian
Seals new secrets, retires old ones, and sets the rotation cadence recorded on each secret.
Security Engineering

Separation of duties

No account holds both approver and deploy in the same namespace. The member who asks for a reveal is never the member who countersigns it, and a custodian who sets a cadence does not hold the credential the cadence applies to. Where a small team cannot staff both sides, the approver comes from Security Engineering and the arrangement is written into the namespace record rather than left to convention.

Asking for a role

Raise the request through the service desk, naming the namespace, the role, the work it is for, and the date you expect to hand it back. Your line manager confirms the need; the namespace owner grants reader and deploy, and Security Engineering grants approver and custodian. Grants take effect on your next sign-in to the console.

Review and expiry

Every grant carries an end date, capped at ninety days. The vault mails the holder and the namespace owner a fortnight before expiry; an unrenewed grant lapses quietly and the member drops to no access at all, not to reader. Namespace owners reconcile their grant list once a quarter against the team roster, and a leaver's grants are revoked on their final working day by the joiners and leavers process rather than by the quarterly review.

What a role does not change

No role renders a value on screen, and no role shortens the countersignature a break-glass reveal needs. Both rules sit in policy PD-04 and hold for every entitlement above.
