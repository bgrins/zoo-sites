Secrets / Privacy

Privacy notice

How Stavelock Systems Ltd handles information about the people who use the vault console. Last revised 2 February 2026.

What we hold

Your operator account name, the namespaces and roles granted to you, and the audit record of every seal, read, copy and rotation made under your account, with its time and the network it came from. Secret values are sealed and are not personal data we can read.

Why we hold it

To run the vault for your organisation under its contract with us, and to keep the audit trail that contract and policy PD-04 require. We do not use any of it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it beyond the sub-processors listed in your organisation's agreement.

How long

Audit records are kept for seven years. Account records are removed ninety days after your last grant lapses, unless an open incident needs them.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the records held about you, or for a correction, through your organisation's namespace owner or by writing to the vault desk. You may also complain to the data protection regulator.
