Signing out

Cadre Workplace has no sign-in of its own. The console opens under your department's single sign-on and stays open for as long as your work account is signed in on this browser, so signing out means signing out of your work account.

On a shared or hot-desk machine, sign out of your work account and close every browser window before you leave the desk. Back to the space plan.
