Privacy notice

HE-POL-02, revision 3. How Halverd Estates Management Ltd uses the personal information held in Cadre Workplace. Halverd is the controller for this information.

What the console holds

  • Space records: the occupant's name, role, department and desk extension for each allocated room.
  • Console accounts: your name, work email, department and the roles you hold.
  • The access log: every space record opened, with the session, the room and the time.
  • Move tickets: the requester's name and department, and the room concerned.

Why it is held

To run the buildings: so reception can direct a visitor, the service desk can reach an occupant about a room, and Estates can report occupancy to the tenant departments. The legal basis is Halverd's legitimate interest in managing the portfolio under its contracts with those departments. Nothing here is used for performance monitoring or sold to anyone.

Who can see it

Only the roles set out in console access and roles, one record at a time. Bulk occupant lists go to Estates and HR on request under HE-STD-07 and nowhere else.

How long it is kept

A space record names its current occupant only; the previous holder is removed on the day a move completes. The access log is kept for twelve months, move tickets for three years, and console accounts until the role ends.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what the console holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or object to a use of it. Write to the service desk at estates.support@halverd-estates.example, marked for the portfolio administrator; requests are answered within one month. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the data protection regulator.
