Console access and roles

HE-STD-11, revision 4. Who may open which part of Cadre Workplace, how a role is granted, and how long it lasts.

Roles

RoleHeld byMay openReview
Plan reader Any employee of a tenant department Space plans for the buildings their department occupies, with departmental tint and desk positions. Space records are not included. Annual
Record reader Department space contacts, reception, Estates One space record at a time, from the plan. Occupant name, role, space type, aspect, department, extension and desk count. Half yearly
Ticket raiser Department space contacts The move ticket queue for their own buildings, and the helpdesk route for raising a ticket. Half yearly
Sheet author Estates CAD only Sheet issue and revision. Nobody else can reissue a sheet, and no sheet changes outside the quarterly reissue. Quarterly
Portfolio administrator Named Estates staff, two per portfolio Role grants, occupancy extracts, and the standards library. Quarterly

How space records are released

A space record is released to the console one room at a time, against the session you signed in with. There is no bulk listing behind the plan and no export: a record reader who needs twenty rooms opens twenty records. If a session lapses while the plan is still on screen, the next record you open is refused and the console asks you to reload the sheet and select the room again. Reloading is the fix; the refusal is not a fault.

Bulk occupant lists are issued to Estates and to HR on request, under HE-STD-07, and are never published to a plan, to the ticket queue, or to a department. A request for one names the buildings, the levels and the purpose, and is approved by a portfolio administrator.

Getting a role

  • Your department's space contact asks the Estates helpdesk, naming you, the role and the buildings. Individuals cannot request a role for themselves.
  • Record reader and above need the department head's agreement, recorded on the request.
  • Grants are made within two working days. Sheet author and portfolio administrator are granted by a second administrator, never by the holder.

Leavers and movers

A role ends on the day the holder leaves the department that asked for it, not on the day anyone remembers to say so. Department space contacts confirm their list at each review, and an unconfirmed list expires rather than rolling forward. Where somebody moves between departments, the receiving department raises a fresh request; a transferred role is not carried across.

What is logged

Every space record opened is logged against the session, with the room and the time, and held for twelve months. The log supports occupancy queries and access reviews. It is not used for performance monitoring, and an extract naming an individual needs the portfolio administrator's authority.
