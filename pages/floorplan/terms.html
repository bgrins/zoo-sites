Terms of use

HE-POL-03, revision 2. The conditions that come with a Cadre Workplace role.

Your account

A console account is personal. Do not share it, sign in for someone else, or leave a session open on a shared screen; sign out of your work account when you leave the desk.

Using records

Open a space record only for the work your role covers: directing a visitor, answering an occupancy question, or raising a ticket. Records are not copied into other systems, lists or messages, and a record reader who needs twenty rooms opens twenty records.

Accuracy

The space record is the system of record for occupancy reporting and the sheet is not. If a record looks wrong, report it to the service desk rather than working around it.

Monitoring

Every record opened is logged, as the privacy notice explains. Use outside these terms can lead to a role being withdrawn, and the log may be used to establish what happened.

Availability

Sheets are reissued at the end of each quarter and a level may be unavailable while its sheet is redrawn. Planned downtime is posted at reception two working days ahead.
