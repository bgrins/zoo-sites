Space classification standard

HE-STD-07, revision 3. Applies to every managed building in the Halverd portfolio.

Room codes

A room code is the compass quadrant of the room followed by the sequence number allocated when the level was first surveyed. The quadrant letters describe the room's position in the building, never its position on the printed sheet: a sheet may be drawn with plan north at any edge, and the north point on the sheet governs.

  • NE, NW, SE, SW — the quadrant the room sits in.
  • Sequence numbers run once per level and are not reused after a room is subdivided.
  • Corridors, cores and risers carry no room code and are not occupiable space.

Space types

  • Corner office — a single-occupancy room meeting two external elevations, one desk position, allocated by grade.
  • Office — a room on one external elevation, one or two desk positions.
  • Project room — bookable, no permanent occupant, no fixed desks.
  • Quiet room — single person, bookable in fifteen minute slots.

Every level of a Halverd building has exactly four rooms that meet two external elevations, one per quadrant. Whether such a room is classified as a corner office depends on its allocation, so the classification is recorded in the space record and cannot be read off the sheet.

Occupancy data

Occupant names, extensions and desk counts are held against the space record and released to the console one room at a time. Bulk occupant lists are issued to Estates and to HR only, on request, and are not published to the plan or to the move ticket queue.

Sheet conventions

  • Scale 1:200 at A3, 1:100 at A1 for fit-out sheets.
  • North point sits below the footprint, with the scale bar to its right.
  • Departmental tint is advisory and is refreshed only when a sheet is reissued.
