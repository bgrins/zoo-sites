Estates service desk

Where to take a question about a building, a plan or this console, and what the desk needs from you to answer it in one pass.

Reaching the desk

  • Telephone 0808 157 0136, weekdays 08:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours the line takes a message; building emergencies go to the building's own duty line, which is posted at reception.
  • Email estates.support@halverd-estates.example. Answered in the order received, and normally the same working day.
  • In person at 14 Ostmark Row, Denhollow DH2 8QP, weekdays 09:00 to 16:00. The desk is on the ground floor to the left of reception.

What goes where

Occupancy figures for a department or a level Portfolio administrator, on request in writing 5 working days

What to include

Name the building, the level, and the room code as it appears on the sheet. A question that names a person rather than a room code is returned to the requester, because occupancy changes and the room code does not. If the question is about a printed sheet, give the sheet reference and revision from the sheet footer; sheets are reissued quarterly and the desk needs to know which one you are reading.

For a console problem, say which tab you were on, what you selected, and the message that appeared. A record refused after a long session is almost always a lapsed session, and reloading the sheet clears it; tell the desk if it does not.

What the desk cannot do

The desk does not change a sheet. Estates CAD reissues sheets at the end of each quarter, so a correction agreed in the first week of a quarter appears on the sheet at the end of it, while the space record is updated on the day the change completes. The desk also cannot release an occupant list, cannot grant itself a role, and cannot move a booking held by a building reception.

If the answer was wrong

Ask the desk to reopen the reference rather than raising a second question, and say what was wrong with the first answer. Anything still unresolved after ten working days goes to the portfolio administrator for the building, whose name the desk will give you on request.
