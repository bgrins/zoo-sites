Sign in

Forgotten password? Write to help@millrace.example from the address on the account; resets are confirmed by hand within a day.

Open an account

Accounts are free and pseudonymous: a username, an email for resets, a password. New accounts can reply immediately and can submit links after their first week. One account per person; the guidelines are the terms you are agreeing to.

We email a confirmation link to the address you give; the account opens when you follow it, within a day of registering.

Why sign in

  • Vote on submissions and replies.
  • Reply in threads immediately.
  • Submit links after your first week.
  • Flag what the stewards should see.

Reading needs no account

The stream, every thread and the feed are public and always will be.
