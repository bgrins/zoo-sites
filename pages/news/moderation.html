Moderation log

Every action the stewards take against a submission, thread or account is recorded here with its reason. Authors are named only when the action is about conduct rather than content. The log is append-only; corrections are logged as new entries.

How flags work

Any member can flag a submission or reply. Flags from accounts with a history of upheld flags weigh more. Nothing is auto-removed: flags queue for a steward, and upheld flags land here.
