Submit a link

You are not signed in. Submitting needs an account that is at least a week old — sign in or open an account. The form below is shown so you can see what a submission asks for; it unlocks once you are signed in.

Title it the way the author would. "Ask Millrace:" and "Show Millrace:" prefixes mark asks and demos.

Leave the link empty to start a text thread instead.

For asks and demos: context, what you tried, what you want from the thread. Ignored when a link is given.

One tag. The stewards merge synonyms weekly.

Duplicates within a few days are folded into the earlier thread. Your own work is welcome — say so in the thread. The guidelines cover the rest.

House rules

  • Title your submission the way the author would.
  • Submit other people's work more often than your own.
  • No paywalled teasers, no outrage bait.

New accounts

New accounts can reply immediately and can submit links after their first week. The wait keeps the front page honest.
