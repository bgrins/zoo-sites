Contact

Millrace is run by a small number of people who also have day jobs, so the fastest route is the right address. Everything below is read by a human; nothing autoresponds except to confirm receipt.

Addresses

  • stewards@Moderation: appeals, flags that need a human now, guideline questions, listing complaints. Answered in order, usually within three days.
  • help@Everything technical and account-shaped: sign-in trouble, deletion requests, broken pages, feed questions, this page being wrong.
  • sponsors@Booking the sponsored hiring slot, invoicing, and listing copy review.
  • security@Vulnerability reports. Plain description beats proof of concept; we acknowledge within one business day and fix before we discuss.

All addresses end in millrace.example.

Before you write

  • Moderation decisions are explained in the moderation log; check it first.
  • Outages and slowness live on the status page.
  • For anything about a specific page, include its address; for anything about a specific action, include roughly when.
