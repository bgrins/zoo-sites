Guidelines

The stream works because a few thousand strangers extend each other more good faith than the internet's base rate. These rules exist to keep that true. The stewards apply them with judgment, not regex.

What to submit

  • Anything a person who builds things would want to read: technical writeups, tools, history, hard-won business notes, good questions.
  • Your own work is welcome. Say it is yours in the thread, and submit other people's work more often than your own.
  • No paywalled teasers, no listicles, no outrage bait. If the value of a link is the argument it will start rather than the thing it says, let it find another stream.

Titles

  • Title your submission the way the author would. Do not editorialize, do not append your review, do not add "must read".
  • Numbers and claims stay as the source made them. If the title is misleading, that is a reason not to submit it, not a reason to fix it.
  • Ask threads start with "Ask Millrace:"; demos of your own work start with "Show Millrace:".

In the threads

  • Reply to the strongest version of the argument, not the weakest sentence in it.
  • Say who you work for when it is relevant to what you are saying.
  • Attack ideas, never people. One sharp sentence about a design beats a paragraph about its designer.
  • Flag spam and vitriol rather than replying to it. Replies feed the ranking; flags feed the log.

Accounts and votes

  • One account per person, one vote per submission.
  • Voting rings, however small and however sincere, get every involved account's votes discounted permanently. We detect more of them than we announce.
  • New accounts can reply immediately and can submit links after their first week.

Edge cases go to stewards@millrace.example. Decisions worth explaining are explained in the moderation log.
