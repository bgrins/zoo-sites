Feeds

The stream reads fine in a feed reader. We publish one feed, in JSON Feed 1.1 format, carrying the current top ten submissions with links to their threads:

millrace.example/news/feed.json

Point any reader that speaks JSON Feed at that address. The feed is regenerated when the stream re-ranks, so polling more than every ten minutes buys you nothing.

Reading a section instead

Every view of the stream is addressable, so a reader that can watch a page can watch a section:

  • /news/?sort=fresh — newest submissions first
  • /news/?view=threads — busiest discussions
  • /news/?tag=build — any tag, e.g. build, asks, jobs

Etiquette for robots

  • Identify your reader in its user agent string.
  • Poll the feed, not the front page; it is a tenth the bytes.
  • Republishing the feed wholesale is fine with attribution; submissions and replies remain the work of their authors.

Want a format we do not publish? Make the case to help@millrace.example — two requests is a coincidence, three is a roadmap.
