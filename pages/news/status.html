Status

All services normal stream, threads, feed, sign-in

Millrace runs on two rented machines and a healthy respect for boring technology. This page is written by hand when something breaks, which is why it is usually short.

Incident history

  • Jul 09Stream re-ranking stuck for 40 minutes; the front page served a stale order. Votes and replies were unaffected. Cause: a wedged ranking job after a deploy; fixed by restarting it, then by making the deploy do that itself.
  • Jun 21Sign-in unavailable for 12 minutes during a certificate rotation that went less smoothly than the runbook promised. Reading was unaffected.
  • May 30Planned maintenance: database failover test, 03:00 to 03:20 local. The stream was read-only for the window, as announced in the footer the week before.
  • May 02Thread pages slow (2 to 6 seconds) for about an hour under a scraping wave that ignored the feed. Rate limits tightened; the feed remains unthrottled.

Seeing something this page does not admit to? Tell help@millrace.example, ideally with the time and what you clicked. The feed is served from the same machines, so it makes a fair independent probe.

By the numbers

  • Uptime, trailing year 99.93 percent
  • Median page render 64 ms
  • Machines two, plus one cold spare

Report a problem

Broken page, wrong count, thread that will not load: write to help@millrace.example and include the address of the page.
