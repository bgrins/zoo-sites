Sponsor a day

Millrace sells exactly one thing itself: sponsored hiring slots on the stream, two a day and one company to a slot. That is the whole of our own ad product. No banners, no newsletter placements, no exceptions for larger budgets. The advertising and measurement partners named in the cookie dialog sell their own placements; every one of them can be refused there.

What you get

  • One listing on the stream for 24 hours, placed mid-page and marked with a hiring chip instead of a points pill.
  • An audience of forty-one thousand people who build things, a striking share of whom are exactly the person you are trying to hire.
  • A plain-text listing: role, company, location or remote, and a link to a real role description. If the link goes to a splash page, the listing comes down and you get your money back — that has happened, and it is in the moderation log.

What it costs

420 dollars a day, invoiced. Book five days in a quarter and the sixth is free. The calendar usually fills two to three weeks out; Tuesdays go first, nobody knows why.

House style for listings

  • Title it like a role, not a rocket: company, role, team, remote or not.
  • Salary ranges are welcome and listings that carry them do measurably better here.
  • Sponsored listings take no replies and cannot be voted on; they stand on what they say.

To book, write to sponsors@millrace.example with the listing text and the days you want. A steward reads every listing before it runs.

Today's slot

See what a listing looks like in place: the hiring chip on the stream, or the jobs tag for recent ones.

Why so little ad product

The stream ranks on votes and replies. Anything we sold that touched the ranking would be worth more than we could charge for it, so we sell placement that does not rank at all.
