Privacy

This page describes what Millrace collects, why, and how long it keeps it. It is written by the people who run the site, not adapted from a template, so it is short and it means what it says.

What we collect

  • Account basics. A username, an email address for resets and steward contact, and a password hash. No real name required, no phone number ever.
  • What you post. Submissions, replies, votes and flags, attributed to your username. Votes and flags are only visible in aggregate; nobody, including sponsors, sees who voted for what.
  • Server logs. Address, path and user agent, kept 30 days for abuse and debugging, then deleted. Vote-ring detection runs on these before they age out.

Cookies

Signed out, you get one session cookie that keeps the site working, and where consent applies, nothing optional runs until you have chosen. Signed in, one more cookie remembers who you are. The measurement and advertising purposes described in the cookie dialog are each individually refusable there, and refusing them changes nothing about how the stream reads.

What we do not do

  • No sale of data, no sharing with data brokers, no lookalike audiences.
  • No tracking pixels in the Friday digest; we count only deliveries and unsubscribes.
  • An advertising profile is built only while that purpose is on in the cookie dialog; switching it off, or rejecting all purposes, stops it. Sponsored listings are the same for every reader.

Deletion

Delete your account from its settings page, or by writing to help@millrace.example from the account's address. The account and email are erased within a week. Submissions and replies stay, attributed to a retired name, because threads are conversations other people are also part of; a steward will unattribute specific posts on request where there is a good reason.

Questions about this policy go to help@millrace.example. Changes are announced in the footer for two weeks before they take effect. This version has applied since March 2026.

The short version

We keep as little as we can, we sell none of it, and the cookie choices you make are respected as made.
