Policies

Booking terms

  • A reservation is confirmed when the booking desk issues a reference. The reference is the only proof of booking the gate lodge accepts.
  • Stays run from two to fourteen nights. A stay occupies every night from check-in to the morning of check-out.
  • Blackout dates are held for maintenance and private lettings and cannot be booked; a stay that would cross one is refused.
  • Booking again with new dates replaces the earlier reservation and issues a fresh reference. The earlier reference lapses.
  • The total quoted at confirmation is the whole price of the stay. Cordwood, sauna and the tarn boat carry no further charge.
  • Cancellations by telephone up to seven days before check-in.

Privacy

The booking desk records the dates you request and the reservation it issues, and nothing else. We keep booking records for the season and the one after it, as the letting ledger requires. We do not pass records to anyone beyond the gate lodge, and we send no post you did not ask for.
