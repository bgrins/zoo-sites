One timber cabin, a ridge to itself

Tamarack Hollow is a single hand-built cabin on the wooded ridge above Fernlee, half an hour past the last paved road. There is no reception desk and no neighbouring plot: the booking calendar below is the whole of our front office. The cabin sleeps four across a loft and a box bed, keeps a wood stove going through the shoulder season, and draws its water from the spring behind the woodshed.

In the cabin

  • Loft with two full beds, box bed off the porch room
  • Wood stove, stacked cordwood included
  • Gas ring, cold pantry, spring water on tap
  • Lake sauna shared with no one

On the ridge

  • Marked paths to Fernlee tarn and the fire tower
  • Rowing boat at the tarn jetty, oars in the woodshed
  • Gate lodge attended mornings for key handover

Getting here

From Fernlee, take Millbrook Road north until the tarmac gives out at the quarry turning, then follow the forestry track signed Tamarack Hollow for another four kilometres. The gate lodge sits where the track forks; collect the key there between eight and eleven any morning, or ring ahead for the lockbox word. Snow chains are sensible from late October.
