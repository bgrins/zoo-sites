Before you arrive

The gate lodge is attended for key handover between eight and eleven any morning. If you will reach the fork later than that, ring 01632 960 177 the day before and we will give you the lockbox word instead.

Getting the last four kilometres right

Tarmac ends at the quarry turning above Fernlee, and the forestry track beyond it is loose stone with a crown down the middle. An ordinary car makes it in dry weather at walking pace; after heavy rain the ruts near the second bend hold water deep enough to matter, and in a hard frost the shaded stretch under the larches stays glazed all day. Snow chains are sensible from late October. Leave the car at the gate lodge and walk the last stretch if you arrive uncertain about the track: it is fifteen minutes on foot, and the woodshed barrow is there for luggage.

Your telephone will lose signal shortly after the quarry turning and will not find it again on the ridge. There is no line and no network in the cabin, which is the point for some guests and a shock for others. Tell whoever expects to hear from you before you leave Fernlee.

What to bring

Worth packing

  • Boots that cope with wet stone, and a second pair of socks
  • A torch each, plus spare batteries for the porch lantern
  • Food for the whole stay; the nearest shop is in Fernlee
  • Matches or a lighter, however well the kindling looks stacked

Already here

  • Cordwood, kindling and the stove tools
  • Bedding and blankets for four, aired between stays
  • Pots, plates and a kettle for the gas ring
  • Oars in the woodshed for the tarn boat

Water, stove and light

Water comes from the spring behind the woodshed and runs cold at one tap. It is clean and it is not treated; boil it if that matters to you. The wood stove heats the whole cabin and the loft above it, so bank it low before bed rather than building it up. Light is oil lantern and candle, both of which we leave filled and trimmed. Nothing in the cabin runs on mains power, and there is nowhere to charge a device except the car.

Leaving

Sweep the stove ash into the pail by the door, stack what cordwood you did not burn back under cover, and shut the shutters on the tarn side; the wind comes up that flank in the night. Take your rubbish down to the gate lodge bins on your way out, and drop the key through the lodge letter slot if nobody is there. Anything left behind waits in the lodge for a season, so ring if you think you have forgotten something.
