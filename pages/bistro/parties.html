We take no bookings for tables. A whole room, or a ticket for a crowd, is a different conversation — and it happens on the telephone.

Large Parties & Private Hire

Groups walking in

Twenty-eight chairs sit in one room, and we hold none of them back, so a group of six or more arriving unannounced may wait while we push tables together. Come early in a service rather than at its peak and the room absorbs a crowd easily; arrive at eight on a Saturday and it will not. Our hours page shows when each service starts and how much of it is left by the time the counter closes.

Large tickets, phoned through

Tickets of six dishes or more are best phoned through on (503) 555-0180 rather than sent through the order-ahead counter. The reason is the pass: six dishes built six different ways land at the same moment, and the cook would rather agree a running order with you than discover it when the ticket prints. On the telephone we can also tell you honestly whether the kitchen has the room for your ticket in the next half hour.

  • Ring while the kitchen is open and say roughly when you want to collect.
  • Talk the dishes through, with anything left off or added noted per dish.
  • We read the whole ticket back before it goes to the pass.
  • Collect at the counter and quote the name the ticket was taken under.

Taking the room

The room can be had to itself on a Monday, when the kitchen is otherwise dark, and occasionally on a Sunday evening after the short Sunday service ends. Both cost the day's cooking rather than a room fee, and both are agreed in person: we would rather you stood in the room and looked at it before committing to it.

For a private day we cook a shortened card of three or four dishes chosen from whatever the market is doing that week, built to order in the usual way. We do not build a separate menu months in advance, because we would not know in March what the barrows will have in October.

Twenty-eight seated is the room's honest limit. Standing, with the front tables cleared, it takes a few more, though the kitchen pass is not a bar and we will not pretend otherwise.

Getting hold of us

Ring (503) 555-0180 during service for anything time-sensitive, or write to hello@brindlefig.example for a private day, which we answer between services rather than during them. We are at 14 Waldron Lane, on the corner of Herrick Street, under the green awning.
