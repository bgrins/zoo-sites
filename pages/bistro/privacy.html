What the order-ahead counter keeps, and for how long.

Privacy

What a ticket holds

An order-ahead ticket holds the dishes you built, each thing you added or left off, the total, the order code and the time it printed. We ask for no name, no address and no card number online; you pay at the counter when you collect, and the card reader there is run by our payment processor, not by us.

How long we keep it

Printed tickets go in the recycling at the end of the day. The electronic record of each ticket is kept for thirteen months so the books can be closed and the kitchen can see what sold, and then it is deleted.

Messages sent to hello@brindlefig.example, including private-hire enquiries, are kept for a year after the last reply.

Cookies

This site sets one cookie, which keeps your ticket together while you build it. It expires when you close the browser. There is no advertising, no tracking and no mailing list to fall onto.

Asking about your ticket

Write to hello@brindlefig.example with your order code, or ask at the counter, and we will tell you what we hold or delete it.
