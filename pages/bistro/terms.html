The short rules of the order-ahead counter.

Order-Ahead Terms

Placing a ticket

A ticket is placed when the order code appears on screen. The kitchen starts cooking when the ticket prints, so check every line before you place it: what is on the ticket is what we cook.

  • Order-ahead is for counter pick-up only, while the counter is open. See our hours.
  • Tickets of six dishes or more are best phoned through on (503) 555-0180.

Paying and collecting

The total on the confirmation is the amount the counter charges when you collect, with no service charge added. Quote the order code at the counter. A ticket not collected within an hour of printing is given to the staff meal, and we cannot hold it longer.

Changes and cancellations

Ring the kitchen within five minutes of placing a ticket and we can usually change or cancel it. After that it is on the pass.

Allergens

Leaving a component off removes that component and nothing else, and our kitchen cannot promise a dish free of cross-contact. Read the allergens & dietary notes before ordering if a trace would put you at risk.
