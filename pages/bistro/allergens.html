Allergens & Dietary Notes

Ask, every visit

The card changes with the market, so a dish that suited you in June may be built differently in October. Tell the counter what you need to avoid before you order rather than after, and the cook at the pass will tell you what is in the dish that day, what can be left off, and what cannot be taken out once it has gone into a base or a dressing.

We keep a written sheet at the counter listing the ingredients of every dish currently on the card, updated each morning when the buying is unpacked. Anyone working the counter will read it to you or hand it over. We do not print it here, because by the time you arrive it may already be a day out of date.

What building to order can and cannot do

Every dish lists what comes with it and what can be added. Untick anything you would rather leave off, whether you are standing at the counter or using the order-ahead counter, and the kitchen will build it that way. What cannot be undone is anything already cooked into the dish before your ticket reached us: a stock, a dressing base, a dough, a smoked or braised element made in the morning.

  • Leaving a component off removes that component and nothing else.
  • An added extra arrives as its own portion, never stirred through.
  • Sizes change the amount, never the recipe.

Shared surfaces

The oven, the grill, the boards and the fryer are shared by everything we cook. Flour is in the air of this kitchen most of the day, nuts are toasted on the same tray as everything else, and the same knives cut meat and vegetables within the same service. We cannot offer a dish free of cross-contact, and we would rather say so plainly than let a guest with a severe allergy rely on us.

If a trace would put you at risk, please tell the counter before ordering. We will be honest with you about whether we can cook for you safely that day, and we take no offence if the answer sends you elsewhere.

Vegetarian, vegan and children

Several dishes are vegetarian as they stand and others become so once a component is left off; the counter will point them out. Fully plant-based building is possible on most days but not every day, because a base made that morning cannot be remade at seven in the evening. Smaller portions for children are cooked from the same card at the medium size and split onto two plates at no charge.
