Privacy and acceptable use

How the dispatch console records what you do in it, and the rules for using it. Issued by the dispatch systems group with the terminal operating instructions.

Privacy notice

The console records your staff login, the board saves you make, the work orders you raise or move, and the handover entries you sign. Those records are kept for six years with the terminal's operational log, because incident investigations and insurers rely on them.

Records are read by supervisors, the terminal manager and the dispatch systems group. They are shared outside Coppermast Terminals Limited only with the harbour authority or an investigator where the law requires it. Nothing in the console is used to monitor individual performance.

To see what the console holds about you, or to correct it, write to the data protection lead at dispatch@coppermast-terminals.example.

Acceptable use

The console is for Coppermast staff and authorised contractors working a Terminal 3 shift. Use your own login: a board save or a handover entry made under someone else's name is a disciplinary matter, because the incoming shift acts on it.

Do not use the board to hold work that has not been raised as a work order, and do not delete or re-tag another person's order without the duty controller's agreement. Faults in the console go to the dispatch systems group, as listed under Escalation.
