About Alderpost

Alderpost is a recruitment firm working a single market: the people who design, build, maintain and regulate the built environment. The firm was founded in 1994 above a print works on Wharfeside Row and still occupies the same building, four floors up from the yard.

Two desks

Work is split between two desks. The northern desk, which is the one this vacancy list belongs to, covers Yorkshire, the north east and the north west from Wendersby. A smaller southern desk works the same disciplines out of a serviced office and shares the vacancy list, so a candidate registered with one desk is visible to both. Neither desk works outside the built environment, and neither takes contingency work on volume campaigns.

What the firm places

The disciplines the desks recruit into are the ones the refine panel is indexed on: structural and geotechnical engineering, highways and transport, building services, environmental consenting, and fire engineering. Appointments run from permanent staff posts through fixed-term programme roles to interim cover, and the desk is as willing to fill a twelve-week gap as a permanent chair.

How the desk works

Every consultant on the desk holds their own clients and their own candidates, which is unusual and deliberate: the person who takes the brief is the person who makes the introduction, so nothing is handed across a floor and re-explained. A vacancy reaches the list only once the consultant has spoken to the hiring manager, agreed the salary the client will actually pay, and written the brief up. Vacancies come off the list on the day the client confirms an offer, which is why the list is shorter than the market and rather more accurate than most.

Standards

Alderpost is a member of a recruitment trade association and works to its code on advertising, candidate consent and the handling of personal data. Candidate records are kept for two years from the last contact and then deleted. Complaints are handled by the desk principal and answered within ten working days; write to the registered office below or telephone the desk line.
