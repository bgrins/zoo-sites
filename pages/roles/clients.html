Clients

The northern desk works retained briefs for utilities, transport authorities, estate owners and the consultancies that serve them. A brief is taken by telephone, written up the same day and worked against the vacancy list until a shortlist is agreed.

How a brief is worked

Each brief fixes four things: the discipline, the base office, the contract type and the salary the client will pay. Those four are the columns the vacancy list is indexed on, so the refine panel narrows the list to the same shape as the brief. A brief that leaves nothing on the list has usually over-tightened on one of the four, most often the salary band, and the counts beside each value show which one before it is applied.

Salary bands

A vacancy sits in the band that contains its advertised minimum, not its ceiling. A role advertised at sixty-two to seventy-four thousand therefore sits in the sixty to seventy-five thousand band, even where a client has authorised more than seventy-five.

Terms of business

Introductions are made on the standard terms issued with the brief. The fee is a percentage of first-year basic salary, invoiced on the start date, with a rebate scale running to twelve weeks. Interim and fixed-term placements are charged on a margin against the day rate or monthly cost.
