Privacy notice

Alderpost Recruitment Ltd is the controller of the personal information candidates and client contacts give the desk. This notice says what the desk holds, why, and for how long.

Candidates

Your curriculum vitae, covering notes, the consultant's notes of your calls, the salary and notice period you have given, and any right-to-work or sponsorship details you choose to share. The desk holds them to find you work and to introduce you to clients, and only ever introduces you to a client you have agreed to on the telephone first.

Client contacts

Names, job titles and contact details for the people who brief the desk and interview its candidates, together with the briefs themselves. These are held for as long as the client relationship lasts and for two years after.

How long

Candidate records are deleted two years after the last contact with the desk. A placement record, which the desk must keep for tax and employment law, is kept for six years from the end of the placement.

Who else sees it

The client you have agreed to, and the consultant on the southern desk if you are registered for work in its area. Nobody else, and never for marketing.

Your rights

Ask to see what the desk holds about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to stop it being used. Write to the registered office or to desk@alderpost.example; requests are answered within one month.
