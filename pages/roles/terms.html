Vacancy search · Terms

Terms

These terms cover the use of the Alderpost vacancy search. Clients engage the desk under separate terms of business, sent with the first brief; the shape of those terms is on the clients page.

The vacancy list

Every vacancy on the list has been confirmed with the hiring manager, and comes off the list on the day an offer is accepted. Salaries are the ranges the client has agreed to pay. A listing is an invitation to apply, not an offer of work.

Applying

Applying through the desk costs a candidate nothing, and the desk never charges a candidate a fee for finding them work. Quote the reference from the vacancy record when you write in, as set out under how to apply.

Using the search

The search is provided for candidates and clients looking for work in the built environment. Do not copy the list wholesale, re-advertise its vacancies, or run automated queries against it.

Complaints

The desk principal handles complaints and answers within ten working days. Write to the registered office or telephone the desk line.
