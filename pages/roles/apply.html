Vacancy search · How to apply

How to apply

Applications reach the desk by electronic mail or by telephone. There is no form to fill in and no account to create, because a consultant reads every application anyway and would rather read a letter than a questionnaire.

Applying for something on the list

Open the vacancy record from the search list and quote the reference printed on it in your covering note; it is the only thing that tells the desk which post you mean. Send the note and your curriculum vitae to desk@alderpost.example, or telephone the candidate line on 0808 157 0142 and ask for the consultant named on the record.

What to send

A curriculum vitae of two or three pages is plenty. Say what you were responsible for rather than what your team was responsible for, and give the contract value or the scale of the works where it explains the job. Set out your notice period, whatever you need in salary, and any professional membership you hold or are working towards. If you need a certificate of sponsorship to work in the United Kingdom, say so in the first paragraph; it changes which clients can consider you and it saves everyone a fortnight.

Registering without a post in mind

Many of the briefs the desk works are never advertised, so it is worth registering even when nothing on the list fits. Write in the same way and say which disciplines, bases and contract types interest you. A consultant will call you inside three working days, and your details go no further until you have agreed which client they may go to.

What happens next

The consultant reads the application, calls you, and either puts you forward or explains why the brief will not carry you. Nothing is sent to a client without your consent on that call. Where a client shortlists you, the desk arranges the interview, briefs you on the panel beforehand, and comes back to you with the outcome and the reasons for it whichever way it goes. References are taken only after an offer, and never from a current employer without your say-so.

If you hear nothing

Chase the desk. Applications occasionally arrive while a consultant is on site for a week, and a second note or a telephone call costs you nothing. Telephone the desk line on 01632 960653 between eight and six and ask who is holding your file.

Your information

Your curriculum vitae and the notes a consultant makes are held for two years from the last time you spoke to the desk, then deleted. Ask at any point to see what is held, to have it corrected, or to have it removed altogether; write to the registered office below and it is done within a month.
