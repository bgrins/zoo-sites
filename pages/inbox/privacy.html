Privacy

Fernmail privacy notice, last revised 8 January 2026.

Your mail is yours

Fernway Communications does not read, scan or index the contents of your messages for advertising, and does not sell any account data. Automated systems touch message content only to filter spam and malware.

What we keep

We hold your account address, sign-in timestamps and the device details needed to warn you about unfamiliar sign-ins. Deleted mail is purged from Trash after 30 days and from backups within a further 30 days.

Disclosure

We disclose account data only under a legally binding order served on Fernway Communications, and we notify the account holder unless the order forbids it. Questions: privacy@fernmail.example, or the postal address below.
