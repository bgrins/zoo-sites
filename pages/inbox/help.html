Help

Answers to the questions the support desk hears most.

Reading and organizing

Click a message to read it in the reading pane on the right; on a phone it opens full screen, and Back to messages returns you to the list. Mark read, Archive and Delete act on the selected message. Archived mail moves to the Archive mailbox and stays searchable; deleted mail waits in Trash. The star beside a message adds it to Starred. The search box matches senders, recipients, subjects and preview text in the open mailbox.

Sending

New message opens a compose form addressed from your own mailbox. Replies quote the original message; forwards include it in full. A copy of everything you send is kept in Sent. Your display name and signature come from Settings.

Spam and retention

Anything marked Spam is deleted after 30 days, and Trash is emptied after 30 days as well. If a real message lands in Spam, select it and choose Move to Inbox.

Account security

Fernmail signs you out of idle sessions automatically and mails you a notice when a new device signs in. If you see a sign-in you do not recognize, write to postmaster@fernmail.example and we will lock the account while we look into it.
