Help

Answers to the questions the support desk hears most.

Reading and organising

Click a message to read it in the left pane. Mark read and Archive act on the selected message; archived mail moves to the Archive mailbox and stays searchable. The search box matches senders, subjects and preview text in the open mailbox.

Sending

New message opens a compose form addressed from your own mailbox. Replies quote the original message; forwards include it in full. A copy of everything you send is kept in Sent.

Spam and retention

Anything marked Spam is deleted after 30 days, and Trash is emptied after 30 days as well. Filters live under Settings, Rules. If a real message lands in Spam, open it and reply to it once; the sender is trusted from then on.

Account security

Fernmail signs you out of idle sessions automatically and mails you a notice when a new device signs in. Change your password from Settings if you see a sign-in you do not recognise. Support: postmaster@fernmail.example.
